## The 2-minute model script (private call or partner-call opener — not public content)
"Okay, so — let me show you how this works, and stop me whenever. Real Broker is a cloud brokerage. There's no office you have to drive to; the brokerage handles the transaction side, the compliance side, and the technology, and you work from wherever your clients are. What that means for me is that the thing I actually do — the open-house and Sunday routine — is the whole job. Not driving to a sales meeting. The part people get wrong is thinking a cloud brokerage means you're alone. It's the opposite for us: you'd have me, you'd have our Tuesday table where somebody brings a real sign-in sheet every week and we run the five steps on it, and above me there's Jordan and a group of people whose job is to help agents in the group win. The model also has a way for me to build something that keeps going past my own closings — that's the part I'd rather walk through with you properly, with the actual documents, on a second call, because I want you to see it, not hear me describe it. Here's what getting started looks like: a welcome call, the sheet and the five texts in your hands that day, your first open house on the calendar within two weeks, and me on Zoom with you the first Sunday you run it. That's it. What questions do you have so far?" (illustrative — demo; number-free by design — every figure lives in brokerage-model.md and is private)

## Competing-model talking points (private, positive, factual)
- **A franchise office** — the honest pro: a local office, in-person meetings, a recognized name on the sign, structured classroom training. What Taylor's model adds: the Tuesday table and a routine taught by someone who did it, and a path for the business to grow beyond the member's own closings (the private-call part).
- **A team inside an office** — the honest pro: leads handed to you, a mentor in the room, admin help. What Taylor's model adds: a pipeline you own (the sign-ins are yours), and a routine you keep wherever you go.
- **An independent brokerage** — the honest pro: flexibility, lower fees, a tight-knit office. What Taylor's model adds: a teaching group that meets every week, and the model's longer-term path (private call).
*Never a claim about another brokerage's leadership, payouts, or people — `03-model-positioning/14`.*
