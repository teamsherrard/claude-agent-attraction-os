## Creative direction (derived by the Brain from the Brain and the one answer · October 2026 — demo)
**Mood:** A calm, well-kept classroom where the routine is on the board and the door is open.
**Energy:** 2 — calm and steady, because Taylor's voice is "warm and patient" and the agents she attracts are tired of noise (illustrative — demo)
**Type:** classic — because the Sunday newsletter and the whiteboard are her two references, and her stories are told slowly
**Colours:** dominant warm cream · support board green · accent copper — because the kitchen table, the whiteboard and the pen are her routine, not a trend (codes in Direction above)
**Photos:** clean cut-out — because she teaches face-on at a table; nothing moody, nothing shouted
**Motifs:** 1. the Sunday routine (a numbered 1 · 2 · 3 device) · 2. Austin's quiet neighbourhood streets (the place device, local attractor) · 3. the open table — "The Sunday Table" as a long plank or a set place
**Avoid:** never looks like the brokerage · never a listing flyer · never a tech startup · never a luxury-only brand · never a "hustle" aesthetic — no shouting caps, no neon, no stopwatch
**Reference recipes (three looks, plain names, the first is the Brain's pick):** 1. the warm paper mentor look — because paper, a hand-drawn rule and a taped photo are already how her Instagram reads · 2. the clean editorial leader look — because a serif headline on cream with a green anchor carries authority without volume · 3. the gold-on-black organization look — only for the organization family, in a quieter green-and-copper register, because "The Sunday Table" will post welcomes and milestones
**Tests:** authority — the routine is shown, numbered, on the board · relatability — the kitchen table and the real phone photos stay · aspiration — a group with a name and a weekly ritual an agent can picture joining
**Member's reaction:** confirmed as proposed; moved the copper accent "a touch warmer" (demo)
