## The framework (Week 2 — the member's signature method, named, 3–7 steps; built from "Teach first" and strategy.md's Teach-it rows)
**Name:** The Five-Step Sunday Routine (Taylor's own phrase since October 2022; the Lead Magnet plugin's live guide carries the same name and the keyword ROUTINE — the free guide is cut from steps 2 to 4)
**How it works:** A Saturday open house gives you names; most agents put them in a drawer. The routine turns the names into a pipeline you own: two questions on the sign-in sheet so Sunday's text has something real to say, one text per name by 6 pm Sunday with one question in it, every answer on one tracker sheet, and a Tuesday look at the sheet — at the table, with the group — so nobody falls through. It happens every Sunday whether you feel like it or not; that is the whole secret, and it costs a Sunday, not a subscription. Taylor wrote it on a legal pad in October 2022 after pulling a year of sign-in sheets out of a drawer; the next quarter three of those names bought (illustrative — demo).
| Step | What you do | What you get | The tool or template | Taught in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · Host | host a Saturday open house most weekends — your listing or another agent's | a sheet of names every week, at no cost but a Saturday | the hosting rhythm — taught live; a hosting checklist built with the first partners | Phase 1 · lesson 4 (live at the table) |
| 2 · Ask | put the two questions on the sign-in sheet and ask them without it feeling like a form | a reason to text each name — their timeline and what would move them sooner | the two-question sign-in sheet (PDF + Google Doc) | Phase 1 · Module 1 (recorded, 12 minutes) |
| 3 · Text | text every name by 6 pm Sunday, one question per text | replies before dinner; the first "spring, probably" that becomes a showing | the five Sunday text scripts | Phase 2 · lesson 2 (live on the first Sunday; recorded with the first partners) |
| 4 · Track | put every answer on one sheet, one row per name | the whole pipeline on one screen; nothing to subscribe to | the tracker sheet (Google Sheet template) | Phase 2 · lesson 3 (live on the first Sunday; recorded with the first partners) |
| 5 · Table | bring the sheet to the Tuesday table; say who you are calling this week | one thing to do each week that gets you a client, and someone who saw your sheet | the Tuesday table (30 minutes, Zoom) | Phase 3 · every Tuesday |
**One line (for graphics, under 20 words):** Host → Ask → Text → Track → Table — a Saturday, two questions, five texts, one sheet, one Tuesday.
