## The framework (Week 2 — the member's signature method, named, 3–7 steps; built from "Teach first" and strategy.md's Teach-it rows)
**Name:** The Five-Step Sunday Routine (Taylor's own phrase since October 2022; the Lead Magnet plugin's live guide carries the same name and the keyword GUIDE — the free guide is cut from steps 2 to 4)
**How it works:** A Saturday open house gives you names; most agents put them in a drawer. The routine turns the names into a pipeline you own: two questions on the sign-in sheet so Sunday's text has something real to say, one text per name by 6 pm Sunday with one question in it, every answer on one tracker sheet, and a Tuesday look at the sheet — at the table, with the group — so nobody falls through. It happens every Sunday whether you feel like it or not; that is the whole secret, and it costs a Sunday, not a subscription. Taylor wrote it on a legal pad in October 2022 after pulling a year of sign-in sheets out of a drawer; the next quarter three of those names bought (illustrative — demo).
