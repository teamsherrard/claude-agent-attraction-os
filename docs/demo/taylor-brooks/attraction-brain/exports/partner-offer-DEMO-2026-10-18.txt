WHO THIS IS FOR
────────────────────────────────────────────

You are two to five years in and doing four to ten deals a year (demo). You work at a franchise office or on a team inside one. You pay for online leads every month and you cannot say where the next closing is coming from.

You have tried the portal leads, a team that promised leads, and a coaching program whose scripts you stopped using in week three. You host open houses, and the sign-in sheets live in a drawer.

It also fits a career-changer in their first two years — a teacher, a nurse, an office manager — who is organized and coachable and has no idea where closing number three comes from.

This is not a lead program — nobody hands you leads; you host the Saturday and you text on the Sunday. This is not a pitch about the brokerage — the model is a separate conversation, on a call, with the documents.

THE PROMISE
────────────────────────────────────────────

I help 2–5 year agents who are paying for leads build a buyer pipeline from the open houses they already host — taught the way a teacher teaches it, one Sunday at a time.

Here is the Tuesday I am talking about: the invoice for the lead subscription landed on the first, somewhere between eight hundred and fifteen hundred dollars (demo). You hosted an open house on Saturday and eleven people signed in (demo). The sheet is in a drawer, and you are quietly worried that this is just what the job is.

In 2022 I opened a lead invoice in a month I had closed nothing. I'm a former teacher, so I did what teachers do — I sat down at the kitchen table on a Sunday and wrote the lesson I wished someone had given me. Two questions on the sign-in sheet, one text per name by six o'clock, one sheet for the answers, one Tuesday to look at it.

The next quarter three of those names bought (demo), and I cancelled the leads in December. The routine has run every Sunday since, and it has never cost me a subscription. It has also worked in someone else's hands: Priya ran it with me from her first Sunday and had three buyers under contract in her first sixty days (demo).

The Sunday Table is the program I built around that routine, for agents who are exactly where I was. I'd rather show you than tell you.

THE FIVE-STEP SUNDAY ROUTINE, IN FIVE STEPS
────────────────────────────────────────────

A Saturday open house gives you names, and most agents put them in a drawer. The routine turns the names into a pipeline you own: two questions on the sign-in sheet so Sunday's text has something real to say, one text per name by six o'clock Sunday, every answer on one tracker sheet, and a Tuesday look at the sheet. It happens every Sunday whether you feel like it or not — that is the whole secret, and it costs a Sunday, not a subscription.
