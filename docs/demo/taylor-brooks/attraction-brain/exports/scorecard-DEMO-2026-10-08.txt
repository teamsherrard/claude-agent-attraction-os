HOW TO USE THIS WEEKLY
════════════════════════════════════════════

1. Every evening at 6 pm the Daily Debrief counts the day — conversations, calls booked, calls held, joins, content — and scores it against the daily slice: Ahead (150% of the slice or more), On pace, or Behind. Never a grade, never a percentage. (Off on this demo brain; say "set up my daily debrief" on a real one.)
2. Every Friday at 4 pm, say "attraction weekly check-in." It rolls the week's daily rows into one weekly row, scores the week, names what moved, and gives you one thing for next week. If activity is on pace and joins lag, that is normal in a first quarter — the fix for a behind week is always activity, never the math.
3. The first Monday of each month, the monthly audit reads the ratios from real rows and replaces the assumptions above. After thirty days of data, the 1-in-4 and 1-in-3 are yours, not defaults.
4. At the end of the quarter, say "refresh my attraction plan." The next 90 days are set from what actually happened, the prior quarter is archived, and this scorecard is regenerated with a new date.
5. Three non-negotiables, every week, in this order: two agent conversations a day before anything else · the Tuesday table at 9 am with a partner presenting · the Sunday block — the routine first, then one reel.
