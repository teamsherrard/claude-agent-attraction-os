2 · WHAT THIS IS
────────────────────────────────────────────

Name: The Sunday Table. The program and the offer share one name — it is the name on my Instagram and the name of our group.

Type: mentorship program — a weekly call plus a 1:1 to start. The teaching is live: at the Tuesday table every week, and on your first Sunday with me on Zoom. One lesson is recorded today (Module 1), so this is not yet a course plus community, and there is no dated cohort, so it is not an accelerator. It is the honest word for what I actually do with a partner — I sit next to you for the first three Sundays.

Who it is for: agents two to five years in, doing four to ten deals a year (a profile, illustrative — demo), at a franchise office or on a team inside one, who are paying for online leads that don't convert and want a pipeline they actually own. It also fits a career-changer in their first two years — a teacher, a nurse, an office manager — who is organized and coachable and has no idea where closing number three comes from.

The 90-day outcome: by day 90 the Sunday routine runs every week without me, every sign-in has one text and one tracker row, and you have had your first showing from a sign-in. That is the path Priya ran — three buyers under contract in her first sixty days (illustrative — demo). No result is promised; the method is.

Format: a live weekly group call (the Tuesday table, on Zoom) · 1:1 on Zoom — the welcome call, your first Sunday, the day-30 check, one 1:1 a month after that · one recorded lesson today (Module 1, twelve minutes); lessons two to five taught live and recorded with my first partners · the group chat · nothing in person.

Cadence: the Tuesday table — Tuesdays at 9:00 am, thirty minutes, on Zoom, one real sign-in sheet a week. No office hours — I reply the same day on weekdays, and Sunday evening for anything about a sign-in sheet. Your 1:1 rhythm: a 45-minute welcome call in week one, me on Zoom for your first Sunday, the day-30 check, then one twenty-minute 1:1 a month.

Duration: a 90-day on-ramp in three phases — Set up, Run it with me, Own it — and then the ongoing partnership. The Tuesday table, the group chat and the monthly 1:1 continue for as long as you are in the organization, and from month four you teach the next person at the table.

What it costs you: nothing today — everything here comes with the partnership. Nothing I offer is priced; if that ever changes, it will be said up front, before anyone joins. What the brokerage itself charges is part of the model conversation — on a call, with the real documents, never on this page.

What this is not: Not a lead program — nobody hands you leads; you host the Saturday and you text on the Sunday. Not a pitch about the brokerage — the model is a separate conversation, on a call, with the documents; this page is about the routine and the people who teach it.

3 · THE FIVE-STEP SUNDAY ROUTINE
────────────────────────────────────────────

How it works: A Saturday open house gives you names; most agents put them in a drawer. The routine turns the names into a pipeline you own. Two questions on the sign-in sheet, so Sunday's text has something real to say. One text per name by six o'clock Sunday, with one question in it. Every answer on one tracker sheet. A Tuesday look at the sheet — at the table, with the group — so nobody falls through. And it happens every Sunday whether you feel like it or not; that is the whole secret, and it costs a Sunday, not a subscription.

I wrote it on a legal pad in October 2022, after I pulled a year of sign-in sheets out of a drawer and sat at the kitchen table with them. I'm a teacher, so I wrote a lesson plan. The next quarter three of those names bought (illustrative — demo). I cancelled the leads in December. It has run every Sunday since, including the ones I didn't feel like.

The five steps:
| Step | What you do | What you get | The tool or template | Taught in |
| 1 · Host | host a Saturday open house most weekends — your listing or another agent's | a sheet of names every week, at no cost but a Saturday | the hosting rhythm — taught live; a hosting checklist built with my first partners | Phase 1 · lesson 4, live at the table |
| 2 · Ask | put the two questions on the sign-in sheet and ask them without it feeling like a form | a reason to text each name — their timeline, and what would move them sooner | the two-question sign-in sheet (PDF and Google Doc) | Phase 1 · Module 1, recorded, 12 minutes |
| 3 · Text | text every name by 6 pm Sunday, one question per text | replies before dinner; the first "spring, probably" that becomes a showing | the five Sunday text scripts | Phase 2 · lesson 2, live on your first Sunday; recorded with my first partners |
| 4 · Track | put every answer on one sheet, one row per name | your whole pipeline on one screen; nothing to subscribe to | the tracker sheet (Google Sheet template) | Phase 2 · lesson 3, live on your first Sunday; recorded with my first partners |
| 5 · Table | bring the sheet to the Tuesday table; say who you are calling this week | one thing to do each week that gets you a client, and someone who saw your sheet | the Tuesday table (30 minutes, Zoom) | Phase 3 · every Tuesday |

One line: Host → Ask → Text → Track → Table — a Saturday, two questions, five texts, one sheet, one Tuesday.

Why five steps and not a tool: because the step most agents skip is the one that costs nothing. The two questions fix the sheet. The six o'clock deadline fixes the Sunday. The tracker fixes the drawer. The table fixes the week you don't feel like it. All of it fits on one page, which is why I can teach it in your first thirty days.
