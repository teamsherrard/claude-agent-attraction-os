`docs/demo/taylor-brooks/attraction-brain/`; every number in it is fictional and tagged. Plugin 3 (0.7.0).*

- [ ] Plugins 1, 2 and 3 installed and current (Check for updates); the demo Brain pulled (`Demo brain: yes`).

## The screen (one sitting, about an hour live; say so)
**Say:** `launch my lead magnet plugin`
- [ ] First paragraph = the Week 2 outcome, Mike's words, verbatim. Then two warm lines and *"Ready? Just say yes."*
  No question box, no list, no mention of compliance in the welcome.
**Say:** `yes`
- [ ] ONE card under 120 words: the title (seven words or fewer, a number or an outcome), the promise, who it is
  for, two lines of Mike's reasoning — no lesson code. Reply line: *yes, or 'another idea'*.
**Optional:** `another idea` → a different kind (plan or proof piece), one card, says it is the other one considered.
**Say:** `yes`
- [ ] At most five intake questions, one per message, each pre-answered from Taylor's Brain; "you pick" works.
- [ ] One progress line, then quiet while it writes (two to five minutes).
- [ ] The guide: in chat only the link, the cover title and hook, five lines on what is inside. The document is in
  Content → Guides → `2026-… · [Title]` as `Lead Magnet — [Title]` — and it OPENS AS A GUIDE: a cover page (title,
  subtitle, the three hook lines, "By …"), one page per section with a Title Case heading, checkboxes and fill-in
  lines where the text has them, no "Pull quote:" / "Image idea:" labels, no design appendix. Export it as a PDF
  and it is ready to hand to an agent.
- [ ] It continues straight into the page — no "want me to write the page?" question.
- [ ] The sales funnel outline: the headline and the eleven section names in one line, and that it lives in the
  Week 3 Kit — NO document of its own.
- [ ] The Week 3 Kit: one line and the link — `Week 3 Kit — Taylor Brooks — 2026-… — DEMO` in the home base folder
  beside the Brain Book, offer and lead magnet together. No full text in chat.
- [ ] The finish line: three files — the offer, the guide, the kit — and where each lives · the kit is the Week 3
  upload · export the guide as a PDF today, upload it to the same folder, share "anyone with the link" · the bios
  phrase · the delivery phrase · the page goes live in Week 3 · the resume phrase (`finish my agent lead magnet`).
- [ ] **Count the file cards Cowork shows at the end: three** (the offer only if it was built this sitting). Seven
  was the failure the owner caught on 2026-11-09.
