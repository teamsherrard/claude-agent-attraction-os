# YouTube demo checklist — Week 5 on a demo Brain that finished Weeks 1 to 4

*What to say, in order, and what to expect. Pairs with `brain-demo-checklist.md` (Week 1), `lead-magnet-demo-checklist.md`
(Week 2) and `shortform-demo-checklist.md` (Week 4). YouTube plugin 0.7.2 · Brain 0.9.17. Every number in a demo Brain
is fictional and tagged. The week's date is still to be confirmed; the plugin is live.*

## Before the screen
- [ ] Plugins 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6 installed and current (Check for updates); the demo Brain pulled (`Demo brain: yes`).
- [ ] The demo Brain has finished Weeks 2 and 4: the offer built (`identity/offer.md` with its content branches), the
  guide live (`memory/magnets.md → ## Current magnet`), the Week 3 Kit on file (`exports/week3-kit-*.txt`, chapter 6's
  YouTube bio and chapter 7's calls-to-action table), the short-form layer set (`identity/publishing.md` with the two
  keywords and the `Content board:` URL, `identity/content-pillars.md`). Without the kit the Channel Page still builds —
  from the Brain — and says so once.
- [ ] **The demo Brain's `config.md` says `Cohort week: 5`** (the Taylor demo ships pinned to week 1; a demo Brain's board
  hides every later week, so without this line the YouTube dot stays grey however much is built). Real members never
  need this.
- [ ] Notion connected in Cowork (the YouTube tab appearing on the same board is the best moment); the member's mail
  connected if you want to show the email note. Compliance `set` (the demo default).
- [ ] A channel link to paste, or the words "starting fresh".

## The screen (one sitting, about 30 minutes live; say so)
**Say:** `set up my YouTube for agents`
- [ ] **The welcome** names Week 5 and the five things, in order (your channel → your Game Plan → your videos on your
  board → your workflows → your first video), names only what is really on file (your offer, your guide, your bios),
  says "we build it together — it works like an interview", and asks one fact: the channel. **ONE STEP PER REPLY — and it runs like an interview (the owner, 2026-11-15):** every reply opens with a win line ("✓ …" — what the member just said or did), hands the member one thing that is theirs (a question, a choice, or a few minutes in their own account) and stops on "Your turn." Nothing more is built until the member answers — after the Channel Page, each of the four questions, the proposal (shown BEFORE the plan is built), the built plan, and the board; then the workflows card and its Yes. Walk away mid-sitting and come back: it is exactly where you left it.
- [ ] **Your Channel Page:** saved as one document under Content → Long-Form, and in the chat only **the three to paste
  today**, each as a copy block with its click path above it: the channel description with the about section, the
  links (the booking link first, the guide second), the upload defaults. One line says the other pieces (keywords,
  playlists, banner, spoken lines, trailer) are in the document, and that `walk me through my channel page` takes them
  one at a time. The banner is the one the brand kit made in Week 3. No "paste it now" question, no wait.
- [ ] **The interview — four questions, ONE per reply, each after a line that plays back what is already on file:**
  (1) which of your three topics you could talk about for twenty minutes with no notes · (2) who you'd love to sit
  down with on camera first (a first name is enough) · (3) which of your stories to tell at full length first ·
  (4) the day you can film a full video. Each asks ONE thing. **Each next reply
  quotes your own words back and says what they became.** Try "skip" on one: it builds that one from your Brain
  and never asks twice. If the short-form sitting was done first, it plays back the question agents ask you ("last
  week you told me…") instead of asking it again.
- [ ] **Your direction, then the Game Plan:** after the last answer it shows your three topics and **your first three
  videos — built on your answers** — with your pace and your filming day, and a **stop**: "Does that sound like
  you?" Nothing is researched or built yet. On the Yes: one line with the research ("about [N] searches"), then two lines pointing at
  the document — never the plan in the chat — and a second stop. Open it: three niche lanes from the offer's content branches, the interview lane, the
  model lane, about fifty titles, **one video a week to start** (two named as the step-up), the first 90 days on the
  8-video cycle, the goal in conversations and calls — never income. **The first video is never dated today:** it is
  recorded on the next filming day at least three days out.
- [ ] **The board:** the same Notion Content Dashboard gains the **YouTube tab** beside Graphics, Reels and Calendar,
  with the next 30 days as dated cards (often three or four right after setup; never the whole plan). **The last
  column is `Reference Video`: one real YouTube video, close to that card's idea, on every card.** Click the link: it is
  a real video, on the topic. Click a card: the hook, **the reference video with its player, "Why this one" and "Watch
  for"**, and the brief; the rest waits for the Kickoff. The chat says it in one line: *"Each video on your board has
  one real video to watch first — found on YouTube and checked."* (A demo's reference videos are real
  ones too; nothing else in a demo is.) Notion not connected → the **Connect button appears right in the chat** and the
  sitting keeps going.
- [ ] **The consent card:** ONE short statement — the Monday Kickoff (Mondays 9:00 am) and the Monthly Channel Review
  (the 1st, 8:00 am), "both only draft", the short email note — closed by "Say Yes". No defaults to pick, no "want me
  to email you". Say Yes. On a real member's Brain it creates the two tasks only now and they appear in Cowork's
  Scheduled list. **On a demo Brain it creates nothing — a demo Brain never gets a real task — and says so in one
  line**; to show the Scheduled list, run the sitting on a real Brain.
- [ ] **The first video:** by its exact title, with its recording day in words — *"open a fresh chat and say 'make my
  attraction video: [title]'"* — the thumbnail named as made in Higgsfield, and the editing step stated: *"open a new
  chat and say 'edit my YouTube video'"* (the editor is Plugin 5, installed in Week 4). A new channel's first video is
  its trailer.
- [ ] **The close:** *"Your Brain Board and your Brain Book are updated — here they are: [the Board link] · [the Book
  link]"*, then at most three numbered next steps (paste the three channel pieces · make your first video · invite
  your first interview guest), then ONE plain line for later: *"…when you want your real view counts read for you: open
  a new chat and say 'connect my YouTube numbers'."* Nothing after that. Open the Brain Board: the YouTube page shows
  the Channel Page, the lanes, this week's video with its "watch first" line, the workflows and the board link.

## After the screen (each one phrase, two minutes)
- `run my attraction kickoff` — this week's video drafted onto its card (the hook three ways, the script start, the
  thumbnail sheet, the SEO start, the six publish boxes), the board topped up, the short note with its **▶ WATCH FIRST**
  line; the same thing Monday's workflow does.
- `script my first three videos` — the first batch: the three videos the member films alone, scripted in one
  sitting (titles, opening lines and lengths in the chat; the scripts in each video's folder and on its card), one
  filming day set on the three cards, and a one-page film-day sheet. No interview is ever in it.
- `make my attraction video: [title]` — in a fresh chat: the packaging, the script in the member's voice with the two
  CTAs, the thumbnail sheet for Higgsfield, the SEO package with the booking line first, two more reference videos with
  "the one thing to do better", and **the publish checklist filled with this video's own title, description, playlist,
  end screen and pinned comment.**
- Open the card and scroll to **✅ PUBLISH IT**: six tick boxes in YouTube Studio's order. Then `my video is published`
  — the card moves to Published.
- `repurpose my attraction video` — three short clip scripts, queued; the next Monday Week Plan turns up to two of
  them into that week's Reels.
- `connect my YouTube numbers` — its own two-minute chat: the exact clicks, one sign-in, then one small read as proof.
  Never part of setup.
- `which videos brought me agents` — the quick read from the Brain's ledgers, the source named in one line.
- `my YouTube scheduled tasks` — the two workflows and their state, the email note included.
- `edit my YouTube video` — the editor's front door (Plugin 5, installed in Week 4).

## What the demo must NOT show
- "Week 4" for anything YouTube · PARTNER/SCALE/GROWTH · "join [brokerage]" as a reason · a compensation figure or an
  income promise · a Saturday beside the member, a group chat or a 1:1 in any script or CTA · two videos a week as the
  default · the whole 90-day plan on the board · nine copy blocks in one chat message · a first video dated today · a
  reference link that does not open, a view count nobody saw, or a card with a made-up video · a thumbnail brief "for
  Claude Design" · a question about connecting live numbers during setup · a task created without the Yes on the workflows card · two steps in one reply, or anything built before you answered · a
  question about email, a posting tool or which workflows · a close without the Board and Book links · anything that
  posts, sends or publishes on its own · a skill name or a file path spoken to the member.
