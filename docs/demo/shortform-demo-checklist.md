# Short-Form demo checklist — Week 4 on a demo Brain that finished Weeks 1 to 3

*What to say, in order, and what to expect. Pairs with `brain-demo-checklist.md` (Week 1) and
`lead-magnet-demo-checklist.md` (Week 2). Short-Form plugin 0.4.0 · Riverside editor 0.4.5 · Brain 0.9.10. Every number
in a demo Brain is fictional and tagged.*

## Before the screen
- [ ] Plugins 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 installed and current (Check for updates); the demo Brain pulled (`Demo brain: yes`).
- [ ] The demo Brain has finished Week 2: the offer built, the guide written, the Week 3 Kit on file (`exports/week3-kit-*.txt`),
  the bios in `identity/profiles.md`, the live magnet in `memory/magnets.md`. Without the kit the calendar opens with Reels
  instead of the six designed posts — the sitting still runs; say so if asked.
- [ ] Notion connected in Cowork (the board is the best moment of the demo) — or leave it off to show the Connect
  button appearing right in the chat; the member's mail connected if you want to show the email note. Leave Metricool
  OFF if you want to show the posting-tool Connect button live.
- [ ] Compliance `set` (the demo default).
- [ ] **The demo Brain's `config.md` says `Cohort week: 4`** (the Taylor demo ships pinned to week 1; a demo Brain's board
  hides every later week, so without this line the Short-Form dot stays grey however much is built). Real members never
  need this — their board shows whatever is built.

## The screen (one sitting, about 25 minutes live; say so)
**Say:** `set up my attraction short-form`
- [ ] The welcome names Week 4 and the five steps (profiles and keywords → Game Plan → board → workflows → first Reel),
  then plays back who the member is from the Brain. No question box. **In the whole sitting the member says Yes exactly once (the workflows card) and names their posting tool.**
- [ ] **Profiles and keywords:** it shows the identity line and the one link, says the bios were written in Week 2, and
  asks NOTHING: Instagram and Facebook both (every Reel and graphic), LinkedIn only when the avatars include team leaders,
  the bio link = the guide. It states exactly TWO keywords: GUIDE (the guide) and the offer's own invitation word, and
  says ManyChat is required (your own account, free plan to start, never connected to Claude). No document.
- [ ] **The Game Plan:** no stop at all — it states the five pillars and the week (three a week is the default: twelve dated posts, six already designed, six to film) and builds in the same breath. The document lands
  in Content → Short-Form. Open it: the pillars come from the offer's content branches; **the 30-day calendar opens
  with the six posts designed in Week 3** ("post, don't make"), then the Reels; section 5's two asks are the guide
  (GUIDE) and the call in Mike's shape (partner with me and get my help, free, to run this).
- [ ] **The board:** the Notion Content Dashboard with its tabs — Graphics (opens first: the six kit posts at Ready to Post, never Recorded), Reels, Calendar (YouTube joins in Week 5). Click one card: the hook, the script, the caption, the ask, filming notes, sources, in that order. Without Notion the **Connect button appears right in the chat** ("Click Connect, sign in, then say 'connected'") and the sitting keeps going; nothing is lost.
- [ ] **The consent card:** ONE statement, plain words — the four it is switching on (Monday week plan 7:00 am · Sunday
  batch prep · Wednesday brokerage news watch · the monthly calendar refill), "every one only drafts", "I'll email you a
  short note when something's ready", the extras in one sentence — closed by "Say Yes". No "all, some or not yet", no
  "want me to email you". Say Yes. On a real member's Brain it creates the four tasks only now and they appear in Cowork's Scheduled list. **On a demo Brain it creates nothing — a demo Brain never gets a real task — and says so in one line**; to show the Scheduled list, run the sitting on a real Brain.
- [ ] **The posting tool — one straight question, never optional:** *"Metricool or GoHighLevel — which one?"* Name one
  and the **Connect button for it appears right in the chat** (Metricool connects in one click from Claude's own
  directory; GoHighLevel is added as its own connector, the key pasted into the connector's settings and never into the
  chat). No "or copy-paste", no "want me to schedule your posts?".
- [ ] **The first Reel:** `script my first attraction reel` → the first Reel after the six posts, hook three ways, the
  keyword, the caption with the call line in Mike's shape; no "stand next to me on a Saturday", no group chat, no 1:1.
- [ ] **The close:** the editing step, stated (*"When your first Reel is filmed, open a new chat and say 'edit my video' —
  that launches your editing plugin"*), the breadcrumb of phrases, then *"Your Brain Board and your Brain Book are
  updated — here they are: [the Board link] · [the Book link]"*. Open
  the Brain Board: the Short-Form dot is Week 4 and green; its page shows the two keywords, this week's posts, the
  workflows switched on, the board link.

## After the screen (each one phrase, two minutes)
- `run my week plan` — this week's posts drafted onto the board (captions for the six designed posts first), and any
  welcome or win post the Brain earned, behind the consent gate.
- `green screen on brokerage news` — three to six REAL headlines from the last 30 days, each with its link, outlet and
  date, and a 30-second take; one card on the board (the week's green-screen slot) with the alternates inside it; the same thing the Wednesday workflow does on its own.
- `attraction carousel` — the copy, and a LEAN build sheet for Claude Design (slides in order, final copy, placeholders);
  strategy stays in the chat.
- `how's my content doing` — the on-demand read: Metricool when connected, otherwise the member's own numbers; says which (there is no Friday note any more).
- `edit my video` — the editor's front door (Plugin 5): it reads the Video Brand Kit registered in Week 3.

## What the demo must NOT show
- A third keyword (PARTNER, SCALE, GROWTH) · "join [brokerage]" as a reason · a Saturday beside the member, a group
  chat or a 1:1 in any script · an invented headline or a link that does not open · a task created without the one Yes · a question about platforms, ManyChat, email or which workflows to switch on · the posting tool offered as optional or as "copy-paste" · a close without the editing step and the Board and Book links · "Recorded" on a designed post ·
  anything that posts, sends or schedules on its own · a skill name or a file path spoken to the member · "Week 3"
  for anything short-form.
