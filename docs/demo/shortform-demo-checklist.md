# Short-Form demo checklist — Week 4 on a demo Brain that finished Weeks 1 to 3

*What to say, in order, and what to expect. Pairs with `brain-demo-checklist.md` (Week 1) and
`lead-magnet-demo-checklist.md` (Week 2). Short-Form plugin 0.10.0 (Mike's simplicity rework, 2026-11-22) · AI Editing
Studio 0.7.3 · Brain 0.9.22. Every number in a demo Brain is fictional and tagged.*

**The shape in one line (Mike):** *"Record these two videos this week, and post this carousel. That's it."* Two Reels the
member films and one carousel they post — three posts, every week, from week one; ONE Monday workflow; the sitting ends at
the first Reel's script.

## Before the screen
- [ ] Plugins 1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 installed and current (Check for updates); the demo Brain pulled (`Demo brain: yes`).
- [ ] The demo Brain has finished Week 2: the offer built, the guide written, the Week 3 Kit on file (`exports/week3-kit-*.txt`),
  the bios in `identity/profiles.md`, the live magnet in `memory/magnets.md`. Without the kit the week's carousel is
  written from the Game Plan instead of a designed post — the sitting still runs; say so if asked.
- [ ] Notion connected in Cowork (the board is a good moment of the demo) — or leave it off to show the Connect
  button appearing right in the chat; the member's mail connected if you want to show the email note. Leave Metricool
  OFF to show its Connect button appearing live.
- [ ] Compliance `set` (the demo default).
- [ ] **The demo Brain's `config.md` says `Cohort week: 4`** (the Taylor demo ships pinned to week 1; a demo Brain's board
  hides every later week, so without this line the Short-Form dot stays grey however much is built). Real members never
  need this — their board shows whatever is built.

## The screen (one sitting, about 30 minutes live; say so)
**Say:** `set up my attraction short-form`

**What to watch for all the way through (the owner, 2026-11-15 and 2026-11-16; Mike, 2026-11-22):** the member is part of
it at every step, it asks very little, and it says little. At each step it shows what it built and waits for a Yes or a
change. It asks only ONE question — the filming day, word for word the same for every member — in plain chat, never on a
card with choices to tap. Every reply: **a win line** of one clause ("✓ …"), **one thing that is theirs**, and **the stop**
("Your turn."). No play-back of what the Brain holds, no recap of what is done, no list of what comes later.
- [ ] **The welcome** is short: five steps named (profiles and your word[s] → Game Plan — two Reels and one carousel a
  week → content board → Monday Week Plan and Metricool → first Reel), "I know you from your Brain, so I won't ask you
  any of that again — just which day you can film", then "Say Yes and I'll start with your profiles". **No play-back of
  who the member is**, no "you are that". Say Yes.
- [ ] **Profiles and keywords — shown, never asked:** the identity line and the one link, the bios from Week 2,
  Instagram and Facebook both, LinkedIn only when the avatars include team leaders, exactly TWO keywords (GUIDE and
  the offer's own word). No document. The reply closes "✓ Step 1 of 5 — done" and ends on **the one question**.
- [ ] **The one blanket question, exactly these words, as plain text in the chat:** *"Which day could you block about
  three hours every week to film, in the same spot — and where is that: your office or your home?"* **Three hours, not
  "an hour"; office or home, never "your car".** No card, no choices to tap. Try **"skip"**: it takes the day from the
  Brain and never asks twice. Walk away before answering and come back ("continue my attraction short-form"): it asks
  the same question again. **Nothing else is asked** — not what the member does off the clock, not what agents say to
  them, nothing from Week 1, nothing about email, platforms, ManyChat or the posting tool.
- [ ] **Your direction, then the Game Plan:** the reply to the answer quotes the filming day back in one line and shows
  the five pillars — **one line each, no source labels** — and the week (two Reels to film and one carousel to post;
  their filming day), and stops: "If that sounds like you, say Yes…" Nothing is built yet. Say Yes: now it builds, and
  the document lands in Content → Short-Form. Open it: **four weeks, every week the same shape — two Reels and one
  carousel, from week one; the kit's designed posts are each week's carousel, one a week.** Count the asks: **about one
  post in ten carries the keyword ask** (Comment GUIDE, or the offer's word with the call line in Mike's shape — partner
  with me and get my help, free, to run this); every other post asks only for a follow, a save, a share or a comment.
  The filming line says three hours and names Instagram's free Edits app.
- [ ] **The board:** the Notion Content Dashboard with its tabs — Graphics (this week's carousel at Ready to Post, never
  Recorded), Reels (this week's two at the top), Calendar (YouTube joins in Week 5). Click one card: the hook, the
  script, the caption, the ask, filming notes, sources, in that order. Without Notion the **Connect button appears right
  in the chat** ("Click Connect, sign in, then say 'connected'") and the sitting keeps going; nothing is lost. The stop
  line: "this week is at the top: your two Reels and your carousel. Say Yes and I'll switch on your Monday Week Plan."
- [ ] **The consent card — ONE workflow:** one statement, plain words — *"your Monday Week Plan — every Monday, 7:00 am,
  your week in one package: the scripts for your two Reels, the cards you read from while you film, your carousel's
  caption, and one industry story to react to when there's a good one. It only drafts; nothing posts on its own. I'll
  email you a short note when it's ready. Say Yes and I'll set it up."* **Nothing else is named** — no Batch Prep, no
  news watch, no calendar refill, no list of by-phrase extras. No "all, some or not yet", no "want me to email you".
  Say Yes. On a real member's Brain it creates ONE task only now and it appears in Cowork's Scheduled list. **On a demo
  Brain it creates nothing — a demo Brain never gets a real task — and says so in one line**; to show the Scheduled
  list, run the sitting on a real Brain.
- [ ] **Metricool — stated, never asked:** in the same reply as "✓ Step 4 of 5 — done. You switched on your Monday Week
  Plan": *"Your posts go out through Metricool — it's free to start. Click Connect, sign in, then say 'connected'."* The
  **Connect button appears right in the chat**. No "Metricool or GoHighLevel — which one?", no "or copy-paste", no "want
  me to schedule your posts?". (GoHighLevel steps appear only if you say "I already run GoHighLevel".)
- [ ] **The first Reel — you choose how it opens:** it names the first Reel on the calendar (week one's first Reel) and
  shows three openings — say 1, 2 or 3, or say it your own way. Only then is the script written, around the opening
  you chose: hook three ways with yours first, the soft ask (a follow, a save, a share or a comment — the keyword only
  if this is the one-in-ten), the caption; **its filming line names the app:** *"Film it on your phone in Instagram's
  free Edits app — it has a teleprompter built in: paste the script, press record, read."* No "stand next to me on a
  Saturday", no group chat, no 1:1.
- [ ] **The close is short and the sitting ENDS there.** "✓ Step 5 of 5 — done. Here's what you did today:" — **four
  lines at most**, each beginning "You…" (profiles and word[s] · filming day and place · Game Plan: two Reels and one
  carousel a week · Monday Week Plan and Metricool, and the opening of the first Reel) — then *"Your Brain Board and
  your Brain Book are updated — here they are: [the Board link] · [the Book link]"* — then **This week:** three numbered
  lines: 1. film your two Reels on [day] (this one's script is above; Edits app, teleprompter) · 2. post your carousel
  on [day] · 3. when a Reel is filmed, open a new chat and say "edit my reel". **Nothing after line 3**: no ManyChat
  line, no list of phrases, no extra workflows, no stories routine, no "later you can…". Open the Brain Board: the
  Short-Form dot is Week 4 and green; its page shows the two keywords, this week's three posts, the Monday Week Plan.

## After the screen (each one phrase, two minutes)
- `run my week plan` — the week in one package onto the board: the two Reel scripts and the cards to film from, the
  carousel's caption, at most one industry story when there is a good one; any welcome or win post the Brain earned,
  behind the consent gate.
- `green screen on brokerage news` — ONE real industry story from the last 30 days (lead generation, the market, new
  agents struggling — never a brokerage headline, whatever the phrase says), with its link, outlet and date, and a
  30-second take with a soft ask; one card on the board.
- `attraction carousel` — the copy, and a LEAN build sheet for Claude Design (slides in order, final copy, placeholders);
  strategy stays in the chat.
- `how's my content doing` — the on-demand read: Metricool when connected, otherwise the member's own numbers; says which.
- `set up my ManyChat` — on demand only: the steps for the member's own account, never part of the sitting.
- `edit my reel` — the editor's front door (Plugin 5): it reads the Video Brand Kit registered in Week 3.

## What the demo must NOT show — each of these is a defect (or a version before 0.10.0)
- **Four workflows** on the consent card, or the words "Batch Prep", "news watch" or "calendar refill" anywhere in the
  sitting · **"about an hour"** or **"your car"** (or "your kitchen") in the filming question · **a "Metricool or
  GoHighLevel" question** · **a ManyChat line in the chat** (a "ManyChat build sheet", "set up ManyChat this week") ·
  **a calendar that opens with carousels, then graphics, then Reels** — any week without two Reels, or a block of
  designed posts before the first Reel · the keyword ask on most posts (the 90/10 rule inverted) · a brokerage headline
  as the industry story, or more than one story a week · a filming app other than Instagram's Edits, or none named · a
  welcome that plays the Brain back · a win line longer than one clause · a close with more than four "You…" lines, or
  anything after the three "This week" lines (phrases, workflows, stories, "later you can…").
- A third keyword (PARTNER, SCALE, GROWTH) · "join [brokerage]" as a reason · a Saturday beside the member, a group
  chat or a 1:1 in any script · an invented headline or a link that does not open · a task created without the Yes on
  the card · two steps in one reply, or anything built before you answered · a second question · a question on a card
  with choices to tap · a question worded differently for this member · a question that assumes the member has
  attracted an agent ("what do agents ask you", "who have you helped") · a question the Brain already answers (anything
  from Week 1) · a "skip" that gets asked again · a question about platforms, email or which workflows to switch on ·
  the posting tool offered as optional or as "copy-paste" · a close without the Board and Book links · "Recorded" on a
  designed post · anything that posts, sends or schedules on its own · a skill name or a file path spoken to the
  member · "Week 3" for anything short-form.
