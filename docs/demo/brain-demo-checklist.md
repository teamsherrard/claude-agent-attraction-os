# AI Brain plugin — demo checklist (the rebuilt Week 1 setup)

Run it in Cowork. Everything the member hears is in bold; everything you check is a box. The whole run is
four short conversations (26 questions, one per message), three answers out loud, then the Book. Answering
as Taylor with the short lines below takes about 25 minutes; a real member takes 45 minutes to an hour.

## Before you start
- [ ] Marketplace re-synced in Cowork (claude-agent-attraction-os at v0.13.0 or later; Brain plugin 0.4.0).
- [ ] Google Drive connected (the home base and the Book are created there). A Microsoft member connects Microsoft 365 instead; the plugin detects which.
- [ ] A fresh chat in a demo project, model **Opus**, effort **Medium** (the plugin asks for this on screen).
- [ ] Voice input ready for the last part: Cowork's microphone in the message box, or your phone's voice-to-text to paste from.
- [ ] Decide which member you are playing. The house demo member is **Taylor Brooks · Real Broker · Austin** (answers below). Saying "demo" or "fictional" in the first message switches on demo mode: every figure is tagged "(illustrative — demo)", the Book is watermarked "— DEMO —", and the demo gets its own workspace. For the Week 1 training video, run it as yourself with no demo wording: same flow, real Brain.

## The run

### 1. Open (two "your turn" moments)
**Say:** `build a demo attraction brain for Taylor Brooks` (or, for your real Brain: `set up my attraction brain`)
Expect: *✓ Connected to your Google Drive* · *✓ No existing Brain found, building yours fresh* · the promise in plain words (four short conversations and three questions out loud, about 45 minutes to an hour, you get your Agent Attraction Brain Book and a home base folder, pause any time) · the Opus-at-Medium tip · then ONE question: *what should we call your home base?* **Your turn.**
- [ ] One question only. No menu, no "how deep do you want to go".

**Say (as Taylor):** `Agent Attraction OS`
Expect: *✓ Your home base is ready, here's the link* (one line). Then ONE question: do you have an old deck, a bio, an onboarding doc or past videos to drop in? **Your turn.**
- [ ] It never mentions sync, push, markers, scaffolds or file names.

**Say:** `skip`

### 2. Conversation 1 — who you are as a leader (13 questions, one per message)
Expect a one-line orientation, then question 1. Every question ends with "your turn". Answer in one message each:
1. *What's your name?* → `Taylor Brooks`
2. *What brokerage are you with?* → `Real Broker`
3. *What's your local market?* → `Austin, TX. Travis and Williamson counties.`
4. *Solo, team leader, or broker owner?* → `Solo. Building a revenue share organization.`
5. *How long licensed?* → `Since January 2021, so my sixth year.`
6. *Before real estate?* → `Nine years teaching high-school history.`
7. *Why real estate, your big WHY?* → `I wanted work where effort shows up in the result. And eventually my Sundays back, for my family.`
8. *Why build a revenue share organization?* → `The routine works in other people's hands, and I'm a teacher. A group that teaches the next person is the only version of this I actually want.`
9. *When did you join your current brokerage?* → `March 2023`
10. *The story behind choosing it?* → `A friend from licensing class kept describing a Tuesday call where agents taught each other what worked that week. I ignored it for most of a year, I figured a cloud brokerage meant an office with no walls. Then I sat in as a guest, watched a four-year agent walk through her sign-in sheet, and thought: that's a classroom. I didn't move for a plan. I moved because the people on that call were teaching.`
11. *Why your sponsor specifically?* → `Jordan built the kind of business I want, a group that teaches, not a roster that counts. Jordan runs our Tuesday teaching call and takes the three-way calls where the model gets walked through with the real documents.`
12. *Describe your story (the long one).* → `Licensed in January 2021 after nine years teaching. Eight months at a franchise desk with a phone, a headshot and no syllabus. Two closings, both from a former colleague. Year two I signed up for online leads because the next desks were doing it, and spent fourteen months chasing people who clicked a photo once, while my open houses quietly produced eleven closings. The low point was an invoice that arrived in a month with zero closings. I opened it at the kitchen table and thought, I'm paying rent on a pipeline I don't own. One Sunday in October 2022 I pulled every sign-in sheet from the year, sat down with a legal pad and wrote a five-step routine: every name gets one text by 6 pm Sunday, every text asks one question, every answer goes on one sheet. Three of those names bought the next quarter. I cancelled the leads in December. By the end of 2023 it was the business: about twenty closings a year, sixty percent from a Saturday sign-in, no lead bill. The Saturday gets you the names. The Sunday gets you the clients.`
13. *The moment selling houses wasn't enough, and why revenue share is the key?* → `August 2026. Priya, who joined my group in June, texted a screenshot of her third buyer contract in sixty days: "the routine works, Taylor." Watching it work in her hands felt bigger than any closing I've had. Selling houses is a result that happens once. A group that teaches the next agent keeps going, and nobody's counter resets in January. Other agents should care because the first agent you help is the first time your business stops starting from zero.`
- [ ] Thirteen separate questions, Mike's wording, no bundles, no follow-ups except possibly one on question 10 or 12 if an answer is thin.
- [ ] Nothing about CRM, calendar links, scheduling, compliance, brand, which agent type or where you attract.
Expect at the end: *That's 1 of 5, who you are is written* + the next orientation line + question 14, all in ONE message.

### 3. Conversation 2 — your unique approach (4 questions)
Expect the framing first: *we're not writing your offer today, that's next week; I want to know what's already in your hands.*
14. *Strategies you've used, or are passionate about and could teach?* → `Open houses plus a Sunday follow-up routine. Also my sphere from teaching, but I never built a system for that.`
15. *Your unique strategy for it?* → `A two-question sign-in sheet. Every name texted by 6 pm Sunday, one question per text. One tracker, a Tuesday look. Five steps on one sheet: Host, Ask, Text, Track, Table.`
16. *What value does your upline provide?* → `Jordan's Tuesday teaching call for the whole group, and Jordan on a three-way call for anyone who wants the model walked through properly.`
17. *Biggest mistakes agents are making, and why you're the solution?* → `Paying for names instead of following up the ones they already have. Everybody hosts the Saturday, almost nobody texts the sheet on Sunday. And trying five things at once. I did both. I can show the exact Sunday that fixed it, and it costs a Saturday, not a subscription. And here you're not learning it alone, there's a Tuesday table every week.`
- [ ] It never calls this "your offer" and never asks for a value proposition.
Expect: *2 of 5, next week has plenty to build from* + orientation + question 18 in one message.

### 4. Conversation 3 — your story and your proof (5 questions)
18. *The moment you almost quit?* → `The invoice in the zero-closing month, with the teaching-jobs tab open on the same screen. What kept me going was the column next to it: the open-house sign-ins had quietly produced most of that year's closings. I'd been looking at the wrong column.`
19. *Mistakes and what they taught you?* → `A relocating couple I met at an open house and never texted. They bought with someone else on the Tuesday. The follow-up IS the business. And fourteen months paying for names I didn't need.`
20. *Biggest achievements?* → `About twenty closings a year the last two years, roughly sixty percent from open-house sign-ins. About one buyer client per two open houses since the routine started. No awards I want public. The routine is the credential.`
21. *Success stories of agents you've helped?* → `Priya Nair. I ran the routine with her from day one, me on Zoom for her first two Sundays. Three buyers under contract in her first sixty days. And Marcus D., the week-one syllabus, Module 1 together, first open house within ten days; he texted all eleven first-Sunday sign-ins and booked a showing from one in week three.`
22. *Why did your agents join YOU?* → `Priya sat in on three Tuesday tables as a guest before she asked. She joined for the table, not the brokerage. Marcus's licensing instructor sent him: "go find the teacher." Neither asked about the model until after they'd run a Sunday.`
- [ ] Proof is kept as stated; nothing inflated, no invented awards.
Expect: *3 of 5, your stories and your proof are in, and I know who they'll land with* (who your story speaks to is DERIVED here, never asked) + question 23.

### 5. Conversation 4 — current and future state (4 questions)

### 6. The voice stop — three answers out loud (one per message)
Expect the instruction: use the microphone in the message box, talk like you're on the phone with an agent you like, 30 seconds to two minutes each. Use voice input, or paste these:
- V1 *Why are you excited about agent attraction?* → `Okay so, the honest answer is Priya. I taught the routine to one person and it worked in her hands, and that did something to me a closing never did. I've been teaching my whole adult life, and real estate was the first job where I only got paid for what I did myself. Attraction is the first version of this business where the thing I'm actually good at, which is teaching, is the thing that builds it. And the routine is small. It's a Saturday and a Sunday. I don't have to sell anybody anything, I just have to show them the sheet.`
- V2 *Your WHY?* → `My family first. My husband and the kids. I want Sundays that are actually ours, and yes, Sunday is the routine, but the routine is ninety minutes and then it's done. And then the teachers, the people I used to share a hallway with, who think the only way out of a salary that never moves is a second job. I was one of them. If one of them gets their license and the first thing they get is a syllabus instead of a lead bill, that's it, that's the why.`
- V3 *Why should someone partner with you?* → `Because I'll sit next to you for the first three Sundays. That's the actual answer. Not a training library, a person. You bring your sign-in sheet to the first call, I give you the five texts, and we run it on your names, live. Priya had three buyers under contract in sixty days doing exactly that. I'm not going to tell you about the model on Instagram, I'll walk you through it on a call with the real documents. I'd rather show you than tell you.`
- [ ] One prompt per message, no read-back, no "did I get that right".
Expect: *5 of 5, I've got your voice. Building your Book now.*

## Optional moves if there's time (each one phrase)
- `open my brain board` — the Brain as a living map: tiles for goals, today, this week and open items; click any dot for its page.
- `show me my Brain` — regenerates the Book from the Brain, no re-interview.
- `open my attraction brain` — the READY BRIEF: three or four things it knows about you, the week you're in, what to type next.
- `paste my compliance link` — the whole compliance setup is a link from the brokerage site, no questions.
- `set my attraction goals` — locks the weekly rhythm behind the 12-month number (on demand, never at setup).
- `just talked to an agent` — hands-free capture from the car into the Brain.

## If something looks off
- Two questions in one message, or a question card with chips on a story question → bug.
- It asks about CRM, calendar links, scheduled tasks, compliance, brand colours, agent type, or where you attract → bug (all are Week 2, the brand chat, or on demand).
- The word "beats", or any file name, step number or sync talk reaches the screen → bug.
- The Book is longer than fifteen pages, has a money chapter, or Section 9 points anywhere except the brand chat and Week 2 → bug.
- "Launch the agent attraction OS" on a built Brain starts the interview again → bug; it should give the READY BRIEF.
