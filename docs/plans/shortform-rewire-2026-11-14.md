## 2. What changes in the journey (`sf-setup`)

Same one-session shape, about 25 minutes, nothing re-asked — with these changes:
1. **Welcome** names Week 4 and the five steps: confirm your profiles and keyword → your Game Plan → your board →
   your workflows → your first Reel.
2. **Profiles = confirm, never rewrite.** The bios exist (`identity/profiles.md`, chapter 6 of the kit). The step
   shows the identity line and the one link, asks the three platform questions only where the Brain is silent
   (platforms in order · link-in-bio tool · LinkedIn yes/no), and refreshes a bio only when the offer, the proof or
   the funnel URL changed since it was written. No Profile Optimization Pack document: the bios live in the kit and
   the Brain; a refreshed bio is shown in chat as a copy block and written back to `profiles.md`.
3. **Two keywords, never more.** `GUIDE` delivers the guide (the magnet's keyword, set in Week 2); the offer's
   `Comment keyword:` (e.g. BUYERS) is the invitation word. PARTNER, SCALE, GROWTH and any other default keyword are
   retired. The keyword step confirms the two in one line; the ManyChat copy, the keyword sheet and every Reel's ask
   use one of the two.
4. **The Game Plan** reads the kit first: the five pillars and their angles come from `## Content branches`; the
   30-day calendar's **first rows are the six kit posts** (already designed in Week 3 — "post, don't make"), then the
   Reels; the CTAs are the kit's CALLS TO ACTION table verbatim (the caption line, the pop-up, the spoken line, the
   end card); the two CTAs of section 5 are the guide (GUIDE) and the call (the booking line, in the law's shape —
   §4). Two stops stay (the pillars as one block; the cadence in one word).
5. **The board stays Notion** (the owner, 2026-11-14: "Notion is important"): the one Content Dashboard, the
   This Week (default), Short-Form and Calendar tabs, the thirty days as dated cards (twelve at the default three a week), every card page in the spec's locked layout; bring-your-own, never required; the Brain Board (§6) is the
   read-only map, the Notion board is the working surface.
6. **The consent card** offers the six recommended workflows (§5) in plain words with times, and names the optional
   extras in one sentence ("say the word any time"). Provision only on a yes; record every line in the config
   block; never claim a schedule that did not save.
7. **The first win** stays: Reel row 1 scripted now (the first Reel after the six kit posts), or today's stories.
8. **The close rebuilds the Brain Board** (§6) and says so in one line, then the phrase breadcrumb.

## 3. Rules that bind every writer

- **The CTA law (Mike, 2026-11-10).** Every call ask is angled at partnering with the PERSON to get their help,
  free, to run the method — never the brokerage. Join register (`Join-gated package allowed: yes` or `unknown`):
  *"If you want my help running this — the lessons, the templates, the community — partner with me and it's free.
  Grab [the call]."* Open register (`no`): *"I'll walk you through it, free, no strings."* The method is named; the
  brokerage is never the reason; compensation never. Captions and end cards take their call line from the kit's
  CALLS TO ACTION table.
- **Scalable by design (doctrine §10e).** Nothing in a script, a story prompt, a caption or a calendar row promises
  the member's time per partner: no "stand next to me on a Saturday", no group chat, no 1:1s, no "I answer my
  phone". The deliverables are recorded lessons, templates, the community, the weekly group call only when the
  member already runs one. The member's OWN batch-filming day (a Saturday, a Sunday) is their routine and is fine.
- **The whole method, not one tactic.** Reels and carousels that teach draw from the framework's steps and the
  guide's pages (the blueprint); the guide is the resource every ask points at.
- **Claude Design hand-offs are lean build sheets (the owner, 2026-11-11).** A carousel doc handed to the Design
  Studio's content skill is page order + final copy + placeholders only — slide 1 … slide N, the caption, the
  sizes, the brand pointer ("your Design Package brief"), the band `THE DESIGN BRIEF FOR AA-CONTENT-DESIGN` kept
  (the design skill keys on it) but short. Strategy (why this hook, the rung, what to watch) goes in the chat reply
  and the Brain's content-log row, never in the design doc. Doc name unchanged: `YYYY-MM-DD · Carousel · [Short
  Topic]`.
- **Member-facing language.** Members are often 60 to 75 and not marketers: plain words, one question at a time,
  never a menu when the Brain answers, never a skill id or a file path in a sentence to the member, never "Brand
  HQ", never "Week 3" for short-form work. A missing input gets one warm line with the phrase that creates it
  ("launch my lead magnet plugin" · "build my partner offer" · "my attraction brand direction").
- **Other plugins by condition, never by name to the member.** The Riverside editor ("when you have the editor
  installed"), the YouTube system ("a later week"), Conversion ("when that plugin is installed") — side doors, never
  requirements; the Design Studio's content skill is "your Claude Design content skill".
- **Compliance** three states as today; the stamp on every public piece; nothing posts on its own, ever.
- **Descriptions** ≤ 1,000 characters, `description: >` block scalars, `name:` = the directory, no hyphenated word
  split by the fold; trigger phrases must not collide with the realtor marketplace (the gate checks).

**Telling the member (the owner, 2026-11-14).** The consent card carries one extra yes, `Email me:` — when a workflow
leaves something ready for the member (the Monday week plan, the Sunday batch prep, the Wednesday news watch, the
Friday note, a win post), it sends ONE short email to the member's OWN address through their connected mail (Gmail or
Outlook; never to anyone else, never a campaign): the subject says what is ready, the body carries the doc link and
the two or three lines that matter. Every workflow also leaves its note in Cowork (the task's own result) and
rebuilds the Brain Board. Recorded in the config block as `Email me: yes YYYY-MM-DD` · `no` · `not offered yet`. No
connected mail → the task says so once and the Cowork note and the board carry it.

Live Instagram numbers come only through the member's connected data (the Composio data engine, "when connected");
otherwise the task works from what the member logs ("add my numbers") and says so in one line.

## 6. The Brain Board — always current (the owner, 2026-11-14)

Every skill that writes the Brain pushes through `attraction-brain-sync` immediately (write → push → verify); the
sync re-renders the member's Brain Board on every push, from the WHOLE local Brain (a partial pull is completed
first). Therefore: (1) `sf-setup`'s last push — after the first Reel — rebuilds the board, and the close says *"your
Brain Board is updated: the Short-Form dot is green"*; (2) every scheduled run ends with a push; (3) the texts the
board shows are kept in `~/attraction-brain/exports/` (the Brain syncs `exports/*.txt`): the Game Plan as
`short-form-game-plan-YYYY-MM-DD.txt`, each Week Plan as `week-plan-YYYY-MM-DD.txt`, each Friday note as
`short-form-performance-YYYY-MM-DD.txt`, the keyword sheet as `keyword-sheet-YYYY-MM-DD.txt`. The Brain Board's
Short-Form page (rendered by the Brain plugin) shows: the two keywords, the five pillars, this week's calendar rows
with their status, the workflows switched on, the board link, the posting tool, the last Friday note's numbers and
the phrases to say.
