# Short-Form Attraction Doctrine — Mike Sherrard's short-form methodology for agent attraction (source of truth)

Every short-form skill follows this. When a skill says "follow Mike's frameworks," it means this file. Where
anything else in the plugin disagrees with this, **this wins.** Every rule is cited to the lesson it comes from
(`module/lesson` under `knowledge/transcripts/`), to the Week 3 cohort doc, or to the workshop (launching) doc.
Where the vault is thin, §15 says so — nothing in here is invented.

> **Scope.** This file owns short-form *execution* for agent attraction: what a Reel, a story, a carousel, and a
> green-screen reaction must do, the five pillars, the CTA ladder, and Mike's Instagram rules. The big stance
> (attraction vs. recruiting, the leader identity, the five pains, the offer, objections) lives in the Brain
> plugin's `attraction-doctrine.md`; long-form lives in the YouTube plugin. The three agree where they touch.
> **The member** is the leader we serve; **agents** are the people they attract (never "leads", "recruits",
> "downline" out loud).

---

## 0. The stance in one paragraph
Short form is not about views. Its job is to move an agent from *never heard of you* to *in a conversation with
you*: **Awareness → Recognition → Familiarity → Trust → Curiosity → Conversation** (Week 3 cohort doc). Reels
give the member width — reach into agents who don't follow them yet; stories give depth — the daily, unpolished
connection that makes an agent feel they already know them (`07-instagram/88`, `/89`, `/90`). Every post is
intentional, sits in one of the five pillars, speaks to an agent's *problem* and never to a brokerage *feature*,
ends on a rung of the CTA ladder, and carries the keyword. Nothing pitches; nothing talks badly about anyone;
compensation stays off the feed.

## 1. What short form must accomplish (`07-instagram/86`, Week 3 doc)
- **Instagram is the digital first impression and the first credibility test.** Agents find the member on
  YouTube (where booked calls come from), then go to Instagram to see who they really are: "what you post
  signals whether you're worth following or partnering with" (`07-instagram/86`).
- **Attraction comes from intention.** Instagram is the recruiting stage, not a place to post for the sake of
  posting. Every post fits a bucket. The test before posting: *"Would a prospective agent see me as a leader
  worth joining from this post?"* If no, reconsider it (`07-instagram/86`).
- **Content echoes leadership, not desperation.** Agents who show up and all they care about is recruiting —
  "EXP-ing over everybody" — give people "a massive ick" (`07-instagram/86`). Post like a leader, not a
  salesperson: value that educates and inspires, public recognition of agents, mentorship on show, authenticity
  balanced with professionalism.
- **What the feed should say:** *I'm a leader with a following · agents succeed in my world · there's culture
  and recognition here · we're building something bigger than transactions · ambitious agents belong here*
  (`07-instagram/86`).
- **Consistency builds authority; inconsistent leaders are not attractive** (`07-instagram/86`, `/90`).
- **The ladder, stated once:** Reels = awareness and recognition; stories = familiarity and trust; the CTA +
  keyword = curiosity; the DM = conversation. Reels plus stories together "build a massive attraction funnel"
  (`07-instagram/89`).

## 2. Chasing vs. attracting (`05-big-picture/35`)
- **Chasing** = cold calling agents who don't know you, constantly pitching and convincing, "selling your soul."
  It feels pushy, transactional, desperate; it creates resistance so agents pull away. Most recruiters burn out.
- **Attracting** = become the leader agents seek out. A personal brand that builds trust *before* the first
  conversation; value shared through content that pulls agents toward you; positioned as the guide to their next
  level. Agents arrive curious: "how can I be part of what you're doing?"
- **Why it wins:** agents join faster (about 80% of their mind is made up before the call), fewer objections, less
  competition (they're rarely interviewing others), longer retention (proud to be your partner), and you attract
  the right agents — "you attract who you are" (`05-big-picture/35`).
- Mike's own figures (two YouTube videos a week for five-plus years; hundreds of agents booking in from content;
  never reached out to a single agent; never spent on ads) are **his**, cited to `/35` — never implied as typical,
  never written into a member's content.
- **Doctrine for skills:** every output pulls; nothing pitches. A request for a "join my brokerage" post, a cold
  DM, or a mass invite is reframed as value first, then an invitation on the ladder (§8).

## 3. Becoming attractive (`05-big-picture/36`)
"If you want to attract, you have to become attractive. This has nothing to do with your appearance." The
checklist Mike gives — and what it means for content:

| Mike's question (`/36`) | What the feed shows |
|---|---|
| Do you show up with energy — every call, every video, even exhausted? | Energy on camera, every Reel; no low-effort "posting to post" |
| How do you speak to yourself? How do you treat others? "I've never written a negative comment on anybody's content, ever" | **No negative comment, take, or dig at anyone or any brokerage — ever** |
| How much value do you share for free? "Information's no longer novel… share it all" | Authority content teaches the real thing, not a tease that defaults to "buy my program" |
| How disciplined are you — business, health, family? Are you a role model? | Personality and story content shows the discipline and the routines, honestly |
| Would you follow yourself? | The post test from §1 |
| Excuses, or ownership? Lead by example, or talk about it? | Behind-the-scenes shows the doing, not the announcing |
| Strong agents follow strong people | New agents follow a clear value proposition; top producers, team leaders, and brokers follow strength |

**The attractive-leader checklist:** radiate confidence, not ego ("don't flaunt" — the cars and the house don't
define you); certainty in the future; clarity and conviction; share knowledge freely; create an environment of
growth and recognition; live at a standard agents aspire to (`/36`).
**Doctrine for skills:** nothing flexes a price tag; material things appear only inside a story with meaning
(the way Mike frames his mom's car — `06-content-framework/39`); discipline shows, ego never does.

## 4. The big picture — where short form sits (`05-big-picture/35b`)
Before you start: niche + value proposition (a mastermind call is enough to begin). Then: **post consistent
content on your niche and make the value proposition known → include calls to action telling agents they can
partner with you and get all of it free → they book a discovery call → you present the model and why you
specifically → handle objections, bridge the gap → they commit → onboard, plug in, support, retain → they
attract → duplication.** Short form owns the middle of that sentence: content + CTA → the booked call. The call
itself belongs to the Conversion plugin; the booking link lives in the Brain.

## 5. The five pillars (canonical: Authority · Perspective · Story · Proof · Personality)
The Week 3 cohort doc names the five: **Authority, Perspective, Story, Proof, Personality** — "so not every post
is instructional." The workshop (launching doc, Day 3) teaches the same five as **Teach · Story · Opinion · Proof ·
Personality**: Teach = Authority, Opinion = Perspective. Mike's four recorded content types (`06-content-framework/37–40`)
and his Reels buckets (`07-instagram/88`) map onto them like this:

| Pillar | Workshop name | Mike's lesson(s) | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Authority** | Teach — "demonstrate expertise" | Value-based content `06/37`; "echo your value proposition" `07/88` | The niche dissected into as many specific, simple, tactical pieces as possible — "cast a wide net around your niche so whatever they search, they find you" (`06/37`). On Reels: the tip of the iceberg — scripts, prospecting tips, attraction strategies, social media and branding advice, tools and systems, leadership frameworks; what's below the water is what partners get (`07/88`). Teach it for real; start with one video. |
| **Perspective** | Opinion — "differentiate beliefs and perspective" | `07/88` other Reels; `07/90` Friday "rant" | Opinion pieces and rants about the industry; green-screen takes on industry news ("shows conviction and authority"); myth-busting (misconceptions about real estate or about your brokerage); future-focus (AI, tech, where it's going); Q&A on the questions agents actually ask (`07/88`). Cardinal rules apply to every word (§11). |
| **Story** | Story — "create emotional connection" | Personal brand `06/39`; storytelling `06/40`; `07/88` | Your journey, vision, and leadership lessons; the mistakes you made and what you learned ("makes you more human"); challenges and how you overcame them, which makes you relatable to agents facing them now (`06/39`, `07/88`). "Facts tell, stories sell" (`06/40`). Former brokerages are never named. |
| **Proof** | Proof — "demonstrate results" | BTS & leadership `06/38`; storytelling `06/40`; culture & recognition `07/88` | Behind the scenes of leading: team wins on the weekly calls, agents applying your training and getting results, new training you're building, events and experiences — "not us saying our culture is incredible; you're seeing it" (`06/38`). Agent breakthroughs, by design across every age, background, market size, and personality so any agent finds someone they relate to; "no success story is too big or too small" — one deal in 30 days is the relatable story for the 71% who closed zero last year (`06/40`). Recognition content creates FOMO; tag the agent so they reshare and it reaches a whole new audience (`07/88`, `/89`). |
| **Personality** | Personality — "connect with the person behind the business" | `06/39`; personal Reels `07/88`; `07/89` | Lifestyle, family, fitness, travel, hobbies, daily routines, values, passions outside real estate — "people join people, not just leaders" (`07/88`). Authentic, unpolished moments. The patio-beer test: would an agent choose to spend a Friday with you? If not, they're probably not joining (`07/89`). Personal posts about passions and hobbies connect with agents who share them (launching doc, Authority content engine). |

- **Brokerage and opportunity content is woven through these pillars; it is never the feed** (launching doc,
  Day 3: "explain how brokerage/opportunity content can be woven through those categories rather than becoming
  their entire feed").
- **The Brain stores the member's pillars** in `identity/content-pillars.md` (written by `sf-setup`): each pillar
  mapped to their avatars, their stories, their positioning, their proof. Skills read it; they never re-derive it.
- **Static posts** (recognition graphics, a personal photo) are fine spliced in between Reels (`07/88`).

## 6. Problems, not brokerage features (Week 3 doc; `07-instagram/86`; `02-prospect-targeting/18` via the Brain doctrine)
Agents leave problems, not companies. Content speaks to the agent's problem — the five pains in Mike's framing:
**financial uncertainty · lack of support, mentorship, training · technology gaps · limited growth · work-life
balance and recognition** — and shows the member solving it, in the member's own lane. The brokerage is the
vehicle; the member is the reason (`03-model-positioning/17`). So:
- A Reel about "our cap / our split / our stock" is a brokerage-feature post → rewrite as the problem it solves
  for the avatar, with the mechanics saved for the private call.
- "Agents succeed when they're in my world" is shown through Proof, not claimed.
- Compensation — splits, caps, stock, rev-share tiers, income — **never appears in content** (compliance
  doctrine; `positioning.md`'s "what stays for the private call").

## 7. The short-form video (structure, length, production)
These mechanics are Mike's short-form frameworks carried over from his realtor system (the plan copies them);
the attraction-specific pieces cite their lesson.
- **Structure — Hook · Value · CTA.** About three seconds to earn the next three. The hook is a bold, specific
  line — **never "stop scrolling" / "don't scroll"** (it reads as desperate). Value is clean and structured
  (a list, a beat sequence, a story with a point). The CTA is one rung of the ladder (§8).
- **Length — 30–60 seconds** for an attraction Reel (Week 3 doc; `sf-talkinghead` default). Tighter is fine
  for a single take; longer belongs on YouTube.
- **Raw beats polished.** "What people want is the more raw, genuine, authentic type content"; the
  sensory-overload editing style of 2021–23 "doesn't work really much anymore" (`07-instagram/90`). Keep editing
  simple; captions are the only mandatory edit; vertical, phone-filmed, one person.
- **One video, every short-form platform.** Post the Instagram Reel to TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook
  Reels — "now you cover all short-form platforms with one video" (`07/90`). LinkedIn carries the carousel as a
  document post for team leaders and broker-owners (master plan).
- **Captions simple, clear, with a call to action** (`07/90`). **3–5 searchable hashtags, never a wall**
  (Mike's realtor short-form rule; the optimizer's `platform-rules.md` applies it). Hashtags lean to the
  agent audience (#realtorlife, #realestateagent, the brokerage-agnostic agent tags) — never the brokerage
  as a hashtag unless compliance says it must appear.
- **Collaborations** with partners and other creators expand the audience (`07/88`).

## 8. The escalating CTA ladder and the keyword (Week 3 doc; launching doc Day 3)
**Follow (save / share) → Comment → DM → Resource → Conversation → Call.** The workshop teaches it as "simple
CTA levels": follow/save/share → comment → DM → lead magnet/resource → conversation → partner call. Every Reel
ends on exactly **one** rung, named out loud and repeated in the caption.

| Rung | The ask, in the member's voice | Fits |
|---|---|---|
| Follow / save / share | "Follow for the next one" · "save this" · "send this to an agent who needs it" | Perspective, Personality, early Authority |
| Comment | "Comment **[KEYWORD]** and I'll send it to you" — the keyword rung | Authority, Proof |
| DM | "DM me the word **[KEYWORD]**" · "message me 'call'" | Story, Proof, Authority |
| Resource | the guide, checklist, template, prompt list, or free training tied to the value proposition (`07/87`) | Authority |
| Conversation | the reply the keyword opens — a real two-way DM | every rung above feeds it |
| Call | "book a call" · "apply to partner with me" · "get everything for free — partner with me" (`07/87`, `05/35b`) | after a conversation; a direct ask only on a rare, explicit opportunity post |

- **The keyword carries every Reel** (Week 3 doc: "the ManyChat keyword carrying every Reel"). One word, chosen
  once in `sf-setup`, saved in `identity/publishing.md`, said on camera and written in the caption. With
  ManyChat connected it triggers the member's sequence (the bonus asset: PARTNER · GUIDE · SCALE · GROWTH · story
  reply · DM qualifier · event · follow-up — Mike's templates, imported into the member's own account). Without
  ManyChat the keyword still works: the member replies by hand, and from Week 5 the Daily Follow-Up Queue
  surfaces the comments. **ManyChat is optional; the keyword is not.**
- **Never skip rungs.** A Reel never jumps from a first-time viewer to "book a call" — the call comes after a
  conversation (`02-prospect-targeting/19`: get them on a private call as quickly as possible, but say as little
  as you have to in text and never explain the model by DM).
- **"Get all of it for free — partner with me"** is Mike's CTA language (`05/35b`, `07/87`): the value
  proposition is what they get access to; the brokerage is never the hook.
- Comment-to-DM mechanics, the DM copy bank, and the hand-off to the Conversion plugin's `cv-dm-flow` belong to
  `sf-comment-to-dm`.

## 9. Mike's Instagram rules (`07-instagram/86–90`)

### 9a. The profile is the recruiting landing page (`07/87`)
Agents check the profile before they reach out; a poorly optimized one is a missed opportunity. Bio, link, and
highlights must spark curiosity and credibility.
- **The bio formula:** state who you are and how you help ("helping agents scale using social media and AI") ·
  who you help (the niche) · credibility (awards, track record, an authority point) · a call to action to free
  resources tied to the value proposition. "Short, clear, outcome driven."
- **The five questions every profile must answer** (cohort doc, Week 2 playbook): **who you are · who you help ·
  what you help them do · why they should listen · what to do next.** A bio that skips one fails.
- **The link:** one link, your recruiting funnel. Early on use a link-in-bio tool with multiple options so you can
  move people down the funnel: free resources (guides, prompts, checklists, a webinar — anything tied to the
  value proposition), book a call / apply to partner, "get everything for free — partner with me." Make the
  link text action-oriented and outcome-based ("get my free [thing]"), never "website."
- **Story highlights are the mini recruiting website:** About you (story, mission, vision) · Agent wins
  (recognition, success stories) · Culture (events, masterminds, behind the scenes) · Free value (guide snippets,
  tips) · Partnership (how agents can work with you) — plus the passions and hobbies people connect over.
- **The outcome:** profile views become conversations; "the more conversations you have, the more agents you'll
  attract."
- Bios for Facebook, TikTok, and LinkedIn follow the same five questions in each platform's length and tone;
  LinkedIn speaks to team leaders and broker-owners.

### 9b. Reels (`07/88`)
- Maximum reach: Reels are pushed to followers and non-followers; video builds trust faster than static;
  "consistency compounds — the more you post, the more authority you build." Reels are what builds the personal
  brand; put a lot of emphasis here.
- **Rotate the five pillars.** Mike's own rotation list in `/88`: value-based education · culture and recognition
  · personal (relatability) · leadership (authority and vision) — plus the "other Reels that attract agents":
  opinion/rants, myth-busting, future-focus, Q&A, collaborations. The five-pillar list above is that rotation
  in the cohort's vocabulary.
- **Cadence: three to five Reels a week, working up to five** (`/88`). Static recognition and personal posts
  spliced in are fine.
- Share the story, vision, and leadership lessons; the mistakes and what you learned; core values, mindset,
  discipline, growth — "the reason most agents fail" — with your perspective and your solutions.
- Culture and recognition on video, not just static: milestones, events, dinners, retreats, masterminds, team
  celebrations, video testimonials from agents, behind the scenes of community calls.

### 9c. Stories (`07/89`)
- "Far more powerful than most people realize." They disappear in 24 hours, so they feel more authentic;
  prospects watch stories daily even when they don't engage — the best way to stay top of mind.
- **Reels are width; stories are depth.** The relationship deepens in stories.
- **The four story categories** (the OS's rotation, from `/89`): **behind the scenes of leading** (daily
  routines, habits, leadership practices, screenshots of the weekly call, what you're learning and investing in)
  · **agent wins** (milestones, shout-outs, testimonials, events, "tag the agent so they reshare") · **personal**
  (family, hobbies, travel, a down day and how you're handling it, Maggie the golden retriever — "they know the
  name of your parents' dog") · **opportunity** (echo the value proposition: quick wins, a tip or script agents
  can use today, screenshots of tools and templates you're building, a sneak peek of what's coming).
- **High-impact story moves:** polls and quizzes (on their business, their goals, what's holding them back,
  favourite tools — "polls get a lot of engagement") · ask-me-anything (every question becomes a Reel) · sneak
  peeks of upcoming events and training ("don't show all your cards") · repost the YouTube thumbnail with the
  link · encourage agents to screenshot the mastermind call and tag you, then reshare.
- **The story rule: be consistent, not complicated.** "Do not go a day without posting stories" — one to five a
  day, a mix of personal and value; don't worry about production quality; include at least one interactive
  element when you can (a poll, a question box, "top or bottom?").
- Stories plus Reels: "Reels is the awareness, stories is the connection."

### 9d. The weekly routine (`07/90`)
- "Consistency is everything, and consistency is what most agents struggle with." Without structure it becomes
  inconsistent; a routine makes it simple and repeatable.
- **The simple week:** Monday brainstorm three to five Reel topics (a blend of value, recognition, culture,
  personal) · Tuesday record them (one to two hours max) · Wednesday edit (or outsource; AI caption tools) · then
  post or schedule. Mike's own rhythm: alternate Saturdays — plan short-form one week, record it the next.
- **Daily stories:** be present every day; grabbing a coffee with an insight, getting ready for the day, a tip, a
  shout-out to an agent, behind the scenes of planning an event or meeting agents, an end-of-day reflection.
- **Sample week (`/90`):** Monday value Reel · Tuesday recognition Reel · Wednesday leadership insight · Thursday
  culture / event recap · Friday personal or opinion ("rants work very well").
- **The Week 3 default mix (cohort doc, `sf-weekly-routine`): 2 attraction · 2 authority · 1 story a week, stories
  daily.** The OS reads it as: *attraction* = Proof + Personality (culture, recognition, the person); *authority*
  = Authority + Perspective (teaching, takes); *story* = Story. Reset to the sample week whenever the member's
  real cadence is lower — three Reels minimum (`/88`).
- **Your own ideas, over time.** Prompts and AI help at the start; "over time you want to put your own spin on
  it… 50% of my ideas come from AI, 50% will forever come from myself" — the member's conversations, coaching
  calls, and the questions agents ask are the best source (`/90`). The Brain's `memory/ideas.md` and
  `memory/objections.md` are that source in this OS.
- **Leaders create leaders:** teach the routine to your agents; their posting drives traffic back to the same
  group and "based on the tier structure, we all win" (`/90`).

### 9e. Culture content (`bonus/instagram-culture-highlights`)
Document the events and experiences you create for your group — broken out by topic (your speaking, the
culture, the community) — so agents see it rather than hear about it. "If you're not using video, you're not
doing everything." It started with one video in Mike's first year; the group was small. **Doctrine for skills:**
show the experience and the people, never the price tag (§3); every event the member runs or attends is a
Proof Reel plus a day of stories plus a highlight.

## 11. The cardinal rules and compliance in content (`03-model-positioning/13`; the compliance doctrine)
1. **Never talk badly about another brokerage.** Give credit, then "here's what I can do for you."
2. **Never talk badly about another person** — another sponsor, leader, or agent. Word gets around.
Plus, in every public piece: former brokerages are never named in a story ("a franchise", "an independent");
no compensation numbers (splits, caps, stock, rev-share tiers, income); no income promises or "what you'll
earn"; no "#1 / best / fastest-growing" without a dated source; agents quoted only with consent; AI-likeness
disclosed on clone content; the brokerage name and license displayed as `identity/compliance.md` says; anything
that becomes a paid ad to agents flagged (Meta Employment category). **The gate is three-state:** `unset` → no
public piece, say so plainly · `set` → apply and remind once · `confirmed` → apply. "If empty, proceed" is
banned. A skill asked to "call out" a brokerage or a person says so in one line and writes the strength instead.

## 12. Coaching answers (advise exactly like this when the member asks)
- **How often should I post?** Three to five Reels a week, working up to five (`07/88`); stories every single
  day, one to five (`07/89`). Consistency beats production quality (`07/90`).
- **Which platform?** Instagram Reels first — "the platform most people are on… put a lot of emphasis on it"
  (`07/88`); repost every Reel to TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Facebook Reels (`07/90`). LinkedIn for team leaders
  and broker-owners (carousel document posts). YouTube is the long-form engine (Week 4).
- **Should I talk about my brokerage?** Woven through the pillars, never the feed (launching doc). Speak to
  problems, not features (§6). The model is explained on a call, never in a Reel or a DM (`02/19`).
- **Should I show the money?** No. Compensation stays off content (§11). What you *show* is proof of
  transformation; what an agent *earns* is never stated.
- **My content pulls clients, not agents.** Your selling brand and your leader lane share a profile unless
  `brand-visual.md` says otherwise; shift the mix to the five pillars aimed at agents, add the keyword rung, and
  let stories show the leading. Consumer content isn't wrong — it's just not attraction.
- **I'm not an influencer.** "You don't need to be a huge influencer to attract agents. But you do need a
  compelling reason for someone to partner with YOU" (launching doc). Start with the value proposition and one
  Reel.
- **I have no agents and no proof yet.** Use the upline's proof, labeled as the upline's; your own journey
  (Story); what you're building (behind the scenes); and earn case studies by helping agents for free first
  (`04-value-proposition/33`, via `identity/proof.md`'s rule). Never manufacture a win.
- **What should I post today?** Stories — one from each category you've missed (`sf-stories`). For a Reel, the
  pillar you're lightest on this week per `memory/content-log.md`.
- **Editing?** Simple. Captions, a clean cut, done (`07/90`). The Riverside editor (`studio-reel`) handles it.
- **Best time to post?** Not covered in the Week 3 lessons. Use the connected posting tool's best-time data
  once it exists; otherwise post when the member is free to reply to comments for the first hour (the keyword
  rung needs the member present).
- **Where do ideas come from?** The questions agents ask you, your coaching calls, your own conversations
  (`07/90`) — `memory/ideas.md`, `memory/objections.md`, `memory/intel.md` first; the hook bank second.
