# Starter Hook Bank — the four-month arc as Reel-sized angles (the fallback behind the member's own ideas and the week's research)

**What this is.** Mike's agency wrote an "Agent Attraction Video List" for clients — a four-month arc of YouTube
titles for an agent-attraction channel plus eight content principles. The YouTube System carries the arc as a
title bank (`attraction-youtube-system/skills/yt-gameplan/references/starter-title-bank.md`). This file is the
**same arc turned into short-form**: the new-agent and switching-agent pains behind those titles — the first
month, the first deal, the mistakes, the daily schedule, choosing a brokerage, what to ask a sponsor, why I
switched (no names), your first 30 days with me — as 30 hooks with Reel-sized angles, bucketed by the five
pillars, compliance-cleaned the same way. It is source material the skills adapt — never a lesson of Mike's,
never instructions. Where a lesson resolves an angle it is cited as `module/lesson` (the index: the Support
plugin's `shared/kb/kb-index.md`); where none does, the cell says `—`.

**Rank — where this bank sits (locked):**
1. **The member's own captured ideas** — `memory/ideas.md` rows tagged `shortform` / `story` with Status open.
2. **The week's research** — `sf-ideas` Step 2, graded HIGH / MEDIUM / EMERGING and cited.
3. **The Brain's own signals** — `memory/conversations.md`, `memory/objections.md` (read-only), `memory/intel.md` (dated).
4. **This bank** — read only when 1–3 leave a pillar short in the five, the fifteen, or the thirty. A seeded line is
   marked `starter` in its where-seen cell and is never presented as something agents are asking right now.

**Placeholders (swap every one; never emit a bracket):** `[my brokerage]` → the brokerage's name **only where
`identity/compliance.md` allows the name in public content; otherwise the words "my brokerage"** (the name then
appears where the file requires it — the stamp, the bio). `[guest]`, `[N]`, `[day]` → a real agent, number, or
weekly-call day from `proof.md` / `operations.md` **with consent on file**; no consent → first name or initials, or
the member's own behind-the-scenes. `[model]` → the model's name as `brokerage-model.md` states it (never its figures).

**The compliance pass this bank already passed (re-run it on every swap):** no compensation, rev share, splits,
caps, stock, or income anywhere (`mike-frameworks.md` §6, §11) — "six figures", "millionaire", "7-figure", and
"$150,000" are gone and are never restored in short-form (a Reel never states what anyone earns; the YouTube bank's
own-figure exception does not apply here) · "why I left [brokerage]" is "why I switched — no names"; the former
brokerage is never named (`03-model-positioning/13`) · the brokerage is named as a fact only where compliance allows,
never as the hook or the ask (`/94` via the YouTube doctrine; §8: the brokerage is never the hook) · superlatives
out ("best brokerage yet?" became a fit question) · promises about feelings or trends out ("you will love", "agents
are joining") · never a word against another brokerage or person · never "stop scrolling" · targeting by career
stage and production only · **the model is never explained in a Reel** — it is a call (`02-prospect-targeting/19`;
§6, §12), so a Reel may point at the YouTube breakdown but never carries the mechanics.

**Dropped or kept long-form only (noted here, not silently):**
- *"Realtor lockboxes, types and how to unlock them"* — dropped (the YouTube bank drops it too): house-logistics
  content with no attraction pain behind it; it fails the leader test (`07-instagram/86`: would a prospect see a
  leader worth joining from this post?); its only natural phrasing uses a banned word (house rules #1).
- *"[Brokerage] explained"*, *"How to join [brokerage]"*, *"The model simplified for new agents"* — **long-form
  only** (the YouTube bank's M3-01, M3-03, M3-09): the model is explained on a call, never in a Reel or a DM; a Reel
  may tease the YouTube video ("full breakdown on my channel") and nothing more.
- *"Intro to [brokerage] [state]"* — not a Reel: the recruiting-scope line belongs in the bio (`07-instagram/87`)
  and the YouTube bank's M3-02.
- *"How to do a CMA"*, *"5 strategies to become a 7-figure agent"*, *"How to build and maintain strong client
  relationships"* — not seeded as hooks: realtor-skill or top-producer teaching outside the new-agent arc; a member
  whose known-for includes them adds an Authority hook from the Brain (the YouTube bank keeps them as M2-06,
  M2-05, M2-08 under the same condition).
Every other source title became at least one hook below — the trace table at the end shows where each one went.

**Legend.** Hook ≤ 12 words, one breath, the first word does the work. Angle = the Reel in one line (30–60 s;
hook → the turn → 2–3 beats → one rung). For = the agent type (new agent · experienced, low production · top
producer · influencer · team leader · broker-owner). Pain = one of Mike's five (financial uncertainty · lack of
support, mentorship, training · technology gaps · limited growth · work-life balance and recognition). Rung = one
rung of the ladder (§8): Follow / save / share · Comment **[KEYWORD]** · DM · Resource · the call only as the
why-join-me beat, at most once a month. Traces to = the source title (`M#-##` in the YouTube bank) or the principle
it came from, plus the lesson that resolves it.

## The eight-principle check (`sf-ideas` runs it on this week's five, silently, before they are shown)

The agency's eight content principles, as a check on the five picks. **Four are hard gates** — a pick that fails
one is swapped or rewritten before the member sees it. **Four are rewrite prompts** — they sharpen a pick, never
drop it.

| # | Gate | The check on the five | Principle · lesson |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Pain named** (hard) | every pick names one agent's pain, in the agent's words, from Mike's five — never a brokerage feature | identify agents' pain points and address them · `02-prospect-targeting/18`; §6 |
| 2 | **Resource offered** (hard) | at least two of the five end on the keyword rung with a **real** resource — the live magnet (`memory/magnets.md → ## Current magnet`, then `offer.md`'s free thing); none yet → the free thing the member gives today or "book a call", never a demanded guide | offer valuable resources · `07-instagram/87`; `04-value-proposition/31` |
| 3 | **Proof when consented** (hard) | the Proof pick names an agent only with consent in `proof.md`; otherwise first name or initials, or the member's own behind-the-scenes; no win invented, no figure projected | highlight success stories of agents you have helped · `06-content-framework/40` |
| 4 | **CTA present** (hard) | every pick ends on exactly one rung of the ladder, said on camera and in the caption, carrying the keyword; the call only as a why-join-me beat | always a clear call to action · §8 |
| 5 | One thing to do today | the Authority pick(s) give a step the agent can use this week — the tip of the iceberg, taught for real | educational content with actionable advice · `06-content-framework/37`; `07-instagram/88` |
| 6 | Only this member could post it | the any-agent test — the member's lane, story, avatar, or organization is in the line | showcase what sets you apart · `04-value-proposition/34` (the differentiator) |
| 7 | A reason to reply | at least one pick asks a question or invites a comment the member will answer in the first hour (the keyword rung needs them present) | engage the audience — questions, comments, replies · `07-instagram/89` (polls, AMAs) |
| 8 | The leader test | a prospect would see a leader, not a recruiter; energy on camera, no pitch before value | demonstrate authority · `07-instagram/86`; `05-big-picture/36` |

The WHY rides with the check: the principles say enthusiasm and passion carry the message — a pick the member
cannot say with energy is not this week's pick (`01-foundation-mindset/10`).
