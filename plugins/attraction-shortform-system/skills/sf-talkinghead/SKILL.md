# Attraction Reels — Talking Head

The member, talking to camera, to one agent — teaching, taking a stand, telling their story, showing proof, or
just being themselves. This is the batchable format: hand them topics, script the ones they choose, lay out the
month, and they film several in one sitting.

**Apply house rules** (`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/house-rules.md`) — plain, warm, never technical; the member,
never "the agent". **The doctrine** is `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/mike-frameworks.md` — lazy-load §5–§9 at
the phase that needs them.

**Week 4 rules that bind every Reel** (the rewire brief, 2026-11-14 — where older text disagrees, these win):
- **Two keywords, never more.** `GUIDE` delivers the guide (`memory/magnets.md → ## Current magnet`); the offer's
  `Comment keyword:` (`identity/offer.md`) is the invitation — the one-pager and the call link. Both sit on
  `publishing.md`'s `Keyword:` line (`GUIDE · [the offer's word]`). No other word in a script, a caption, a hook or a
  CTA line, ever.
- **The call ask follows the law:** the person · their help · free · to run the method — never the brokerage, never
  compensation. Join register (`offer.md → Join-gated package allowed: yes` or `unknown`): *"If you want my help running
  this — the lessons, the templates, the community — partner with me and it's free. Grab [the call]."* Open register
  (`no`): *"I'll walk you through it, free, no strings."* The spoken line, the pop-up, the end card and the caption line
  are the kit's CALLS TO ACTION table (chapter 7 of the newest `exports/week3-kit-*.txt`), verbatim.
- **Scalable by design.** Nothing in a script promises the member's time per partner — no in-person days beside the
  member, no chat group, no one-to-one calls, no "call me any time". What partners get: recorded lessons, templates,
  the community, and the weekly group call only when `identity/operations.md` shows the member already runs one. The
  member's OWN batch-filming day is their routine and is fine to show.
- **The whole method, not one tactic.** An Authority Reel teaches one step of `offer.md → ## The framework` or one page
  of the guide in full; the guide is the resource every ask points at.
- **Member-facing words:** Week 4, plain words, never a skill id or a file path in a sentence to the member; the six kit
  posts are already designed (Week 3) — "post, don't make"; a missing input gets one warm line with the phrase that
  creates it ("launch my lead magnet plugin" · "build my partner offer").

Three modes (pick from what they said; never ask which):
- **"What should I film?"** → the calendar first (the front door rule below): this week's rows from the Game Plan
  are the list; `sf-ideas` when the week has no rows or they want fresh research.
- **"Script these" / a named topic / "my first attraction reel"** → Phase 2 (setup hands over the first talking-head row after the six kit posts as the topic, **together with the opening the member chose at setup's last stop — that opening leads THE HOOK, marked "your pick", and the script is written around it**; one Reel, no further stop; the kit posts are never scripted here).
- **"My 30-day calendar" / "plan my month"** → Phase 3: the calendar lives in the Short-Form Game Plan — hand to
  `sf-gameplan` (UPDATE mode, section 6), then state the phrase that scripts the first three ("script my attraction reels").

## Front door rule
"What should I film this week?" is answered by the member's own plan first: `memory/content-calendar.md`'s rows dated this week (the Game Plan dated them; the Monday Week Plan may already have drafted them — a row at `Scripted` has its script on the board and in the Content folder). When the week has rows, Phase 1 lists them (plus the member's own `ideas.md` rows) and offers to script the ones still at `Idea` — a row that is one of the six kit posts (the Game Plan's first rows) is a designed graphic, not a Reel: *"that one's already made — it's in your Offer folder; post it"*, never scripted here. When the week has no rows, say one line — "Let's find this week's Reels first" — and hand to `sf-ideas` (research + the week's picks at the member's cadence, three by default + the hook bank), or "plan my next 30 days of reels" to map the month.

## Phase 1 — The topic list (by pillar, balanced from the log)
**Read `references/topic-guide.md`.** **Open now:** `memory/ideas.md` (tags `shortform`, `story` — the member's own
ideas go to the TOP; mark `used` once scripted) · `memory/content-log.md` (what's covered; which pillar is light this
week/month) · `memory/objections.md` (an objection heard this month is a Perspective Reel waiting to happen) ·
`memory/content-performance.md` (what worked — the performance ledger from `sf-analytics`; lean on it for hooks, pillars,
and rungs; skip if it doesn't exist yet) · `identity/publishing.md` (the keyword, what it opens, the weekly mix).
Produce ~8–10 topics the member can choose from: this week's calendar rows still at `Idea` first (the plan they
already said yes to), then the member's own ideas (from `ideas.md`), then the pillar they're lightest on this
month, then the rest — never more than three from one pillar. Each topic: a working **hook** (one line) · the **pillar** · **who it's for** (the avatar, one
line) · the **story or proof it can carry** (a story-bank hook, a win with consent, or "none — straight teach")
· the **rung** it ends on · one line of **why this is strong for you**. Speak to an agent's *problem*, never a
brokerage feature. Present simply:
> "Here are strong ones for this week — I've leaned on [pillar] because you've been light there. I'm scripting [#, #
> and #] — name other numbers any time."
A statement, then the scripts — never a Yes line, never an open menu, never a question first (the owner,
2026-11-14: told, not asked; 2026-11-16: "we don't really need more questions"). **If they're unsure or say "you pick"**
(house rules #8 + `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/advisor-playbook.md`): the top 3 are already named — script them now.

## Phase 2 — Scripts (for the chosen topics: the week's Reels at the member's cadence — three by default — or the one they named)
**Read `references/script-guide.md`.** **Open now — the writing step:** `identity/voice-print.md` (their SPOKEN voice:
scripts are read aloud, so write for the ear in their cadence and signature phrases; empty → `voice.md` alone; never
fabricate) · `identity/voice.md` + `identity/voice-samples.md` (tone + their real phrasing) · `identity/journey.md`
(incl. `## Why join me` if written) + `identity/strategy.md` (the story's turning points, the known-for) · `identity/positioning.md`
(the one line "why I'm here"; what stays for the private call — never in a Reel) · `identity/proof.md` (wins with
consent for Proof Reels; first name / initials otherwise) · `identity/offer.md` and `memory/magnets.md` if Step 1 left them closed (the Resource rung is the
guide — GUIDE; an older `offer.md` at `Status: seeds` with no live guide → the ask is the call line in the law's shape,
never a demanded guide) · `identity/publishing.md` (the two keywords, what each opens) if Phase 1 didn't open it.
**NO QUESTION BEFORE A SCRIPT (the owner, 2026-11-16: "we don't really need more questions because they've already
gone through a lot of this when they were building their AI brain").** The scripts are written from the member's
Brain — their voice, their story, their method, and `## In your words` — with nothing asked first. The member's own
wording is always welcome and never required: the batch closes with ONE line, a statement, never an offer and
never a question — *"Tell me how you'd open any of these and I'll rewrite it around your words."* When they do,
their sentence leads THE HOOK as option 1 marked *your words* (tidied only to be sayable in one breath — never a
claim added; a named brokerage or person and any income figure left out, said once), it is appended to
`identity/content-pillars.md → ## In your words` (`- **[working title]** (YYYY-MM-DD): "[verbatim]"`, pushed with
Phase 6) and the row's `Carries` cell gains `your words YYYY-MM-DD`.

For EACH Reel, use these exact section names:
- **THE BRIEF** — pillar · for whom · story used (hook from the bank, or none) · rung + keyword · *built on your
  words* when it is.
- **THE HOOK — 3 ways** — three options (**option 1 is the member's own sentence, marked *your words*, whenever the
  row carries `your words`, the member gave their own wording for this Reel, or setup handed over an opening they
  WROTE; an
  opening they only picked from three is listed first and marked *your pick* — never *your words***; one
  may come from `content-pillars.md → ## Hooks bank` when a bank hook fits this Reel), each sayable in one breath (~8–12 words), styles labelled (question ·
  contrarian · number/stakes · mistake · callout · curiosity-gap — never three of the same), the strongest marked
  with one line why. The first word is the hook. Never "stop scrolling", never "hey guys", never credentials first.
- **THE SCRIPT** — word-for-word, written for speech, **30–60 seconds (~80–150 words, show the count)**. Shape:
  hook → the turn → 2–3 steps of real substance (the story's point, the teach, the take, the proof; an Authority
  Reel teaches ONE step of `## The framework` or one page of the guide in full and names the guide as where the rest
  lives) → the CTA line with one of the two keywords, said plainly. Every line sayable in one breath; in their spoken cadence.
- **THE BULLET VERSION** — the same Reel as 5 riff lines (hook and CTA word-for-word). Most members film from
  this — give it equal weight.
- **THE SHOTS** — one line per step: where to stand, what to show, the one b-roll to grab (a screenshot of the
  call for Proof, the kitchen table for Story — simple, phone-filmed).
- **THE CTA** — one ask per Reel (a keyword ask uses one of the two keywords), the words from the kit's CALLS TO ACTION table (the
  spoken line): the guide — *"[what the guide holds, one clause]. Comment **GUIDE** and I'll send it."*; the invitation
  — *"If you want my help running this, free, [the booking line]."* (the law's shape; open register: *"I'll walk you
  through it, free, no strings."*). GUIDE on most Reels; the invitation on about one post in four — most naturally a
  Proof Reel or a "what you get" Reel (a Proof Reel may carry the guide instead); a Personality Reel asks for a follow
  only; never two asks on one Reel (house rules #13). The brokerage is never the reason; never the model by DM. The keyword sheet (`sf-comment-to-dm`)
  carries the same two words — never a third.
- **THE POP-UP + END CARD** — from the same table and the member's pop-up set (designed in Week 3, in their Offer
  folder): the name card in the first three seconds · one mid-video pop-up, six words or fewer, two lines at most, three
  seconds (`Comment GUIDE · free guide` or `My help, free · [the call in three words]`) · the end card in the last five
  seconds (the guide's title · Comment GUIDE · the stamp — or the call's headline · the booking line · the stamp).
  Never over the face, never in the lower quarter where captions sit.
- **THE CAPTIONS** — hand hook + script + format `talking head` + pillar + rung + keyword to the optimizer's
  rules (`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/sf-optimizer/references/platform-rules.md`): Instagram + Facebook caption
  with the CTA line and 3–5 hashtags · TikTok one-line · YouTube Shorts title/description/tags · cover text + 2–3
  on-screen cues. Do not write your own hashtag rules here.
After all Reels: **THE BATCH NOTE** — which to film in one session (same outfit, same spot), and the one to post
first. A **Story** Reel pulls its story from the bank, told in their words, former brokerage never named; a
**Proof** Reel names an agent only with consent on file.
