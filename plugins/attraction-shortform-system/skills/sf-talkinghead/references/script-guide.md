# Script Guide — Attraction Reel scripts (30–60 seconds, read aloud)

For each chosen topic, deliver a package the member can film immediately. Everything in their spoken voice
(`voice-print.md` first, then `voice.md` + `voice-samples.md`), speaking to one agent ("you"). Doctrine:
`shared/mike-frameworks.md` §7–§8.

## The structure — Hook · Value · CTA (every Reel)
1. **Hook (first ~3 seconds).** One bold, specific line that names the agent's problem or the surprise. State the
   stakes or the payoff; no wind-up. **Never "stop scrolling"**, never "hey guys / welcome back / in today's
   video", never credentials first. The first word is the hook.
2. **The turn (one line).** "Here's what nobody told me." / "That changed when…" / "Most agents get this
   backwards."
3. **Value (2–3 beats).** By pillar: the teach (a step they can use today) · the take (the belief and the why) ·
   the story (the wall → the turning point → the point) · the proof (what happened, in order, with the agent's
   permission) · the personal (the moment and what it says about how they lead). Specific and theirs.
4. **CTA (one rung, with the keyword).** Said plainly, in their words: *"Comment **PARTNER** and I'll send it."* ·
   *"Save this for the next time you feel stuck."* · *"DM me 'call' if you want to talk it through."* One rung per
   Reel. The model, the money, and the brokerage pitch never appear — that is the private call.

## Length — 30–60 seconds (the attraction Reel)
- **30–45 seconds (~80–110 words)** — one teach, one take, one personal moment.
- **45–60 seconds (~110–150 words)** — a story with a point, a proof with a before/after.
Show the word count. Offer a 15-second cut only when they ask for a punchy single line.

## The hook, three ways (always)
Three options, each one breath (~8–12 words), styles labelled, the strongest marked with one line why. Mix the
styles — **question · contrarian · number/stakes · mistake · callout · curiosity-gap** — never three of the same.
Examples of the register (Mike's workshop lines, adapted, never copied verbatim into a member's Reel):
"You can have your best year ever — and January 1st it resets." · "The reason agents aren't coming to you isn't
your brokerage." · "If two weeks off slows your income, you don't have freedom yet."

## Two delivery formats (always give both)
- **Teleprompter version** — word-for-word, the way they actually talk (contractions, short sentences, their
  signature phrases from `voice-print.md`). Mark it clearly as read-aloud.
- **Bullet version** — the same Reel as 5 riff beats (hook and CTA kept word-for-word). Most members film from
  this — it sounds more like them.

## The shots (one line per beat)
Where to stand, what to show, the one b-roll to grab — a screenshot of the weekly call for Proof, the
whiteboard for Authority, the kitchen table or the gym for Personality. Phone, vertical, captions. Nothing fancy
(`07-instagram/90`).

## Stories and proof (never invented)
- A Story Reel pulls a real story from `identity/story-bank.md` — tell it in their words with the true details
  that make it land; the point is required; the former brokerage is "a franchise" / "an independent"; stamp
  `Used-where` after delivery so stories rotate.
- A Proof Reel names an agent only when `proof.md` says OK to use publicly; otherwise first name or initials, or
  "one of my agents". Numbers are the member's stated numbers, nothing projected. "No story too big or too
  small" (`06-content-framework/40`) — one deal in 30 days is the relatable one.
- No story on file for this beat → write the Reel without one and mark `[your story here — say 'build my
  story bank' to add it]`.

## Rules that keep it good
- **The read-aloud test governs:** if a line can't be said in one breath while walking, rewrite it.
- **The delete test:** cut any word not earning its place. **The any-agent test:** if another leader could say
  it, make it theirs. **The so-what test:** the first line answers "why should this agent care?"
- **One idea per Reel.** **No hype words** ("insane", "you won't believe"), no fake urgency.
- **The cardinal rules** (`03-model-positioning/13`): never a negative word about another brokerage or person.
  Compensation never in a Reel. No earnings claims. Nothing that becomes a pitch.
- Captions, hashtags, cover text, and on-screen cues come from the optimizer's `platform-rules.md` — don't write
  them here; give it a strong hook and clear beats to pull from.
