# Calendar Guide — the 30-day attraction calendar (lives in sf-talkinghead)

Turn the pillars into a month of filming — **20 Reels, five a week for four weeks**, in the member's weekly mix,
with "stories daily" as a standing line. Vertical, 30–60s, phone-filmed. The routine that runs it week to week is
`sf-weekly-routine`; the film-day logistics are `sf-batch-publish`. Doctrine: `shared/mike-frameworks.md` §9b–§9d.

## Inputs (all from the Brain — never a questionnaire)
`identity/content-pillars.md` (the five pillars and their seeds) · `identity/publishing.md` (cadence, weekly mix,
batch day, keyword) · `memory/content-log.md` (what's been posted; what's light) · `memory/ideas.md` (the
member's ideas, first) · `memory/objections.md` (this month's objections → Perspective Reels) · `story-bank.md`
(stories with an empty Used-where) · `proof.md` (wins with consent) · `memory/intel.md` (a news item that
deserves a take → hand to `sf-greenscreen` rather than scripting it here).

## The weekly mix
Default **2 attraction · 2 authority · 1 story** (the Week 3 cohort standard):
- *attraction* = **Proof** (an agent's win, the weekly call, an event, new training) + **Personality** (the
  person behind the business)
- *authority* = **Authority** (teach) + **Perspective** (a take, a myth, a question answered)
- *story* = **Story** (one journey beat with a point)
The member's own mix in `publishing.md` overrides the default. Mike's sample week (`07-instagram/90`): Monday
value · Tuesday recognition · Wednesday leadership insight · Thursday culture / event recap · Friday personal or
opinion. Three Reels a week is the floor; five is the goal (`07-instagram/88`). If their cadence is three, the
month is 12 Reels: 1 attraction · 1 authority · 1 story each week — say so plainly.

## Output (use these exact section names)
- **THE MONTH AT A GLANCE** — one paragraph: what this month establishes and why this order.
- **WEEK 1 → WEEK 4** — five Reels each, in a table: # · day · hook · pillar · for whom · what it carries (story
  hook / win / teach) · rung + keyword. Rules:
  - **Week 1 establishes who they are:** one Story Reel (the hardest stretch or the turning point), one Authority
    on the known-for, one Proof, one Personality, one Perspective. An agent who finds them in Week 1 should be
    able to answer the five profile questions from the feed alone.
  - The member's own ideas and this month's objections land in Weeks 1–2, not Week 4.
  - No story is used twice in the month; no pillar has more than two Reels in a week.
  - Every Reel ends on a rung; the keyword appears on at least three Reels a week; the direct call-rung ("book a
    call" as the Reel's CTA) at most once in the month, and only as a why-join-me beat — an OS rule, not a Week 3
    lesson.
  - Brokerage and opportunity content is woven through (a Proof Reel about the weekly call, a Perspective Reel on
    "the brokerage isn't the reason") — never a week of it.
- **STORIES, DAILY** — one line: the four-category rotation runs every day regardless (`sf-stories`).
- **THE BATCH PLAN** — Mike's rhythm as the default: Monday plan the week's topics, Tuesday record (1–2 hours),
  Wednesday edit, then post or schedule (`07-instagram/90`); or his alternating-Saturdays plan-then-record. Name
  the sessions, what films together (same outfit, same spot), and the hours the month really takes.
- **THE THREE TO FILM FIRST** — one line each on why.

## Rules
- Exactly 20 (or 12 at a three-a-week cadence), numbered, five (or three) per week — count before delivering.
- Hooks the way an agent would say the problem, not the way a leader describes the solution.
- Nothing invented: no stat without a source, no story not in the bank, no win without consent.
- The cardinal rules and compliance three-state (read from the first line of `identity/compliance.md`, `Status:`)
  apply to every line, even in a plan.
- Save as `[YYYY-MM] · 30-Day Calendar` in `03 · Content/Short-Form/[YYYY-MM · Month]/`; log each entry as
  `Idea` in the locked row shape; offer the content board; close with *"pick your first five and say 'script
  these'."*
