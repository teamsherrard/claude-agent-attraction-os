# Calendar Guide — the 30-day attraction calendar (the rules; the calendar itself lives in the Short-Form Game Plan)

Turn the pillars into a month of filming — **12 posts at the default three a week (20 at five), four weeks**, in the member's weekly mix,
with "stories daily" as a standing line. Vertical, 30–60s, phone-filmed. **Owner: `sf-gameplan`** (section 6 of the
Game Plan writes it to `memory/content-calendar.md`; "plan my next 30 days of reels" appends the next window);
`sf-talkinghead` reads this file only to hand a calendar ask to the Game Plan and to script against a row. The
routine that runs it week to week is `sf-weekly-routine`; the Monday drafts are `sf-week-plan`; the film-day
logistics are `sf-batch-publish`. Doctrine: `shared/mike-frameworks.md` §9b–§9d.

## Inputs (all from the Brain — never a questionnaire)
`identity/content-pillars.md` (the five pillars and their seeds) · `identity/publishing.md` (cadence, weekly mix,
batch day, the two keywords — `GUIDE · [the offer's word]`) · `identity/offer.md` (`## The framework` — the method's
steps, one Authority Reel each; `## Content branches` — the angles; the `Comment keyword:`; the register) ·
`memory/magnets.md → ## Current magnet` (the guide every ask points at) · the newest `exports/week3-kit-*.txt`
(chapter 7: the six post outlines — the first rows of a new window while still unposted, designed in Week 3, "post,
don't make" — and the CALLS TO ACTION table every CTA cell quotes) · `memory/content-log.md` (what's been posted; what's light) · `memory/ideas.md` (the
member's ideas, first) · `memory/objections.md` (this month's objections → Perspective Reels) · `story-bank.md`
(stories with an empty Used-where) · `proof.md` (wins with consent) · `memory/intel.md` (a news item that
deserves a take → hand to `sf-greenscreen` rather than scripting it here).

## The weekly mix
Default **one attraction · one authority · one story at three a week (2 · 2 · 1 at five)** (the cohort's Week 4 default — not a lesson of Mike's; his sample
week is below):
- *attraction* = **Proof** (an agent's win with consent, a recorded lesson partners got, the community, an event, the
  weekly group call only when the member already runs one — `operations.md`) + **Personality** (the person behind the
  business)
- *authority* = **Authority** (teach) + **Perspective** (a take, a myth, a question answered)
- *story* = **Story** (one journey moment with a point)
The member's own mix in `publishing.md` overrides the default. Mike's sample week (`07-instagram/90`): Monday
value · Tuesday recognition · Wednesday leadership insight · Thursday culture / event recap · Friday personal or
opinion. Three a week is where every member starts; five is the step-up the Monthly Calendar Refill or the Monthly
Deep Dive proposes after a month at three has held (`07-instagram/88`). At three the month is twelve posts — in the
first window the six kit posts and six Reels — 1 attraction · 1 authority · 1 story each week; say so plainly.

## Output (use these exact section names)
- **THE MONTH AT A GLANCE** — one paragraph: what this month establishes and why this order.
- **THE SIX KIT POSTS, THEN THE REELS** — the kit rows first, then the Reel rows at the cadence (six at the default three a week, fourteen at five), in one table: # · **date** (real, `YYYY-MM-DD`) · day · format (talking
  head · green screen · carousel) · pillar · working title · hook · for whom · what it carries (story hook / win /
  teach / finding #) · rung + keyword · System ID (`sf-YYYY-MM-DD-NN`) — that is the row as the Brain keeps it
  (`memory/content-calendar.md`, the locked eleven columns); in the document the member reads, the table is five
  columns: Date (written "Tue, Nov 17", never 2026-11-17) · What (the format and the working title) · The hook · The
  ask (in words: "Comment GUIDE" · "Comment [WORD]" · "Follow") · Status. Rules:
  - **Dates make sense:** a Reel's publishing date is never earlier than the day after the batch day that films it;
    a week made only of designed posts carries the one line "This week you only post — nothing to film."
  - **The six kit posts come first** when a window opens and they are still unposted: the Game Plan's first rows, one
    every two or three days, Format `Graphic` or `Carousel`, Status `Ready to Post` (never `Ready to Film` — nothing
    was recorded; the owner, 2026-11-14) — they are designed already (Week 3, the member's Offer
    folder): "post, don't make"; posting flips them to `Published`. Then the Reels.
  - **The first Reels establish who they are:** the kit posts already carry Story and Proof, so the first Reels are Personality, Perspective and the Authority teach (a Story Reel — the hardest stretch or the turning point — when the cadence allows). An agent who finds them this month should be able to answer the five profile questions from the feed alone.
  - The member's own ideas and this month's objections land in the first two weeks of Reels, not the last.
  - No story is used twice in the month; no pillar has more than two Reels in a week.
  - Every post ends on one ask, by the call-ask rule (house rules #13): GUIDE is the ask on most posts; the invitation
    — the offer's own word, or the kit's call line in the law's shape (the person · their help · free · to run this) —
    on about one post in four, most naturally a Proof piece or a "what you get" piece (a Proof piece may carry the
    guide instead); a Personality Reel asks for a follow only; never two asks on one post; the six designed kit posts
    keep exactly the asks the kit printed; the CTA cell quotes the table's line, never a third word.
  - Teaching Reels walk the method: across a month the Authority rows cover the framework's steps in order (one step
    per Reel, the guide named as where the rest lives) — the whole method, not one tactic.
  - Scalable: no row promises the member's time per partner (no in-person days beside the member, no chat group, no
    one-to-one calls); what partners get is recorded lessons, templates, the community, the weekly group call only when
    the member runs one. The member's own batch day is their routine and is fine.
  - Brokerage and opportunity content is woven through (a Proof Reel about a recorded lesson partners got, or the
    weekly group call when the member runs one; a Perspective Reel on "the brokerage isn't the reason") — never a week
    of it.
- **STORIES, DAILY** — one line: the four-category rotation runs every day regardless (`sf-stories`).
- **THE BATCH PLAN** — Mike's rhythm as the default: Monday plan the week's topics, Tuesday record (1–2 hours),
  Wednesday edit, then post or schedule (`07-instagram/90`); or his alternating-Saturdays plan-then-record. Name
  the sessions, what films together (same outfit, same spot), and the hours the month really takes.
- **THE THREE TO FILM FIRST** — one line each on why.

## Rules
- Exactly twelve at the default three a week (twenty only when `publishing.md → Cadence:` says five), numbered, three
  (or five) per week, the six kit posts counted among the first window's twelve — count before delivering.
- Hooks the way an agent would say the problem, not the way a leader describes the solution.
- Nothing invented: no stat without a source, no story not in the bank, no win without consent.
- The cardinal rules and compliance three-state (read from the first line of `identity/compliance.md`, `Status:`)
  apply to every line, even in a plan.
- The calendar is section 6 of `[Name]'s Short-Form Game Plan — YYYY-MM-DD` (saved to `03 · Content/Short-Form/`)
  and is written to `memory/content-calendar.md` in the Brain's locked row shape; a calendar-only refresh may also
  save `[YYYY-MM] · 30-Day Calendar` in the month folder. Calendar rows are NOT logged to `content-log.md` — a row
  enters the log when its piece is scripted. The board (`sf-board`, mapping mode) seeds the rows as dated cards;
  close with *"pick your first three and say 'script these'."*
