# Hook Formulas — reaction Reels for agent attraction

The hook is the only line read word-for-word. ≤12 words, one breath, the first word does the work. **Never "stop
scrolling."** Never a negative word about a brokerage or a person. Mike's workshop lines (the launching doc's
green-screen openers) show the register — adapt the shape, never paste his line into a member's Reel.

### 1. The Fact Lead
Open with the fact. The fact does the work.
Template: "[Brokerage / the industry] just [changed X]. Here's what it means for you."
Example: "The commission rules just changed again. Here's what it means if you're two years in."

### 2. The Reset
Mike's signature attraction frame: everything resets.
Template: "You can have your best year ever — and on January 1st, [reset]."
Example: "You can close 40 homes this year and still start at zero in January."

### 3. The Question Nobody Asks
Template: "Nobody asks agents this: [the question]."
Example: "Nobody asks agents this: what keeps paying you when you stop selling?"

### 4. The Honest Take
Signals a real opinion, not a pitch.
Template: "My honest take on [what just happened]."
Example: "My honest take on the fee change everyone's messaging me about."

### 5. The Myth
Template: "Most agents believe [X]. This week proved otherwise."
Example: "Most agents think the brokerage is the reason agents leave. It isn't."

### 6. The Direct Address
Template: "If you're [avatar's situation], you need to see this."
Example: "If you're paying for leads that don't convert, watch this before you renew."

### 7. The Contrarian (generous)
Challenge the assumption, credit everyone.
Template: "Everyone's reading this as [bad/good] news. I see something else."
Example: "Everyone's calling this bad news for agents. I think it's the opposite."

### 8. The Window
Template: "There's a window open for agents right now. Here's what it is."
Example: "There's a window open for new agents this year that wasn't there last year."

### 9. The "What We Did"
The Proof beat up front.
Template: "Here's what our group did the day this came out."
Example: "Here's what we changed on our Tuesday call the week the rules changed."

### 10. The Specific Number
Template: "[Number] — that's [what it is]. Here's why it matters to you."
Example: "71% — that's the share of agents who closed zero deals last year. Here's the way out."
(Only a number with a source the item provides; Mike's 71% figure is from `06-content-framework/40` — cite it
to him if the member uses it.)

## Hook selection
| Item type | Best formulas |
|---|---|
| A brokerage's own announcement (the member's vehicle) | Fact Lead · What We Did · Direct Address |
| An industry ruling or policy change | Honest Take · Fact Lead · Window |
| A model question agents are asking | Myth · Question Nobody Asks · Contrarian |
| Tech / AI news | Window · Direct Address · Contrarian |
| A rumour or a "who's leaving where" story | **not an item** — see search-guide §4 |

## Hook quality test
≤12 words, one breath? · speaks to the avatar's situation? · would an agent mid-scroll stop? · a different
formula from the last few days (`content-log.md`)? · no hype words, no "stop scrolling"? · no brokerage or person
characterized negatively? All yes, or rewrite.
