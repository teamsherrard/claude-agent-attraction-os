# Talking Points Guide — the green-screen package (hook + riff bullets, read back before it leaves)

The deliverable is a **hook + talking points**, not a script. A member riffing from bullets sounds like the leader
reacting in real time; a member reading a script looks stiff. The hook is word-for-word; everything after it is a
prompt. Doctrine: `shared/mike-frameworks.md` §5 (Perspective), §6, §8, §11.

## The beat order (4–6 bullets)
1. **What happened** — the fact, one beat, as published ("fees change on the 1st — here's the line").
2. **What it means for you** — the avatar's situation named ("if you're 2–5 years in and paying for leads…").
   This is where the value lives.
3. **The take** — the member's belief, with conviction and generosity: credit everyone involved ("that
   brokerage does a lot right"), then the member's perspective on what matters ("but the brokerage was never the
   reason agents stay or go — the problem is").
4. **What we do about it in my world** — the Proof beat: the routine, the call, the training, the thing partners
   get (the iceberg tip, `07-instagram/88`). Never the mechanics, never the money.
5. **CTA** — the rung + keyword, in their words: *"Comment **PARTNER** and I'll send you what we use."* /
   *"Save this for the agent who's panicking about it."*
(6. optional — a forward look: "here's what I'm telling my agents this month.")

## The model-comparison Reel (concepts, not numbers)
When the item or the member calls for "cloud vs franchise", "flat fee vs split", "is [model] worth it":
1. **The honest pro of the other model** — first, genuinely ("a franchise gives you a name people know on day one").
2. **What each model is built for** — concepts: who thrives there, what it optimizes for.
3. **What the member's model is built for** — from `brokerage-model.md` / `positioning.md`, plain English.
4. **The problem it solves for the avatar** — one of the five pains, in their words.
5. **"The mechanics are a conversation, not a Reel"** — the CTA: DM / book a call.
**Never:** splits, caps, stock, fees, tiers, income, "what you'd make"; a named brokerage characterized ("they
nickel-and-dime you"); a claim about another brokerage's leadership, payouts, or people (`03-model-positioning/14`,
via the Brain doctrine). If the only comparison available is a dig, it's not a Reel.

## On-screen text notes — 2–3 overlays
≤6 words each: the fact, the avatar line, the keyword. These are the "background spec" if the member wants a
graphic from the Design Studio (we describe; we don't render).

## Rules that make it work
- **A real take.** "Here's what I'm telling my agents" / "most people read this as bad news; I don't." A neutral
  recap is not content.
- **Generous.** The take is about the *agent's problem*, never the other company's failure. The cardinal rules
  are the brand (`05-big-picture/36`: "I've never written a negative comment on anybody's content, ever").
- **"You"-focused.** One agent, their stage, their situation.
- **No news-anchor language.** Never "according to this article," "experts say," "a report found."
- **Shorthand, not prose.** If a bullet reads like a sentence to recite, cut it. Could they glance and keep talking?
- **One fact minimum**, exactly as published, with the source on screen.

## The read-back (do it every time, before delivery)
Read the package back line by line against: a negative characterization of any brokerage (named or obvious) · a
negative word about any person · a former brokerage named · compensation of any kind · an earnings claim · a
"#1 / best / fastest-growing" without a dated source · an unverified fact stated as fact · a pitch instead of a
take · anything the brokerage's own policy in `compliance.md` forbids. Rewrite what fails; if the take can't
survive the rewrite, pick another item.

## Example (shape only — fictional member, illustrative)
**Item:** an industry outlet reports a rule change on how buyer agreements must be signed · fact: "effective the 1st"
```
HOOK (word-for-word):
"The buyer-agreement rules just changed. Here's what it means if you're new."

TALKING POINTS (riff):
• Effective the 1st — signed before the first showing, no exceptions (that's the line on screen)
• If you're in your first two years: this is the conversation you were already avoiding — now it's required
• My take: this is good for agents who can explain their value out loud. That's a skill, and it's learnable
• What we did: Tuesday's call was the whole script, role-played twice — it's in the vault for our group
• CTA: comment SCRIPT and I'll send you the one-page version we use

ON-SCREEN: "Effective the 1st" · "New agents: watch this" · "Comment SCRIPT"
Runtime: ~45 sec
```
Note: no brokerage named, nobody criticized, no money, one fact, a real take, one rung with the keyword.
