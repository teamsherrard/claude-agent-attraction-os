# The Story Prompt Deck — four categories, pull one when there's nothing to say

Built from `07-instagram/89` (what to post on stories), `07-instagram/90` (the daily routine), `06-content-framework/38–39`,
and the launching doc's "passions and hobbies" note. The cohort lists this deck as a new asset; this is the OS's
version. Every prompt becomes a story with a **text overlay (≤15 words)**, a **sticker**, and a **reply CTA**.
Pull from the category the member hasn't hit this week first. Doctrine: `shared/mike-frameworks.md` §9c.

## The daily rhythm (`07/90`)
Morning: getting ready / the coffee with one insight · Midday: a tip, a shout-out, behind the scenes of the day ·
Evening: the reflection or the personal moment. One to five a day; never a day without (`07/89`).

---

## 1 · Behind the scenes of leading (Proof)
*"Are you actually doing what you say you're doing?" (`07/89`)*
1. The weekly call — a screenshot (names blurred unless consented) + "the one thing we covered today."
2. What I'm learning this week — the book page, the course screen, the mentor's line. "What I'm investing in to lead better."
3. The routine — the 5am alarm, the gym, the prospecting block. "Discipline on a Tuesday."
4. Planning an event — the venue, the list, the spreadsheet. "Something's coming for my agents."
5. A 1:1 with an agent (with their okay) — "today's coaching call: one problem, one fix."
6. Recording day — the tripod, the notes. "Five Reels, one hour. Here's the plan."
7. Building new training — the slide deck screenshot. "New module for the group — dropping Friday."
8. The morning after a hard day — "yesterday was rough. Here's what I'm doing about it today."
9. A leader's desk — "what my Monday actually looks like."
10. The thing nobody sees — the follow-ups, the onboarding doc, the thank-you note.
Stickers: question box ("what's your Monday look like?"), poll ("gym before or after calls?"), slider.
Reply CTAs: "Reply 'CALL' if you want the recording" · "Reply with your Monday routine."

## 2 · Agent wins (Proof — recognition creates FOMO)
*"Tag the agent — they reshare, and it goes to a whole new audience." (`07/89`)*
1. First deal — "[First name]'s first closing. Eight months of zero before this." (consent)
2. A milestone — the cap, the award, the listing count. "Earned, not given."
3. The call recap — "[N] agents on today's call" (the real number) + the biggest takeaway, tagged.
4. An agent applied the training — "she used the open-house routine from Tuesday. Here's what happened."
5. A testimonial line — verbatim, attributed as given, consent on file.
6. A reshared screenshot — the agent's own story of the call, reposted with a line.
7. An agent's content win — "he posted his first Reel. Go follow him."
8. The group at an event — the dinner, the room, the people (the experience, never the price).
9. The new partner — "welcome [first name] — here's what we're building together" (never the brokerage pitch).
10. A quiet win — "she said no to a bad listing this week. That's growth."
Stickers: mention @agent, poll ("who's next?"), "DM me your win" box.
Reply CTAs: "Reply 'WIN' with yours — I'll share it" · "Reply if you want to know what she changed."

## 3 · Personal (Personality — relatable leaders are attractive leaders)
*"Would they want to go for a patio beer with you?" (`07/89`) · "People join people, not just leaders." (`07/88`)*
1. The family Sunday — the dinner, the parents, the kids. One honest line.
2. The dog — Maggie-level consistency: a weekly appearance people start to expect.
3. The workout — "day 212." No preaching.
4. The hobby — the bike, the boat, the guitar, the garden. The agents who share it will reply first.
5. Travel — where, with whom, and the one thought it gave you about the business.
6. The down day — "not every day is a highlight. Here's what I do with the bad ones."
7. Food for thought — a line from a book, a podcast, a conversation. "Chew on this."
8. The thing from before real estate — the old job, the old town, the old you.
9. A value, plainly — "why I still return every call the same day."
10. The reason — the WHY line from the journey, on a quiet evening.
Stickers: poll ("coffee or tea?" — small, human), question box ("what do you do outside of real estate?"), quiz.
Reply CTAs: "Reply if you ride too" · "Reply with the book that changed your year."
Rules: real, never flaunted (`05-big-picture/36`); nothing on the member's off-limits list (`content-pillars.md`).

## 4 · Opportunity (echo the value proposition — value, never a pitch)
*"Quick wins for agents in your audience… screenshots of tools, templates, systems… polls and quizzes." (`07/89`)*
1. The one-line tip — "the text I send the morning after an open house: [line]."
2. The script line — "when they say 'we're just looking,' say this."
3. The tool screenshot — "what I'm building for the group this week" (a tease, not the whole thing).
4. The poll on their business — "what's holding you back this quarter?" (four options from the five pains).
5. The quiz — "how many deals should a 2nd-year agent close? A / B / C" → reply with the real answer.
6. The AMA box — "ask me anything about [the known-for]" → every question becomes a Reel.
7. The sneak peek — the upcoming training, the event date, the guest. "Don't show all your cards."
8. The YouTube thumbnail — "new video — link up" (from Week 4).
9. The resource — "the checklist I give every agent who joins — reply 'LIST' and I'll send it" (only a real one
   from `offer.md`; at `seeds`, offer "what I'm building" or a call).
10. The invitation, rarely — "I'm doing three partner calls this week — reply 'CALL' if you want one." (The Call
   rung, at most once a week, never the model, never the money.)
Stickers: poll, quiz, question box, link (the link-in-bio resource or the booking link), countdown (an event).
Reply CTAs: "Reply 'LIST' / 'SCRIPT' / 'CALL'" (the story-reply flow word) · "Reply with your answer."

## Interactive sticker rules (`07/89`)
- At least one interactive story a day ("it gets insight on your audience and boosts engagement").
- Polls get the most engagement: two clear options, one tap. "Top or bottom?" works for a logo, a hook, a title.
- The question box turns into content — every answer is a Reel or a story tomorrow.
- A poll about the agent's business is the opportunity category; a poll about coffee is the personal category.

## The reply CTA → the conversation
A reply is the DM rung of the ladder. The reply word (CALL · LIST · SCRIPT · WIN · YES) is the story-reply
sequence's trigger when ManyChat is connected; otherwise the member replies by hand the same day. A real
conversation hands to `sf-comment-to-dm` and on to the Conversion plugin — never logged here.

## Never in a story
A split, a cap, stock, a fee, an income figure · a negative word about any brokerage or person · a former
brokerage's name · an agent's name or face without consent · a call screenshot with unconsented names · an
invented attendee count · a price tag as the point of an event · "stop scrolling".
