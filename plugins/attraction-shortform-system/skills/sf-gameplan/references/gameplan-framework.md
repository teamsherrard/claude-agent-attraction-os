# Short-Form Game Plan Framework — Mike's Instagram method, made personal

The backbone of the Short-Form Game Plan. It reads like a premium coaching document — honest positioning, five
pillars with real angles, a week the member can sustain, thirty hooks, the ask that starts conversations, and
thirty days with a date on every post. Substance and structure are what make it worth the cohort price — never
visual design. The short-form twin of the YouTube Game Plan (`attraction-youtube-system/skills/yt-gameplan`):
one strategy, two engines; the five pillars are the same five everywhere.

**This framework is the short-form doctrine made personal** (`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/mike-frameworks.md`):
§1 what short form must accomplish (`07-instagram/86`) · §5 the five pillars (`06-content-framework/37–40`,
`07-instagram/88`) · §6 problems, not brokerage features · §7 the video · §8 the ladder and the keyword · §9a the
profile (`07-instagram/87`) · §9b Reels (`/88`) · §9c stories (`/89`) · §9d the routine (`/90`) · §11 the cardinal
rules (`03-model-positioning/13`). Only lessons that resolve are cited; where the vault is thin, doctrine §15 says
so and this framework labels the rule an OS default.

## Section 1 — Positioning (the read, scaled; honest, never flattery)
- **The verdict** comes from `memory/content-log.md`, `memory/ideas.md → ## Past content (imported)` and the
  profile's handles: a feed built for buyers and sellers (*"your content pulls clients, not agents — the fix is
  the mix, not the effort"* — doctrine §12), a blank slate (*"clean slate, clear plan"*), or a consistent leader
  with no ask (*"strong output, no conversations — nothing on the feed tells an agent what to do next"*).
- **The one line** — from `offer.md`'s UVP when Week 2 built it (`## UVP`, the `Comment keyword:` line); at seeds,
  built from `strategy.md` + `avatars.md` and labeled "(draft until your offer is built)". Never demand the offer.
- **The two CTAs** — 1. Book a call (`operations.md` booking link, `brain.md` quick reference) · 2. The guide /
  keyword — the live magnet (`memory/magnets.md → ## Current magnet`) first, the free thing they give today
  second, the call last. The brokerage is never the hook (`07-instagram/87`, `05-big-picture/35b`).
- **The keyword** — one word, chosen once, carried by every Reel (doctrine §8): the offer's `Comment keyword:`
  (the invitation word `attraction-offer` coined) first; GUIDE when a live magnet rides on it; a resource word
  otherwise; never a brokerage name, never a word their followers already type by habit. Setup normally wrote it
  at the profile step; this plan proposes it only when the line is empty.
- **Why I'm building this** — `journey.md → ## The WHY` verbatim in their voice; drafted and labeled
  "(draft — your Brain's WHY is written in Setup)" where empty (`01-foundation-mindset/10`: write it down, replay it
  on the hard days). Enthusiasm carries it.

## Section 2 — The five pillars (doctrine §5; the former setup "pillars" step, now here)

|---|---|---|

| **Perspective** — what I believe | the takes the member holds (the "one thing they wish struggling agents understood"), the objections heard (`memory/objections.md`), three myths to bust; never about a named brokerage or person | `07-instagram/88` · `03-model-positioning/13` |
| **Story** — where I've been | the three journey beats with "who relates to this", the `## Why join me` breath, the story-bank hooks tagged for a Reel; former brokerages never named ("a franchise", "an independent") | `06-content-framework/39` · `/40` |
| **Proof** — what's happening in my world | agents already helped (`proof.md`, consent column — otherwise first name or initials), the weekly call, the training, the events, the recognition; the upline's proof labeled as the upline's; zero proof → one honest line and "earn it by helping agents free" (`04-value-proposition/33`) | `06-content-framework/38` · `07-instagram/88` |
| **Personality** — who I am off the clock | the profile's "before real estate" line, the journey, the passions and routines they will share; the off-limits list | `07-instagram/88` · `/89` |

Rules: develop, never transcribe (the echo test); every angle passes the leader test (`07-instagram/86`: would a
prospect see a leader worth joining from this post?) and the any-agent test; brokerage and opportunity content is
woven through the pillars, never the feed; compensation never appears (doctrine §6, §11). The one stop: the five
as one block, "say 'good' or tell me what's off." The Personality questions (2–4 together) only when the Brain is
blank there. Write `content-pillars.md` on the yes — the file shape is the contract's; the header lines are kept.

## Section 3 — The mix and the cadence (doctrine §7, §9b, §9d)
- **Three to five Reels a week, working up to five** (`07-instagram/88`); three is the floor. Propose the number
  from `goals.md`'s hours and `strategy.md`'s constraints — never ask for hours the Brain holds.
- **The default mix "2 attraction · 2 authority · 1 story, stories daily"** is the cohort's Week 3 standard, not a
  lesson of Mike's — label it **"the OS default until Mike's decision"** in the doc (doctrine §15). Attraction =
  Proof + Personality · authority = Authority + Perspective · story = Story (§9d). At three a week: 1 · 1 · 1.
  Mike's sample week (`07-instagram/90`) is the fallback shape.
- **Formats**: the talking-head Reel (30–60 s, hook → value → one CTA, captions the only mandatory edit, raw beats
  polished — `07-instagram/90`) · the green screen (Perspective on brokerage and industry news from `memory/intel.md`,
  facts with a source, never a dig) · the carousel (no filming; "Why I switched — no names", pain-point,
  myth-busting; a LinkedIn document post for team leaders and broker-owners) · stories (one to five a day, never a
  day without — `07-instagram/89`; the four categories).
- **Platforms**: Instagram Reels first; one video reposted to TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Facebook Reels
  (`07-instagram/90`); LinkedIn carries the carousel. Written to `publishing.md → Platforms (priority order):`.
- **The batch day**: Monday plan · Tuesday record (one to two hours) · Wednesday edit · post or schedule from
  Thursday (`07-instagram/90`), or Mike's alternate Saturdays; fitted to `operations.md → Filming window:`.
- **The 15-minute routine** (an OS default — Mike's lessons give the moves, the OS gives the minutes): reply to
  every comment with a question within the day · answer every DM the same day · tag the agents featured so they
  reshare (`07-instagram/89`) · one interactive story element a day · five real comments on agents' posts in the
  lane, never a negative one (`05-big-picture/36`).
- One word confirms it. Write `Cadence:` · `Weekly mix:` · `Batch day(s):` · `Platforms` to `publishing.md`; mirror
  `Cadence` and `Platforms` into `content-pillars.md`'s header lines; `## Weekly mix` restates it.

## Section 4 — The hook bank (doctrine §7 — the hook; §9b)
- **Thirty, six per pillar**, each under 12 words, one breath, the first word does the work; styles varied
  (question · contrarian · number/stakes · mistake · callout · curiosity-gap), no two the same shape; never "stop
  scrolling", never "hey guys", never credentials first.
- **Order of sources (locked, the same rank `sf-ideas` uses):** the member's own angles (section 2) and captured
  ideas → the Brain's signals (`memory/conversations.md` read-only, `memory/objections.md`, the avatar's "biggest
  problem in their words") → fresh research only where the Phase 6 pass already ran → the starter hook bank last,
  only to fill a short pillar, every seeded line marked `starter`, placeholders swapped per the bank's rules.
- Each hook carries its pillar, the agent it is for, and the rung it ends on. Appended as one dated block to
  `content-pillars.md → ## Hooks bank`.

## Section 5 — CTAs and the comment-to-DM flow (doctrine §8)
- The ladder, stated once: Follow / save / share → Comment → DM → Resource → Conversation → Call. Every Reel ends
  on exactly one rung, said on camera, written as the caption's last line, pinned as the first comment.
- The ask map: Authority → Comment / Resource · Perspective → Follow / save / share · Story → DM · Proof → DM ·
  Personality → Follow only. Five spoken ask lines in the member's voice, one per pillar.
- **The call line at most once a month** — an OS rule, not a Week 3 lesson (doctrine §8; `02-prospect-targeting/19`:
  the model is explained on a private call, never in a DM; never skip rungs).
- The keyword and what it opens; by hand or ManyChat (Mike's sequences — PARTNER · GUIDE · SCALE · GROWTH — import
  into the member's own account; optional; the keyword is not). The full CTA bank (fifteen lines), the DM bank and
  the paste sheet are `sf-comment-to-dm`'s keyword sheet — pointed at, never duplicated.

## Section 6 — The 30-day calendar (doctrine §9b–§9d; the rules in `sf-talkinghead/references/calendar-guide.md`)
- **Built with `sf-ideas`' method and order:** the Brain's own stories and systems first (the story bank, the proof
  with consent, the weekly call, the training, the events, the member's captured ideas, this month's objections —
  they land in Weeks 1–2), fresh research second (ONE pass, ≤ 8 searches, graded HIGH / MEDIUM / EMERGING, cited by
  number in SOURCES; web search only — the live data connection is never touched during setup), the starter bank
  last (`starter`, never presented as a demand signal).
- **Dates are real.** The first row lands on the first posting day on or after the batch day that follows today
  (UPDATE mode: the day after the last mapped date); posting days follow the cadence (five a week → Monday to
  Friday; three → the sample week's Monday · Wednesday · Friday unless the member said otherwise); every row carries
  `YYYY-MM-DD` and the weekday. Twenty rows at five a week; twelve at three — said plainly, the floor moves with it.
- **Row shape:** `#` · date · day · format (talking head · green screen · carousel) · pillar · working title · hook ·
  CTA (rung + keyword) · carries (story hook / win / teach / finding #) · System ID `sf-YYYY-MM-DD-NN` (the publish
  date and the row number — `sf-board` creates and finds the card by it; the Monday Week Plan updates it).
- **The calendar-guide rules bind:** Week 1 establishes who they are (one Story, one Authority on the known-for,
  one Proof, one Personality, one Perspective — an agent who finds them in Week 1 can answer the five profile
  questions from the feed alone); the member's own ideas and this month's objections in Weeks 1–2; no story used
  twice; no pillar more than twice in a week; the keyword on at least three rows a week; the direct call rung at
  most once in the month and only as the why-join-me beat; brokerage and opportunity content woven through, never
  a week of it; nothing invented — no stat without a source, no story not in the bank, no win without consent.
- **STORIES, DAILY** is a standing line, never twenty story rows: the four-category rotation every day; the rhythm
  morning · midday · evening (`07-instagram/90`); the daily stories prompt (section 8) drafts them when it is on.
- **THE BATCH PLAN** names the sessions (Mike's Tuesday, or alternate Saturdays), what films together (same outfit,
  same spot), and the hours the month really takes. **THE THREE TO FILM FIRST**: one line each on why.
- Written to `memory/content-calendar.md` (the Brain's calendar; the contract's locked shape). Rows are not logged
  to `content-log.md` until a piece is scripted.

## Section 7 — What we measure (the KPIs `sf-analytics` reads)
People who saw you · Reels posted of the cadence · story days of seven · keyword comments · conversations started
(from `memory/conversations.md`, never estimated) · calls booked from content (`memory/sales-funnel.md` once the
Conversion plugin exists; before that the booking form's answer or nothing). The member's own median is the
benchmark; growth = today minus the last baseline in `memory/content-performance.md`. Sources, honestly: live data
once connected after setup · the posting tool · "add my numbers" screenshots; "not available" is a real value.
The compliance line from Step 1b. Views are not the score — agents are (doctrine §0).

## The gate (hard; run before assembling — count, never estimate)
1. Every section present at its floor (the table above).
2. Twenty dated rows (twelve at three a week, said plainly); every row a real date, a pillar, a hook, a CTA with the
   keyword, a System ID.
3. Five pillars, exact names, three angles each.
4. Thirty hooks, six per pillar, under 12 words.
5. The keyword present in sections 1 and 5 and on at least three rows a week.
6. No compensation, split, cap, stock, rev-share, or income figure anywhere.
7. No brokerage named other than the member's own — and that one only where `compliance.md` allows it.
8. No `[bracket]` placeholder. A fact the member declined to give is flagged at the top, never left in brackets.
9. No negative word about any brokerage or person; former brokerages unnamed; every named agent consented; every
   number sourced or tagged "(illustrative — demo)".
A plan that fails a line is fixed, then re-counted. Never ship past the gate.

## UPDATE mode (on-demand re-runs — update, never rebuild)
- Read `content-pillars.md → ## Game Plan anchors` first: the plan date, the calendar window, the research used.
- Refresh only what the member asked for or what moved: a new 30-day window from the day after the last mapped
  date (rows at `Scripted` or later in `memory/content-calendar.md` are never rewritten; rows still at `Idea` past
  their date are re-dated into the new window, once, and said so); new research inside the same budget; a pillar
  they named; the mix if their cadence changed (one word again); the workflow statuses from `config.md`.
- Everything the member confirmed stays verbatim. The doc saves as a new dated copy (newest is current); the
  anchors block is rewritten; `publishing.md` lines change only if the member changed them.
- "Plan my next 30 days of reels" = UPDATE scoped to section 6 (+ the anchors) — then offer the board top-up
  (`sf-board`, mapping mode) in one line.

## Demo mode
A fictional member: no research, no live data, fictional agents and wins, every figure tagged "(illustrative —
demo)", the SOURCES band says "Demo — nothing searched", filename `… — DEMO — YYYY-MM-DD`, the demo workspace
only; nothing written to a real Brain. Same structure, same gate.

## Tone + premium feel
Honest, confident, encouraging, specific — a coach who has watched a hundred leaders start posting, not a hype
guru. Bespoke on every line (their lane, their agent, their stories, their organization). In the member's voice.
Plain language in front of them — "the agents you attract", never "leads", "recruits", or "avatar". The premium
feel is depth + structure + the stamp, delivered as a clean doc; for a designed showpiece the member takes it to
Claude Design (words only from here).
