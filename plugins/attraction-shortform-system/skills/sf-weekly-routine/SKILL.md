# The Weekly Routine Planner

Consistency is the whole game. Mike has not missed a week since he joined his brokerage, and that
consistency, not cleverness, is why the engine works (`07-instagram/90`). Most leaders do not fail on
quality; they fail by quitting. This skill makes the week simple and repeatable: what to film, when to
film it, what to post each day, and the fifteen minutes that turn attention into conversations.

**Apply** `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/house-rules.md` and the advisor stance in
`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/advisor-playbook.md` (recommend first; one easy question at most).

**Week 4 rules that bind the plan** (the rewire brief, 2026-11-14 — where older text disagrees, these win):
- **Two keywords, never more** — `GUIDE` (the guide) and the offer's `Comment keyword:` (the invitation), both on
  `publishing.md`'s `Keyword:` line; every row's ask is one of them, and every call ask is the law's shape (the person
  · their help · free · to run the method — never the brokerage).
- **The six kit posts come first** when they are still unposted: the Game Plan's first rows, already designed in
  Week 3 (the member's Offer folder) — "post, don't make"; a day that carries one says *post it*, never *film it*.
- **Scalable by design.** No row, story prompt or routine line promises the member's time per partner — no in-person
  days beside the member, no chat group, no one-to-one calls, no "call me any time"; the weekly group call appears
  only when `identity/operations.md` shows the member already runs one. The member's OWN batch day is their routine.
- **The workflows around this plan** (switched on by the consent card's Yes, read from `config.md → ## Short-Form
  (Week 4)`): the Monday Week Plan drafts the week; the Batch Prep leaves the teleprompter doc the evening
  before the filming day — the cards come inside the Monday package (never a separate Batch Prep task since 2026-11-22);
  instead of re-planning them); "how's my content doing" scores the week any time (the on-demand read; no weekly
  note). Never claim a workflow that is not switched on.
- **The engagement routine is text, not a task** (the owner, 2026-11-14): the fifteen minutes live here and in the
  Game Plan's section 3 — never a scheduled list of agents.

**The Monday Week Plan and this planner, one line each way:** the Monday Week Plan (`sf-week-plan`, scheduled
with the member's yes) drafts the week's posts from the Game Plan's calendar every Monday; this planner is the
member-run rhythm around those drafts — the batch block, the daily story habit, the engagement slot, the check-in
— and when the Monday plan ran this week, it plans around those drafts instead of re-choosing them.

## Two modes (detect from the message)
- **PLAN** (default) — "plan my attraction week", "set my batch day": build the week.
- **CHECK-IN** — "how did my week go", "did I hit my routine": score the week that just ended and set the
  next one. Runs happily on a Friday or Sunday.

## The rhythm (Mike's lessons, carried whole — the two OS defaults labeled as such)
- **Reels: two a week, plus one carousel** — Mike's cohort week (2026-11-22), until momentum is real. The default mix
  every week — the cohort's Week 4 standard, not a lesson of Mike's (his sample week is in Step 2; confirm the
  mix with him, `mike-frameworks.md` §15): **1 attraction · 1 authority · 1 story** at the default three a week (2 · 2 · 1
  only at five, the step-up) — attraction = a Proof or
  Personality Reel; authority = an Authority or Perspective Reel (one step of the member's method, `offer.md → ## The
  framework`, per teaching Reel — the guide as where the rest lives); story = a Story Reel (`mike-frameworks.md` §9d).
  Static posts — the week's carousel (a kit post while they last), then recognition and personal moments — are spliced in; they do not replace
  the Reels.
- **Stories: every day, one to five**, a mix of personal and value; never a day without one
  (`07-instagram/89`). Reels build width and awareness; stories build depth and connection.
- **One video covers every short-form platform** (`07-instagram/90`) — the OS reads it as: Instagram and Facebook,
  both, every Reel and every graphic (Facebook Reels is the same video reposted); TikTok and YouTube Shorts take the
  same video any time the member wants more reach (optional reposts — nothing new to make, never asked); LinkedIn
  for the carousels only when they attract team leaders (the owner, 2026-11-14). Editing stays simple: captions are
  the only mandatory edit; raw and genuine outperforms sensory overload — *"filmed? New chat → 'edit my reel'"* (the
  AI Editing Studio).
- **The batch day** — Mike's simple option: **Monday** brainstorm the two Reel topics · **Tuesday**
  record them (one to two hours, all in one sitting) · **Wednesday** edit or hand to the editor · post from
  Thursday or schedule the week (`07-instagram/90`). Mike's own version is bi-weekly Saturdays (plan one
  Saturday, record the next, long-form and short-form alternating). Offer the simple option first; fit it
  to `operations.md`.
- **Idea capture** — ideas come from conversations, coaching calls, and the questions agents ask; capture
  them the moment they land (the Brain's capture skill, tag `shortform`) and let AI fill the other half.
  Mike: about half his ideas are AI-assisted, half are his own from experience, and the member should know
  their audience better than any prompt (`07-instagram/90`).
- **The engagement routine (an OS default: 15 minutes a day, same time every day — Mike's lessons give the
  moves, the OS gives the minutes)** — reply to every comment with a
  question within the day · answer every DM the same day · tag the agents you feature so they reshare it
  (their story reaches a whole new audience, `07-instagram/89`) · reshare agents' screenshots of your calls
  and wins (`06-content-framework/38`) · one interactive story element a day (poll, question box, "ask me
  anything") · leave real comments on five agents' posts in your lane, never a negative one, ever
  (`05-big-picture/36`). Every conversation that starts here is handed to `sf-comment-to-dm`.
- **Leaders create leaders** — encourage the agents in the organization to post and tag the member; their
  combined reach is the multiplier (`07-instagram/90`).

## Step 2 — PLAN: the week (use these exact section names)
- **THE WEEK AT A GLANCE** — one table, Monday to Sunday: day · what posts (the calendar row's working title
  and pillar — the Game Plan's dates are the default — a kit post's day reads "post [Post n] — already designed"; or
  "stories only") · the story prompt for that day · the ask (the 90/10 rule (house rules #13): about one post in ten carries a keyword ask — Comment GUIDE, or the offer's word with the call line in the law's shape; every other post asks only for a follow, a save, a share or a comment on the topic; never two asks on one post; "follow" on a Personality day) · the
  engagement slot · minutes. Mike's sample
  calendar (`07-instagram/90`) is the default shape: Mon value (Authority) · Tue recognition (Proof) · Wed
  leadership insight (Perspective) · Thu culture or event recap (Proof or Personality) · Fri personal or
  opinion (Story or Perspective). Rebalance to the mix in force (one of each at three; 2·2·1 at five) and to their realistic number.
- **THE BATCH BLOCK** — the day, the start time, the length (about three hours), the two titles
  in film order, the one setup (phone at eye level, natural light, one outfit). When the Batch Prep is on, say
  the teleprompter cards will be waiting the evening before; otherwise point at the film-day plan ("film day for my
  attraction reels") for a full run sheet when they have more than five.
- **THE DAILY STORY HABIT** — seven prompts, one per day, from `sf-stories`' four categories (behind the
  scenes of leading · agent wins · personal · opportunity); each under ten words; one interactive element; a reply ask
  is "Reply GUIDE" or a plain reply — never a third word; no prompt promises the member's time per partner.
- **THE ENGAGEMENT ROUTINE** — the fifteen-minute checklist above, with the time of day they will do it.
- **THE CHECK-IN TABLE** — empty, for the end of the week: Reels filmed · Reels posted · story days · comments replied
  · DMs answered · conversations started · calls booked.
- **ONE CALENDAR BLOCK** — one line, the Yes shape: *"Say Yes and I'll put the batch block and the daily engagement
  slot on your calendar."* (Google Calendar or Outlook, per the Brain's connector). Only on the Yes; never silently.

## Step 3 — CHECK-IN: score the week and set the next one
Read `memory/content-log.md` (rows this week, by pillar), `memory/conversations.md` (rows with Channel =
DM or comment this week), and, if `sf-analytics` has written one, the latest block in
`memory/content-performance.md`. Report, plainly:
- the check-in table filled in from the log (never from memory or guesswork; a number you cannot see is
  "not logged yet")
- the mix they actually posted vs the mix in force (one of each at three; 2·2·1 at five), and the pillar that went missing
- the one thing that started a conversation this week (name the post)
- **THE ONE CHANGE** for next week (exactly one), then the next week's plan (Step 2)
Encourage. A thin week is data, not failure.

## What this skill never does
- Writes nothing to `identity/` and nothing to `memory/content-log.md`. Rows are written by the skills
  that make and ship the content (`sf-talkinghead`, `sf-stories`, `sf-carousel`, `set-up-my-metricool-and-post`). Calendar
  events are the only thing it creates, with a yes.
- Never posts, schedules, or sends. Never asks for the niche, the avatar, the hours, or the weekly number
  when the Brain has them.
