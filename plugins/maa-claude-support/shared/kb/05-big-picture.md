# The Big Picture (Week 3)

A short module that zooms out before the content work begins. Mike contrasts chasing agents (it sucks, recruiters burn out) with attracting them (his numbers from two YouTube videos a week, no outreach, no ads), lays out the whole journey on one slide (niche and value proposition, consistent content with calls to action, discovery call, present the model, handle objections, commit, onboard, plug in, retain, duplicate), and delivers the concept he says is worth the entire program times ten: becoming attractive, which has nothing to do with appearance and everything to do with how you show up, speak to yourself, treat others, share, and live.

Lessons: 35 Attracting vs. Chasing · 35b The Big Picture · 36 Becoming Attractive

### 35 — Attracting vs. Chasing
*Loom: https://www.loom.com/share/096d3466f75a4788acea7b7498651266 · 4 min*
**In one line:** Chasing agents is pushy, transactional, and burns recruiters out; attraction means agents arrive 80% decided because your brand built trust before the first conversation.
**Mike's points:**
- Chasing: cold calling agents who have no idea who you are, constantly pitching and convincing people you are worth listening to. It feels like selling your soul, creates resistance, and agents pull away.
- Attracting: become the leader agents seek out. A brand that builds trust before the first call makes conversion easy.
- His story from that day: one of his agents was on a call, the prospect asked if he knew Mike, Mike popped in, and the agent joined on the spot because he had watched all the videos. That took five and a half years of brand building; it was not like that at the start.
- Share value through content that pulls agents toward you; if he gives this much away free, people wonder what is behind the scenes.
- His numbers: 357 agents year one, 1,000 year two, about 600-plus agents a year booking from YouTube since; over 90% convert after one 30-minute call with no follow-up and no CRM (98% when he took the calls himself); never reached out to a single agent since 2020 and never spent a dime on ads; all from two YouTube videos a week.
- Why it works: agents join faster (about 80% decided before booking), fewer objections, less competition (rarely interviewing others), longer retention, and you attract the right fit for your culture.
**Say it like Mike:** "Chasing agents. This sucks. Most recruiters burn out." · "Your presence pretty much gets the deal done." · "I've never reached out to a single agent since joining the brokerage in 2020."
**Answers these questions:** Why not just message agents directly? How many agents did Mike attract each year? What conversion rate should I expect from calls? Does Mike run ads for attraction?
**Do / don't:** Do let content and brand do the convincing before the call. Don't cold pitch agents who have never heard of you.

### 35b — The Big Picture
*Loom: https://www.loom.com/share/f1ed1177dc0f4ba99270896706709c9c · 2 min*
**In one line:** The whole journey on one slide: build your package, post consistently with calls to action, convert on a call, then onboard, retain, and duplicate.
**Mike's points:**
- Before you start: identify your niche and create your unique value proposition. It can be a mastermind call to begin; build more training and tools behind the scenes over time.
- Getting started: post consistent content based on your niche and make your value proposition known. It takes time, but start.
- Include calls to action telling agents they can partner with you and get everything free.
- Agents book a discovery call; you present the model and why they should join you specifically.
- Handle objections and bridge the gap from where they are to where they want to be, with clarity. Then they commit.
- After the commit is when the game starts: onboard them, plug them into what you and your upline provide, support and retain them, help them attract, and duplication begins.
- Order of operations: package first, content second, present and convert third, then everything after conversion.
**Say it like Mike:** "That doesn't mean you have to go create a bunch of training. It could be a mastermind call to start with." · "After we convert them is when the game starts."
**Answers these questions:** What is the sequence of this whole system? Do I need training built before I post? What happens after someone joins? Where does content fit in?
**Do / don't:** Do get the package and the content going in that order. Don't treat the signed agent as the finish line.

### 36 — Becoming Attractive
*Loom: https://www.loom.com/share/2b4a479b3a024f1e98d01ef8d88d569c · 8 min*
**In one line:** If you want to attract, become attractive: energy, discipline, how you talk to yourself and treat others, what you share free, and whether you would follow yourself.
**Mike's points:**
- He says this one concept is worth the program times ten (probably a hundred) and is why most agents fail to attract and retain. Nothing to do with appearance.
- How do you show up? With energy on every call and video even when exhausted (he recorded this in the middle of the night). With discipline, always.
- How do you speak to yourself? Like you would encourage a friend, or constantly tearing yourself down?
- How do you treat others? He has never written a negative comment on anyone's content and never will. Friends making $200M-plus a year are the most humble; some at $500K think they walk on water. Treat people like your parents.
- How much value do you share free? Information is no longer novel; AI answers anything in seconds. Share it all, not fluffy theory plus "buy my program."
- How disciplined are you in health, family, body, time? Are you a role model?
- The mind-reading test: if a prospect could hear your thoughts and watch your whole day, would they still join you? Would you follow yourself?
- Excuses or ownership? Lead by example or just talk? New agents will follow a clear value proposition; top producers, influencers, team leaders, and brokers only follow strong people.
- Checklist: confidence not ego (don't flaunt the cars and house; it could all disappear and what's left is the man and the knowledge), clarity and conviction, share freely, create growth and recognition, live at a standard agents aspire to.
- Physical shape signals discipline (he says the workforce data supports it; he was teased for being overweight). You don't need a six-pack; take care of yourself.
**Say it like Mike:** "If you want to attract, you have to become attractive." · "Would you follow yourself?" · "Strong people follow strong people. They don't follow weak people."
**Answers these questions:** Why am I posting consistently but not attracting? What does "becoming attractive" actually mean? Should I show off my lifestyle? Does fitness really matter for attraction? How do I attract top producers?
**Do / don't:** Do audit energy, discipline, self-talk, and generosity. Don't lead with ego or negative comments about anyone.
