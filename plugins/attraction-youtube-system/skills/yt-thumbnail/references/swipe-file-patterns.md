## 1. The rules from the lesson (confirmed, citable)
- **Click-through rate is the gate.** A beautiful video nobody clicks gets 17 views. Aim for a 6–10% CTR
  read after a month, never on day one (friends inflate the first days).
- **The psychology of a click:** curiosity (pain to run from, or pleasure to run toward) · emotion (speak to
  a pain point, a desire, or a strong opinion) · clarity (what the video is about, in seconds).
- **Bold and emotional beats complex and clever.** High contrast, branded, the member's face in every one,
  big emotion, simple composition.
- **3–5 words max, large bold text.** Never a sentence.
- **The thumbnail text is DIFFERENT from the title.** Two chances to earn the click; the same words waste one.
- **A real facial expression** that matches the emotion of the title (surprise, concern, confidence, delight).
- **Contrast colors for attention** — Mike's purple backgrounds with white or yellow text; the member's own
  brand colors from `identity/brand-visual.md`, pushed to their highest-contrast pairing.
- **Visuals support the title without repeating it;** the viewer should feel "this is me, I need to watch".
- **Three thumbnails per video** — YouTube's Test & Compare picks the winner; one direction per thumbnail.
- **Test, track, tweak:** review CTR monthly; low CTR → new title and thumbnail; note which titles and visuals
  keep winning and let that shape the style.
- **Titles that attract agents** speak to pain and desire: "the truth about … nobody tells you", "the five
  mistakes keeping agents stuck", "how to … and be the exception", "how [name] closed … in 90 days".

## 3. Composition defaults (from the lesson rules; Claude Design owns the final layout, built from the brief)
- Face: roughly a third of the frame, cut out, on the brand background; eyes toward the text.
- Text: upper or side block opposite the face, 3–5 words, brand display font, highest-contrast pairing.
- One supporting element max (a guest's face for interviews; a brokerage wordmark only where
  `compliance.md`'s logo rule allows it; never a second brokerage's logo).
- Mobile check: legible at 320px wide; if not, fewer words.
