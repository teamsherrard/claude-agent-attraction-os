# Agent Interview Strategy — the videos that convert

Niche videos attract; interviews convert (`08-youtube/95`). An interview is five assets at once: content,
social proof, recognition for the guest, retention for the organization, and a recruiting asset that keeps
working (the long-form cohort doc). Apply `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/house-rules.md` and §5 (interviews that
convert) of `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/attraction-youtube-doctrine.md`. Obey
`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/brain-contract.md`: read `~/attraction-brain/brain.md` first, write back then push
immediately via `attraction-brain-sync`, and read `identity/compliance.md` before anything public.

**Week 5 rules that bind every interview** (the rewire brief, 2026-11-14 — where older text disagrees, these win):
- **The guest's consent** is recorded before anything public names them; **no figure the guest did not
  volunteer** — "closed her first deal" over an invented number; any figure labeled as theirs.
- **The casual call ask follows the law** — the person · their help · free · to run the method; never the
  brokerage as the reason, never compensation, never a promise of the guest's time or the member's. Join register
  (`identity/offer.md → Join-gated package allowed: yes` or `unknown`): *"If you want my help running what
  [guest] just walked you through — the lessons, the templates, the community — partner with me and it's free.
  [the booking line]."* Open register (`no`): *"I'll walk you through it, free, no strings."* The method
  (`## The framework`) is named. The spoken lines come from the Week 3 Kit's chapter 7 CALLS TO ACTION table (the
  newest `exports/week3-kit-*.txt`), verbatim; the guide ask ("comment GUIDE and I'll send it") is the second CTA.
- **Scalable by design** (the Brain's doctrine §10e). The guest describes the help as it is — the recorded
  lessons, the templates, the community, the weekly group call only when the member already runs one — never
  in-person days beside the member, a chat group, or one-to-one calls as the thing agents get.
- **The invite is in the member's voice** (`identity/voice.md`), a draft the member sends. The pipeline file's
  row shape is unchanged.
- **Member-facing words:** Week 5; plain and warm; the editing next step stated, never hedged (Plugin 5 — "when it's filmed: 'edit my interview'"); never a
  skill id, a file path or a task id in a sentence to the member.

**Cadence:** 4 of every 8 videos are interviews (3 niche · 1 model breakdown · 4 interviews) — at one a week
(the first month's default) that is one interview a fortnight, the first inside the first month. If the member has
no one to interview yet, say so without drama: the first guests are the people they are helping right now, and
"help agents for free and earn the stories" is the doctrine (`04-value-proposition/33`). Never manufacture a
guest, a result, or a quote.

## Step 2 — The guest list (who to interview — `08-youtube/95`)
**Read now:** `memory/top-50.md` (agents at Joined / Onboarded / Active, and friends at other brokerages who are
willing to be guests) · `identity/proof.md` (agents already helped, consent column) · `identity/avatars.md` (which
type each story is FOR) · `memory/content-log.md` (who has already been featured).
Rank candidates from these files and Step 1's, one line each, never a protected characteristic, never a number the
member did not state:
1. **New agent who closed fast** — a first deal inside the first 30–90 days.
2. **Capped / hit an award tier** using the member's method.
3. **The turnaround** — struggling at a previous brokerage, joined, now producing ("this one works magic" —
   `95`). The former brokerage is never named: "a franchise", "an independent".
4. **Top producer who scaled further** with the member's program.
5. **Team leader or broker-owner** who joined — speaks to that avatar directly.
6. **Attracted their first agent** — proof the duplication works.
7. **Guests outside the organization** (an admired friend at another brokerage, a vendor, a coach) — allowed
   when the member's org is small; label them as "guest expert", not "success story".

For each: name · type (six avatar types) · the transformation in one line (before → after, in their stated
words) · which avatar the story lands with · consent status (from `proof.md`: on file / ask). Then the
quarterly habit from the lesson: draft the "did you hit one of these?" note to the whole organization (first
deal fast · capped · attracted your first agent · turned your business around · scaled with the program) with
the member's booking link — **draft only**, the member sends it.

## Step 3 — The title: hook AND transformation (every interview)
**Read now:** `identity/offer.md` (`## The framework` — the method the story proves, the mechanism in the title;
the framework's name when the offer has one, never the program's type or cadence in a title; the header's
`Join-gated package allowed:` — the register of the call ask) · `identity/voice.md` (the title, the edification
lines, and the invite in their voice) · `identity/compliance.md` — the first line, `Status:` (a title is public;
the gate below applies from here).
The title carries who they are, what they built, and the mechanism: **"How [first name] [transformation] and
used [the method]"** — e.g., "How Priya closed 6 deals in 90 days as a brand-new agent using YouTube".
Rules: the result is the guest's own stated number or none (say "closed her first deal" rather than inventing
a figure); the mechanism names the method, never the brokerage's compensation; the title speaks to the avatar's
pain or desire (`08-youtube/97`); three options, one recommended. Thumbnail text differs from the title (`97`) —
hand the packaging to `yt-thumbnail` after the recording.

## Step 4 — The question set, by guest type (the structure — `08-youtube/95`)
Mike does not script questions; he knows the outcome and guides in real time. Give the member the outcome
sentence ("by the end, a [avatar] watching should believe [X]") plus a short set they can glance at. Every
set covers the five steps, in any order: **backstory** (what business was like before) · **the shift** (what
made them decide to move, what the hesitation was) · **the experience** (what it has been like since partnering
— the lessons, the templates, the community, in their words; never a line that promises the member's time to
the next agent) · **the results** (outcomes: freedom, income category, support — never a dollar figure the guest
did not volunteer, and any figure labeled as theirs) · **advice** (for an agent in the same spot).

Add the type-specific steps:
- **New agent:** the first-30-days routine; what they would tell a licensing-class friend.
- **Turnaround:** what was missing before (no names, no blame — the cardinal rules apply to the GUEST'S
  answers too; if they trash a brokerage or a person, the member redirects on camera and that part is cut).
- **Top producer:** what changed in leverage and time; what they were skeptical about.
- **Team leader / broker-owner:** what they kept (brand, people), what overhead disappeared, how the
  transition went for their agents.
- **Attracted their first agent:** how the conversation happened; what they said; what surprised them.

**Interviewer rules (non-negotiable, `95`):** the guest is the star; never interrupt; let emotion sit; open
questions; nod and listen; add a layer only after they finish, to help the viewer contextualize. Ask for
emotions, not just numbers. **Record the intro LAST** — after the conversation, the member records a 45–75 s
intro that hooks the best moments they just heard (the outro 30–45 s — `yt-script`'s interview playbook writes
both); the edit puts it first.

## Step 5 — Edification lines + the two asks
**Read now:** the newest `exports/week3-kit-*.txt` → chapter 7's CALLS TO ACTION table · `memory/magnets.md →
## Current magnet` (the guide's title and funnel URL).
- **Edification (opening):** two lines in the member's voice that make the guest look great before they
  speak — their stated result, who they were a year ago, why the member wanted them on. True facts only.
- **The guide ask (around the first minute, after the hook):** the kit's spoken resource line — *"[what the guide
  holds, one clause]. Comment GUIDE and I'll send it."*
- **The casual call ask (`95`, `98`) — near the end, with the next-video pointer (`99`):** the kit's spoken call
  line in the law's shape, the guest's example as the lead-in: *"If you want my help running what [guest] just
  walked you through — the lessons, the templates, the community — partner with me and it's free. [the booking
  line]."* (open register: *"I'll walk you through it, free, no strings."*). Warm, inviting, never a pitch; never
  the brokerage as the reason; never a promise of the guest's time.

## Step 6 — The invite + the guest's distribution ask (drafts, the member sends)
- **The invite** (DM or email, in the member's voice — `identity/voice.md`): why them, the 30–45 min format, the
  recording link (their recording tool, whatever it is), "you are the story", the one prep line ("think about where
  you were before and the moment things changed").
- **Release/consent:** a plain line asking their okay to publish, use clips, and share their name as written.
  Record the answer in `interview-pipeline.md` and point `attraction-voice-proof` to update `proof.md`'s
  consent column (this skill never writes `proof.md`). Nothing public names the guest before the yes.
- **The distribution ask:** when it goes live, ask the guest to share it to their story, post it with a line
  of their own, send it to three agents they know who are where they were. Give them the three sentences.
  Recognition is retention (`03-model-positioning/17`): the member tags them, celebrates them, and — when they
  use Claude Design — their brand project's content skill can make the win post (the short-form system writes
  the words: "a story about my agent's win").
