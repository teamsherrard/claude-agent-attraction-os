# Agent Interview Strategy — the videos that convert

Niche videos attract; interviews convert (`08-youtube/95`). An interview is five assets at once: content,
social proof, recognition for the guest, retention for the organization, and a recruiting asset that keeps
working (the long-form cohort doc). Apply `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/house-rules.md` and §5 (interviews that
convert) of `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/attraction-youtube-doctrine.md`. Obey
`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/brain-contract.md`: read `~/attraction-brain/brain.md` first, write back then push
immediately via `attraction-brain-sync`, and read `identity/compliance.md` before anything public.

**Week 5 rules that bind every interview** (the rewire brief, 2026-11-14 — where older text disagrees, these win):
- **The guest is anyone successful in the member's upline, downline or brokerage** (Mike, 2026-11-23): their
  sponsor, the people above them, an agent they sponsor, a peer, a team leader or a broker-owner at the same
  brokerage — and, as before, a coach, a vendor or anyone the member simply wants to talk with. A guest who
  succeeded with the member's own method is a bonus, never a requirement — *"if they have someone who did, great;
  otherwise just interview anyone successful."* Never only same-method agents.
- **An interview has no script** (Mike, 2026-11-23): the output is the question list and the invite (plus the
  title, the edification lines and the two CTA lines, the kit's). Nothing is scripted — not an intro, not an outro;
  `yt-script` writes nothing for an interview.
- **The guest's consent** is recorded before anything public names them; **never a dollar, income or commission
  figure — not even one the guest volunteers**; a deal count or a milestone only as the guest stated it, with
  their okay, labeled as theirs — "closed her first deal" over any number.
- **The casual call ask follows the law** — the person · their help · free · to run the method; never the
  brokerage as the reason, never compensation, never a promise of the guest's time or the member's. Join register
  (`identity/offer.md → Join-gated package allowed: yes` or `unknown`): *"If you want my help running what
  [guest] just walked you through — the lessons, the templates, the community — partner with me and it's free.
  [the booking line]."* Open register (`no`): *"I'll walk you through it, free, no strings."* The method
  (`## The framework`) is named. The spoken lines come from the Week 3 Kit's chapter 7 CALLS TO ACTION table (the
  newest `exports/week3-kit-*.txt`), verbatim; the guide ask ("comment GUIDE and I'll send it") is the second CTA.
  **Two guards:** the lessons, the templates and the community are named only when `identity/offer.md → ## Value
  stack` lists them — an offer still at seeds, or no kit on file → the plain line, *"If you want my help with
  this, it's free. [the booking line]."*; and "what [guest] just walked you through" is said only of a guest the Brain
  says joined the member — for any other guest the call line has no lead-in about them.
- **Scalable by design** (the Brain's doctrine §10e). The guest describes the help as it is — the recorded
  lessons, the templates, the community, the weekly group call only when the member already runs one — never
  in-person days beside the member, a chat group, or one-to-one calls as the thing agents get.
- **The invite is in the member's voice** (`identity/voice.md`), a draft the member sends. The pipeline file's
  row shape is unchanged.
- **Member-facing words:** Week 5; plain and warm; "the agents you want to attract" — never "your agents", "your
  team" or "your organization" as a fact unless the Brain holds such a person; the editor is "your AI Editing
  Studio" and its next step is stated, never hedged, word for word (*"When it's filmed, open a new chat and say
  'edit my YouTube video' (or 'edit my interview') — your AI Editing Studio edits it."*) — never a plugin number,
  an engine's name or an install question; never a skill id, a file path or a task id in a sentence to the member.

**Cadence:** 4 of every 8 videos are interviews (3 niche · 1 model breakdown · 4 interviews) — at one a week
(the first month's default) that is one interview a fortnight, the first inside the first month — once a guest
is named. Never manufacture a guest, a result, or a quote.

**Nobody to interview yet — the normal first-time member** (the owner, 2026-11-16 and 2026-11-21 — this wins over
any older line). Nobody named in the member's own words and nobody on file — no row in the pipeline, no agent who
joined in `memory/organization.md`, `identity/proof.md` or the Top-50, no `interview` idea of their own — →
**nothing is drafted to nobody**: no guest list, no invite, no question map, no "did you hit one of these?" note, no
pipeline row, no document. One short, true line with a real next step, by how they got here:
- **The plan's interview week has arrived** (this skill was reached from the week's video; its slot reads "A
  conversation with an agent — guest to be named"): the week's video is the next topic video from the member's four
  pillars — the next `Idea` row of `memory/content-calendar.md → ## Long-form (YouTube)` whose bucket is Problem,
  Situation or Future — and the interview moves to the first open week after a guest is named (the plan and the
  Monday Kickoff re-date it — this skill never re-dates a row). Say exactly: *"No guest named
  yet, so this week's video is '[title]', from your topics. Your first interview goes on your plan the day you
  name someone — say 'set up an agent interview'."* — and hand that title back to the video's chat, which makes
  it. No week is ever empty. (No plan on file → the second sentence alone.)
- **The member asked** ("who should I interview", "set up an agent interview") and named nobody: *"Your first
  interview goes on your plan the day you name someone — anyone successful in your upline, your downline or your
  brokerage: your sponsor, someone above you, an agent you sponsor, a peer at your brokerage. Tell me their first
  name and one line about them, and I'll write the questions and the invite. Until then your plan carries on with
  your topics."*
Never "the people you are helping", never "your agents", "your team" or "your organization" as a fact, never a
week that will fill it, never a list of what is missing, never an apology for being new. ("Help agents for free
and earn the stories" — `04-value-proposition/33` — is the doctrine behind the first guests; it is never said to a
member as something they are already doing.) **A guest the member names is a real guest even when the Brain does
not hold them** — Step 2 starts from that name.

## Step 2 — The guest list (who to interview — `08-youtube/95`; Mike, 2026-11-23: anyone successful in the upline, downline or brokerage)
**Read now:** `memory/top-50.md` (agents at Joined / Onboarded / Active, and anyone whose row says, in the member's
own words, that they would be a guest) · `identity/proof.md` (agents already helped, consent column) ·
`identity/avatars.md` (which type each story is FOR) · `memory/content-log.md` (who has already been featured).
**Nobody named and nobody in any of these files or Step 1's → the "nobody to interview yet" line (above), and
stop** — never a list with nothing in it. Otherwise the guest the member named comes first; then rank the
candidates from these files and Step 1's, one line each — by type of agent, stage of career and market, never a
protected characteristic (age, race, gender, language — whatever a "relatability" line elsewhere says), never a
number the member did not state. **Who qualifies — anyone successful in the member's upline, downline or
brokerage** (their sponsor and the people above them · an agent they sponsor · a peer, a team leader or a
broker-owner at the same brokerage), plus a coach, a vendor or anyone the member wants to talk with; types 1 to 6
below are the bonus cases — a guest the Brain says joined the member — never the requirement:
1. **New agent who closed fast** — a first deal inside the first 30–90 days.
2. **Capped / hit an award tier** using the member's method.
3. **The turnaround** — struggling before, joined, now producing ("this one works magic" — `95`). The former
   brokerage is never named ("a franchise", "an independent") and never the reason: the story is what the agent
   changed, not what a company lacked.
4. **Top producer who scaled further** with the member's program.
5. **Team leader or broker-owner** who joined — speaks to that avatar directly.
6. **Attracted their first agent** — proof the duplication works.
7. **Anyone successful who has not joined the member** — the member's sponsor or someone above them in the
   upline, a successful peer, team leader or broker-owner at the brokerage, an agent from the sponsor's team, a
   vendor, a coach, or anyone the member simply wants to talk with. Always allowed, and the usual first guest of a
   member who has not attracted an agent yet (Mike, 2026-11-23: "otherwise just interview anyone successful").
   Label them "guest expert", never "success story"; an agent from the sponsor's team is introduced as that team's,
   never as the member's.

For each: name · type (the pipeline file's own list — new agent · experienced · top producer · team leader ·
broker-owner · switched · outside the org; a guest who has not joined the member is `outside the org` in the file,
"guest expert" to the member) · their story in one line — for an agent who joined
the member, the transformation (before → after, in their stated words); for anyone else, what they do that other
agents should hear, in the member's words, never guessed · which avatar the story lands with · consent status
(the pipeline's Consent cell first, then `proof.md`: on file / ask). **Only when `memory/organization.md` holds at
least one agent:** the quarterly habit from the lesson — draft the "did you hit one of these?" note to those agents
(first deal fast · capped · attracted your first agent · turned your business around · scaled with the program)
with the member's booking link — **draft only**, the member sends it. No agent on file → no note, and nothing is
said about it.

**The pick is the member's — one stop, only when they have not made it** (house rule #23). The member named the
guest → say so in one line and build Steps 3 to 7 in the same reply. The member named nobody → the reply shows
the candidates, one line each, and ends: *"I'd start with [first name] — [why, one line]. Say **Yes** and I'll
write [first name]'s title, questions and invite — or tell me who you'd rather start with. **Your turn.**"*
Nothing of Steps 3 to 7 is read, built or saved before the answer; after it they are built together in one reply
(a chat run — an unattended run never asks, and never drafts for a guest nobody has named). **A guest the Brain
does not hold, named with nothing about them:** the working title is "A conversation with [first name]", the
invite says only that the member would love to have them on, and one line says how to sharpen it — *"Tell me in
a line what [first name] does that other agents should hear, and I'll sharpen the title."* — never a guessed
story.

## Step 4 — The question list, by guest type (the structure — `08-youtube/95`)
Mike does not script questions; he knows the outcome and guides in real time. Give the member the outcome
sentence ("by the end, a [avatar] watching should believe [X]") plus a short list they can glance at. **This list
is the whole preparation — an interview has no script** (Mike, 2026-11-23).

**A guest the Brain says joined the member** (a row in `organization.md`, `proof.md → Agents helped`, or the Top-50
at Joined or later) — every set covers the five steps, in any order: **backstory** (what business was like before)
· **the shift** (what made them decide to move, what the hesitation was) · **the experience** (what it has been
like since partnering — the lessons, the templates, the community, in their words; never a line that promises the
member's time to the next agent) · **the results** (outcomes in their words: freedom, time, support, a milestone —
never a dollar or income figure; if the guest volunteers one the member moves on and that part is cut) ·
**advice** (for an agent in the same spot).

**Any other guest — a peer, an agent from the member's sponsor's team, anyone the member simply wants to talk
with (a "guest expert")** — none of the five above: never "what made you decide to move", never "since
partnering", never a word about their brokerage or the member's. Their set: **their start** (how they got into
the business) · **the method** (the one thing they do that other agents should hear, step by step) · **the
mistake** (what they would skip if they started again) · **what it changed, in their words** (no figure) ·
**advice** (for an agent starting it this week).

Add the type-specific steps (a guest who joined the member only):
- **New agent:** the first-30-days routine; what they would tell a licensing-class friend.
- **Turnaround:** what they changed first, and what was missing in how they worked — never in a company (no
  names, no blame — the cardinal rules apply to the GUEST'S answers too; if they trash a brokerage or a person,
  the member redirects on camera and that part is cut).
- **Top producer:** what changed in leverage and time; what they were skeptical about.
- **Team leader / broker-owner:** what they kept (brand, people), what overhead disappeared, how the
  transition went for their agents.
- **Attracted their first agent:** how the conversation happened; what they said; what surprised them.

**Interviewer rules (non-negotiable, `95`):** the guest is the star; never interrupt; let emotion sit; open
questions; nod and listen; add a layer only after they finish, to help the viewer contextualize. Ask for
emotions, not just numbers. **Record the intro LAST** — after the conversation, the member records a short intro,
unscripted, from the two or three best moments they just heard: the transformation the title promises, who it is
for, the edification line (Step 5), the guide ask; the outro is the thank-you and the casual call line (Step 5),
said from the list — nothing scripted, by anyone (Mike, 2026-11-23); the edit puts the intro first.

## Step 6 — The invite + the guest's distribution ask (drafts, the member sends)
- **The invite** (DM or email, in the member's voice — `identity/voice.md`): why them, the 30–45 min format, one
  line that the recording link comes the day before, "you are the story", the one prep line (a guest who joined
  the member: "think about where you were before and the moment things changed"; any other guest: "think about
  the one thing you do that other agents should hear").
- **Release/consent:** a plain line asking their okay to publish, use clips, and share their name as written —
  and, for a guest who has not joined the member, one honest sentence that the video ends with the member's own
  invitation to agents (their okay covers that too). Record the answer in `interview-pipeline.md`'s Consent cell —
  this plugin's record, read first at Step 2, so a guest who said yes is never asked again (this skill never
  writes `proof.md`). The member reports the guest's reply without it → the one question, in these words: *"Did
  [first name] say yes to being published under their name? **Your turn.**"* Nothing public names the guest before
  the yes.
- **The distribution ask:** when it goes live, ask the guest to share it to their story, post it with a line
  of their own, send it to three agents they know who are where they were (a guest expert: three agents who
  would use it). Give them the three sentences. **Only for a guest who joined the member** — recognition is
  retention (`03-model-positioning/17`): the member tags them, celebrates them, and — when they use Claude Design —
  their brand project's content skill can make the win post (the short-form system writes the words — its phrase,
  "a story about my agent's win", is named only when `config.md` holds a `## Short-Form (Week 4)` block). A guest expert gets the thanks and the three sentences — never a win post, never "my
  agent".

## Step 6b — When they say yes: the booking and the recording ("book my agent interview")
Half the plan is interviews and the invite is drafted — the yes must turn into a date. Read `identity/channel.md →
Filming day:` first (the day the member gave at setup — house rule #19), then `identity/operations.md` (the
`Filming window:` line, or the batch day recorded there), and this guest's pipeline row. **Which guest:** the one
named; "book my agent interview" with no name → the row at `Invited` (one row → that guest, said in one line; two
or more → the one question, in these words: *"Which guest — [name] or [name]? **Your turn.**"* — a fact the Brain
cannot hold; no row at `Invited` and nobody named → the "nobody to interview yet" line — never a booking for
nobody). The reply opens with the win: *"✓ [First name] said yes — your interview is on."*
1. **Two recording times, stated — never "when works for you?".** The member's next two filming days that are at
   least three days out (the day they gave at setup first; none → the filming window; no window on file → the
   batch day and the same day a week later; none of these on file → the plan's own recording day for this video
   and the day before it), at the window's own time or 10:00 am, said as a default — "tell me if another time is
   better": *"Two times for [guest]: [the day, in words — "Thu, Dec 10" — and the time], or [the day and the
   time]."* With them, **the ready reply for the member to send the guest** — a copy block in the member's voice
   (`identity/voice.md`): *"So glad you're in. I can do [time 1] or [time 2] — whichever suits you. It's 30 to 45
   minutes, and I'll send the recording link the day before."* The member sends it; nothing is sent from here.
2. **The guest prep note — a copy block the member forwards**, short and warm, in the member's voice: **what to
   expect** (a conversation, not a presentation — you are the story, nothing to prepare) · **how long** (30 to 45
   minutes) · **the three questions you'll get first** (the first three of THIS guest's own question map — Step 4,
   word for word; "what made you decide to move" only for a guest the Brain says joined the member) · **how to
   look and sound good on camera** (face a window; the camera at eye level; a laptop or desktop rather than a
   phone; earbuds or headphones with a microphone; a quiet room; a plain background; a glass of water) · one line
   on consent (the recording, the clips and their name as written — only with their okay). Nothing in it promises
   the member's time beyond the recording.
3. **The recording step, stated:** *"The day before, open a new chat and say 'prep me for a guest recording' — your
   AI Editing Studio sets up the recording and gives you the link to send [guest]. When it's filmed, open a new
   chat and say 'edit my YouTube video' (or 'edit my interview') — your AI Editing Studio edits it."* Never a plugin number, an engine's name or
   "not installed yet?". **Only when this session can see the editor is missing** (no AI Editing Studio skill in
   its skill list) add: *"Install the AI Editing Studio from the same place you installed this plugin, then say
   'set up my video editor' once."* **Only when the Brain shows the member's editor does not record a guest** (the
   editor's own `editor/config.json` in the Brain reads `default_engine: edits`) the first sentence is instead:
   *"Record this one on any video call that saves the recording, and send [guest] that link the day before."*
4. **A calendar hold — only on the member's explicit yes, because it writes to their calendar.** Once the guest
   has picked a time (the member says which), print the date, the time and the title for the member to add —
   *"Interview: [guest] — [working title]", 60 minutes* — and, **only when a calendar connection is in the
   session**, state once, as a breadcrumb, never a menu: *"Say 'put it on my calendar' and I'll add it."* Only on
   those words create ONE event on the member's own calendar — that title, that time, 60 minutes, the prep note in
   its description, no one invited, nothing sent to anyone. No calendar connected → the line is not said; the
   printed date, time and title are the hold. An event is never created silently.
5. **The pipeline and the card:** move the guest's row in `memory/interview-pipeline.md` to **`Booked`** with the
   Recording date (`Booked` is written only with a real date the guest picked — until then the row stays `Invited`
   and the two times are noted in its Notes); when they have the board (`identity/publishing.md → Content board:`;
   that file absent → `identity/channel.md → Board:`), find the video's card (System ID → exact title → near match;
   a card that still reads "A conversation with an agent — guest to be named" → the earliest such card at `Idea`

   Interview Prep document's WHEN THEY SAY YES band. Push via `attraction-brain-sync` (write → push → verify). No
   board → the pipeline row is the record; nothing is lost.
