# Make This Video — one chat = one video

This chat IS the video — **three replies, and it waits after each** (house rules #23, ONE STEP, THEN WAIT; it wins
over every older "no stop" line — "The three replies", below). Everything saves to this video's folder. The member
only ever feels "we're making my video." Apply
`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/house-rules.md`, §8 (the video structure) and §10 (the two CTAs) of
`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/attraction-youtube-doctrine.md`, and `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/brain-contract.md`
(read `brain.md` first · write then push via `attraction-brain-sync` · `compliance.md` before anything public).

**Week 5 rules that bind every video** (the rewire brief, 2026-11-14 — where older text disagrees, these win):
- **Two CTAs, two keywords.** The resource is the Week 2 guide — `memory/magnets.md → ## Current magnet` (keyword
  GUIDE; its funnel URL on description line 2 and in the pinned comment; the spoken line may say "comment GUIDE
  and I'll send it"). The call is the booking link on description line 1, in the law's shape. The second keyword
  is the offer's `Comment keyword:` (`identity/offer.md`); both sit on `identity/publishing.md`'s `Keyword:` line.
  Never a third word.
- **The call ask follows the law** — the person · their help · free · to run the method; never the brokerage as
  the reason, never compensation. Join register (`offer.md → Join-gated package allowed: yes` or `unknown`):
  *"If you want my help running this — the lessons, the templates, the community — partner with me and it's
  free. Grab [the call]."* Open register (`no`): *"I'll walk you through it, free, no strings."* The method
  (`offer.md → ## The framework`) is named every time. The spoken lines, the video pop-ups and the end cards come
  from the Week 3 Kit's chapter 7 CALLS TO ACTION table (the newest `exports/week3-kit-*.txt`), verbatim — never
  a new join line of this plugin's own. **The help named is only what the offer records** (`offer.md → ## Value
  stack`): the offer's `Status:` reads `seeds`, or nothing is recorded there (the member skipped Week 2) → name no
  lessons, templates or community — *"If you want my help with this, it's free. [the booking line]."* — and where
  the method would be named, name the video's own topic.
- **Scalable by design** (the Brain's doctrine §10e). No line in a script, a description or a pinned comment
  promises the member's time per partner — no in-person days beside the member, no chat group, no one-to-one
  calls, no response times. Partners get the recorded lessons, the templates, the community, and the weekly
  group call only when `identity/operations.md` shows the member already runs one.
- **The offer first.** Titles and lanes from `offer.md → ## Content branches`; hook material from `## The
  framework`; the 90-day outcome from `## The program` — what a partner can show by day ninety (the routine
  running, a first step done): a title or an opening may name it as what the video teaches toward, never as a
  promise ("you will…"), never a number of agents, deals, views or dollars — the method is promised, a result
  never is; the story's spine from `identity/journey.md → ## Why join me`. Then the Brain's other signals,
  research third, the starter bank last.
- **Cadence** is the `Cadence:` line of `identity/channel.md → ## Game Plan anchors` — one video a week the
  default for the first month, two the step-up. This chat makes one video regardless.
- **Member-facing words:** Week 5; "your content board", "its card", "the YouTube tab at the top of your board";
  "your thumbnail sheet" (the member makes the thumbnail in Higgsfield), "your title and description" (never "the
  SEO package"), "how it opens" / "three ways to open" (never "the hook"), "your Game Plan", "your three topics" /
  "a topic video", "your pace", "your filming day", "extra footage" (never "b-roll"), "your Channel Page"; never
  lane, niche, bucket, pillar, cadence or packaging in a sentence the member reads (they stay in the Brain's
  rows); the editing next step stated, never hedged, in the words of Step 7 (never "Plugin 5", an engine's name,
  "marketplace" or a "Not installed yet?" question); the short-form system by phrase, and only when `config.md`
  holds a `## Short-Form (Week 4)` block; never a skill id, a file path or a task id in a sentence to the member.

> If the member wants one piece only (just the script, just the title and description, just the thumbnail sheet),
> jump to that skill; never force the whole sequence.

## The three replies — one step, then wait (the owner, 2026-11-15: "it should WAIT for me")
A reply shows what it built in a few lines (the document holds the rest), opens with a true win line when there
is one, and ends on its stop line; nothing of the next reply is read, built, saved or said before the member
answers. "Do it all" runs straight through only when the member says so — never offered.
1. **The direction** (Steps 0–1) — the title · how it opens, three ways, the recommended one first · the story it
   will use · the videos to watch first. Nothing is written or saved before the Yes. It ends: *"Say **Yes** and
   I'll write your script — or tell me what you'd change. **Your turn.**"*
2. **The script** (Step 2) — opens *"✓ You chose your title and how it opens."* Shows the title, the opening line,
   about how many minutes it runs and where it is saved — never the whole script in the chat. It ends: *"Say
   **Yes** and I'll write your thumbnail sheet and your title and description, and put them on your card — or tell
   me what you'd change in your script. **Your turn.**"* (no board: *"Say **Yes** and I'll write your thumbnail
   sheet and your title and description, and add your six publish steps — or tell me what you'd change in your
   script. **Your turn.**"*)
3. **The rest, and the close** (Steps 3–7) — the thumbnail sheet, the title and description, the card, then the
   close (Step 7). Its pieces follow from the script the member has just approved, so they are built together —
   never a wait between them.
A change at a stop → make it, show only what changed, the same stop line again; a question → one plain answer,
then the stop line; silence is never a Yes.
