# SEO Engine — findable by the agents who are already searching

The description is the member's 24/7 sales assistant (`08-youtube/98`): the next step sits above the fold,
convenience converts, keywords make it discoverable. Apply `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/house-rules.md`,
`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/seo-knowledge-base.md` (the re-keyed attraction keyword sets: comparisons · rev
share · switching · sponsor questions · the five pains), and §9–§11 (titles and thumbnails · the two-CTA
model · the bingeworthy channel) of `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/attraction-youtube-doctrine.md`. Obey
`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/brain-contract.md`.

**Week 5 rules that bind every package** (the rewire brief, 2026-11-14 — where older text disagrees, these win):
- **Two CTAs, two keywords, in this order.** Line 1 = the call: the booking link in the law's shape. Line 2 = the
  resource: the Week 2 guide's funnel URL (`memory/magnets.md → ## Current magnet`), with "or comment GUIDE and
  I'll send it". The pinned comment repeats the guide's link and GUIDE. The offer's `Comment keyword:` is the only
  other word; both sit on `identity/publishing.md`'s `Keyword:` line. Never a third word.
- **The call line follows the law** — the person · their help · free · to run the method; never the brokerage as
  the reason, never compensation. Join register (`identity/offer.md → Join-gated package allowed: yes` or
  `unknown`): *"If you want my help running [the method] — the lessons, the templates, the community — partner
  with me and it's free. Book here: [link]"*. Open register (`no`): *"I'll walk you through it, free, no strings:
  [link]"*. The method (`## The framework`) is named. The wording is the Week 3 Kit's chapter 7 CALLS TO ACTION
  table (the newest `exports/week3-kit-*.txt`), verbatim — never a new join line of this plugin's own. **The help
  named is only what the offer records** (`offer.md → ## Value stack`): its `Status:` reads `seeds`, or nothing is
  recorded there (the member skipped Week 2) → name no lessons, templates or community — *"If you want my help
  with this, it's free. Book here: [link]"* — and where the method would be named, name the video's own topic.
- **Scalable by design** (the Brain's doctrine §10e): nothing in a description or a pinned comment promises the
  member's time per partner — no in-person days, no chat group, no one-to-one calls, no response times.
- **Member-facing words:** Week 5; plain and warm; never a skill id, a file path or a task id in a sentence to
  the member. To the member this is always "your title and description" — never "SEO", "the SEO package",
  "packaging", "lane", "retention" (say "how long people keep watching") or "CTR" ("how many people click"). The
  saved document keeps its file name and the card keeps its section heading; the sentences around them use the
  member's words.

> **One chat = one video.** Normally Step 4 of `yt-make-video`. For a video made outside the system, run it
> in that video's chat: ask for the title and a two-line rundown in these words, then go — *"What's the video's
> title, and what is it about in two lines? **Your turn.**"*

## Step 2 — Produce the SEO package (to the member: their title and description)
1. **The title — the member's, and two spares.** The title already chosen (in `yt-make-video`'s first reply, in
   the Kickoff's draft, or on camera when the video is filmed) is printed first, word for word, labelled **YOUR
   TITLE** — never re-chosen here. Under it, two spare titles, each labelled **Spare** — "only if this one is slow
   after a month" (the same rule as a spare thumbnail). In the skeleton: the band YOUR TITLE holds the title; the
   band SPARE TITLES holds the two, numbered. No title chosen yet (a package asked for on its own, nothing scripted) → three
   are written and the recommended one is marked YOUR TITLE, as a statement — never "which do you prefer?". Every
   title: the viewer's own question first; pain or desire named (`97`); the brokerage name
   where the video is about the model; year when freshness matters ("[Brokerage] explained 2027"); about 50–60
   characters and never over 70, the phrase in the first 30. Voice-matched; no clickbait if `voice.md` forbids it.
   The cardinal rules apply to titles: never a negative framing of another brokerage or person, never an income
   or a result promised to the viewer.
2. **Description** — open the Step 2 files now (the guide, the register, the kit's lines, the keywords, the
   contact line; `compliance.md`'s disclosure fields for line 3 and for the block at the end).
   - **First three lines = the two CTAs, warm and inviting (`98`):** **line 1 the call** — the kit's call line in
     the law's shape, the method named, the booking link at its end (join register: *"If you want my help running
     [the method] — the lessons, the templates, the community — partner with me and it's free. Book here:
     [link]"*; open register: *"I'll walk you through it, free, no strings: [link]"*); **line 2 the guide** —
     *"Free guide — [the guide's title]: [funnel URL] (or comment GUIDE and I'll send it)"* (the Starter Resource's
     download link only as the fallback); **line 3 the compliance and contact line** — the brokerage name and
     license display as `compliance.md` requires, then the contact line (the handles, a phone or an email — only
     what is on file); the one order in every file. Above the fold, before any summary.
     Never the brokerage as the reason, never a compensation word, never a line that promises the member's time.
   - A 150–250 word summary in the member's voice with the video's keyword set used naturally (primary 2–3×),
     speaking to the avatar's situation, never "real estate agents" in general; it may close on the YouTube bio's
     one line.
   - **Chapters** — the NAMES from the script's sections, descriptive and searchable ("How the cap works at
     [brokerage]", not "Part 2"). **A time is never estimated from a script.** Before the edit the names sit in
     their own block under the description, headed "CHAPTERS — add after your edit", outside the text to paste.
     **When the member comes back from the edit** (they say 'title and description for my attraction video', or
     they paste the section map) → write the real times beside the names, the first at `00:00`, move the
     block into the description, and replace it in the document and in the card's 🔍 SEO PACKAGE section — in
     place, nothing else changed. No section map ever comes back → the description goes out without chapters;
     nothing wrong is ever pasted.
   - **Related videos** — 2–3 of the member's own (the binge path, `99`), the Explained video for any model
     content, the matching interview for any case-study content. A first video, or none of those published yet →
     the block is left out; never a link to a video that does not exist, and nothing is said about it.
   - Brokerage name, license display, and the rev-share marketing / earnings disclaimer line exactly as
     `compliance.md` requires.
3. **Tags (four to six, specific)** — the exact primary phrase · one variation · the long-tail version · one to
   three secondaries; never broad ("real estate"). Tags matter little; say so once, in those plain words, and
   move on.
4. **Hashtags** — 3–5 from the avatar's search language; the brokerage's name only on a model video.
5. **Pinned comment** — the guide's funnel URL + "comment GUIDE and I'll send it" (the kit's wording), then one
   real question for the viewer to answer (comments are a ranking signal and the start of a conversation). For
   interviews: a line of thanks to the guest by name. Never the brokerage as the reason.
6. **Watch-time extras** — the playlist (this video's topic playlist, **by its exact name** — the lane's row in
   `identity/channel.md`; the Explained playlist first on the channel page — `99`; a playlist not made on the
   channel yet keeps that same name, and step 4 of the publish steps tells the member to create it there),
   end screen = the next logical video + subscribe (**a first video: the playlist alone**), and one YouTube info
   card (the small "i" in the corner of the video) at the point the script points to another video or the
   playlist — never to the guide: a card that links off YouTube needs the YouTube Partner Program (YouTube Help,
   checked 2026-11-21), so the guide's link lives in the description and the pinned comment — **the playlist and
   the end screen are what steps 4 and 5 of the publish steps use, so they are never left vague or left out**; the
   end card's text is the kit's (the guide's title · Comment GUIDE · the stamp, or the call headline · the booking
   line · the stamp).
7. **Why these (for the member)** — three plain sentences: the phrase the title uses and where it was seen · why
   the description opens with the call and then the guide (the next step is the first thing a viewer reads) · that
   the tags are simply the phrases agents type. Never a claim about demand, ranking or views that was not seen;
   never "this will rank".

## Compliance (3-state — never a stop, never a detour)
`identity/compliance.md`'s first line, `Status:` — **`unset`** → the package is still written, delivered and
saved, **stamped "hold until your compliance basics are set"** (the line under its title; line 3 and the
disclosure block keep their place as one bracketed line each, never invented), and ONE line is said at the close:
*"One thing before this goes on a video: say 'paste my compliance link' — two minutes — so your brokerage's wording
is on it."* (inside `yt-make-video`, that chat's close says its own one line and this skill stays silent). **`set`**
→ apply, remind once at the close: *"One thing: check this wording with your brokerage, then say 'confirm my
compliance'."*; **`confirmed`** → apply. Checks, in every state: no
income or rev-share earnings claims, no compensation numbers, no "#1 / best / fastest-growing" without a
dated source, the two cardinal rules in titles and summary, brokerage name + license display, AI-likeness
disclosure if a clone appears in the video, Meta Employment note if any line becomes an ad, nothing that promises
the member's time per partner.
