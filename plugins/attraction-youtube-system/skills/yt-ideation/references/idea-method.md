# Idea Method — internal rubric, cycle balance, packaging, voice

> INTERNAL ONLY. The member never sees the rubric or the scores. They see a short ranked list with a reason each.

**Applies the attraction doctrine** (`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/attraction-youtube-doctrine.md`): §3 the
funnel, §4 the three niche buckets, §5 interviews, §6 model videos, §7 the 8-video cycle, §9 the title
formulas, §14 what the data says to repeat, §15 the cardinal rules. Buckets are tags (Problem · Situation ·
Future · Interview · Model); the pillar a row carries is the Brain's (Authority · Proof · Perspective).
**The offer first** (the rewire brief, 2026-11-14): `identity/offer.md → ## Content branches` is where every lane
and title starts; `## The framework` is hook material; `## The program`'s 90-day outcome is the promise.

## The scoring rubric (rank candidates with this, silently)
| Criterion | Question | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Branch fit | Does it sit on one of the offer's content branches — a step of the method, a pain in the overlap, the story's turning point? (a title off every branch needs a strong signal to stay) | ×3 |
| Demand | Do agents demonstrably search or ask this? (autocomplete, the top videos' views, a recurring comment or objection) | ×3 |
| Attraction value | Does it pull the member's named avatar toward a conversation — does it speak to one of the five pains and point at the guide and the call? | ×2 |
| Gap | Is it underserved, served badly, or missing from the member's own channel? | ×2 |
| Fit | Does it match the known-for, the avatar, and the voice? (a title that breaks the voice fails) | ×1 |
| Timeliness | A dated news item, a season (licensing cycles, year-end brokerage moves), a fresh win to interview | ×1 |
| Hook | How strong is the title/hook on curiosity · emotion · clarity? | ×1 |
Priority = B×3 + D×3 + A×2 + G×2 + F×1 + T×1 + H×1 (max 65). Rank high → low inside each bucket; show only the
order. A `starter` title (from the starter bank) scores Demand only once research confirmed the phrase; otherwise
Demand = 0 and the "why" says it is from the starter arc — it can never outrank a branch topic, a Brain signal or
a researched one.

## Cycle balance (the member never manages it)
Read `identity/channel.md` → `## Game Plan anchors` → `Cadence:` and cycle position, and the content-log's last
eight YouTube rows. The cycle is 3 niche · 1 model · 4 interviews; it holds at either pace (one cycle in eight
weeks at one a week, four at two). **At one a week — the default for the first month — the first four videos run
niche · interview · niche · model**; Why I Switched goes early in cycle one when `journey.md` holds the story. A
batch fills what the cycle is short on; niche picks spread across Problem / Situation / Future; the interview slot
names a real guest from `memory/interview-pipeline.md` (or says "invite [guest]" with the transformation). Never
two model videos in one cycle; never a batch that is all one bucket.

## Packaging-first checklist (every idea, prepared behind the scenes)
- **Title (doctrine §9):** one of the seven formulas where it fits (keep the number for the row), one promise,
  ≤70 characters, the viewer named ("real estate agent", the avatar type) when the search needs it, the year on
  model videos. Curiosity · emotion · clarity; bold and emotional outranks clever.
- **Hook:** the pain point in the first line, in the member's voice; the promise in the second (the framework's
  step or the 90-day outcome — shown, never earned). Written out word for word later by `yt-script` — here one
  line.
- **Thumbnail text:** 3–5 words, **different from the title**; the expression it needs. Surfaces in the video
  chat for `yt-thumbnail`'s build sheet.
- **Bucket → pillar for the eventual row:** Problem / Situation / Future → Authority · Interview → Proof ·
  Model → Perspective.
- **Hard gates:** no compensation figures or earnings implied · no negative word about a brokerage, sponsor, or
  person · former brokerage unnamed · no protected-characteristic targeting · "#1 / fastest-growing" only with a
  dated source · vary the angle at the top of any list (never two titles on the same angle opening the batch).

## Voice-matched titles — good vs bad (a calm, consultative member who never hypes)
- ✅ "Why most new agents quit in year one — and what the ones who make it do differently"
- ✅ "How rev share actually works — the concept, without the sales pitch"
- ✅ "How Priya closed her first three deals part-time — with a database of 40 people"
- ❌ "You're LOSING $10,000s at your brokerage!! (MUST WATCH)" ← hype + a dollar figure = two fails
- ❌ "The REAL reason [Brokerage X] agents are miserable" ← names a brokerage negatively = cardinal rule fail
Earn the click with specificity and a real promise, calibrated to the member's brand.

## Topic memory and coverage (read live — no tracker)
From `memory/content-log.md` (YouTube rows) and the public channel: branches covered vs thin · buckets covered vs
thin · avatars served vs neglected · stories used recently (rotate) · guests already interviewed · model videos
already made this year (the yearly remake is allowed and labeled) · exact topics made (never a duplicate; a new
angle on a winner is encouraged — doctrine §14).

## Sourcing ideas (in this order)
1. The offer's `## Content branches` and the Game Plan's title bank built from them — the next titles on the cycle.
2. The member's captured ideas (`memory/ideas.md`, tags `youtube` / `interview`, Status open).
3. What agents keep asking: `memory/objections.md`, the member's pasted comments, `memory/intel.md` (dated).
4. The template library (`shared/idea-templates.md`), filled with the member's real avatar, niche, model, guests.
5. Fresh signals from `yt-research` (what agents search) and `yt-outliers` (what worked elsewhere) — third, after
   the Brain.
6. Coverage gaps from the read above.
7. The starter bank (`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/yt-gameplan/references/starter-title-bank.md`) — last, and only
   for a slot 1–6 left empty; marked `starter`; never ranked above a branch topic, a Brain signal or a researched one.

## Every idea must justify itself (the moat)
Each idea's "why" cites at least one **real** signal, named by type:
- 🧭 **Branch** — a segment of the member's own offer (a framework step, a pain in the overlap, the turning point)
- 📈 **Demand** — autocomplete shows agents typing it; the top videos on it have real views (approximate, seen)
- 🔥 **Timely** — a dated industry or brokerage item from `intel.md` or a sourced article (facts only; `title ·
  outlet · date · link`)
- ❓ **Asked** — a recurring objection, comment, or question the member actually heard (`objections.md`, their paste)
- 👀 **Proven** — an outlier video on the same concept (what worked, never what was wrong with it)
- 🤝 **Proof ready** — a guest at Candidate or later with a real transformation
- 🗺️ **Gap** — a branch, bucket, avatar, or question the member's channel has not covered
- 📚 **Starter** — from the starter bank (the agency's four-month arc, compliance-cleaned): agency-curated, not a
  signal for this member — the "why" says so plainly; it becomes Demand only once research confirms it
Lead with the strongest real signal. Never fabricate search volumes or view counts; if no number was seen, say
"agents search this" without one.

## Output rules
- The count proposed in Step 1 from the cadence (default four at one a week — a month), ranked inside the cycle
  balance. Mix timely with evergreen.
- GENERATE the full package per idea (title · hook · thumbnail text · bucket · branch · avatar · pain · signal ·
  formula), PRESENT only two lines each. Expand only on request.
- No scores, no rubric, no file names in what the member sees. Plain, warm, short.
- Always end with the handoff: pick one → new chat → "make my attraction video: [title]."
