# Script Studio — full mode (the default and only mode): ONE video, both forms, in the member's voice, ready to read

Apply `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/house-rules.md` — the doctrine (#1), the Brain (#2), the 3-state gate (#3),
voice (#5), sourcing (#6), docs (#7). The Brain Contract (`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/brain-contract.md`):
this skill writes the **content-log row at Scripted**, stamps a story's **Used-where**, and nothing else.

**Lazy-load:** `references/format-playbooks.md` (the four formats; an interview has no script) at Step 2;
`references/script-format.md` (layout, conventions, the two CTAs on the page) at Step 3; doctrine §8 (structure)
and §10 (the two CTAs) only if a line needs re-grounding. Never the whole doctrine.

**Week 5 rules that bind every script** (the rewire brief, 2026-11-14 — where older text disagrees, these win):
- **Two CTAs, two keywords.** The resource is the Week 2 guide (`memory/magnets.md → ## Current magnet`; keyword
  GUIDE; "comment GUIDE and I'll send it" or "link in the description"). The call is the booking link, in the
  law's shape. The offer's `Comment keyword:` is the second word; both sit on `identity/publishing.md`'s
  `Keyword:` line. Never a third word in a script.
- **The call ask follows the law** — the person · their help · free · to run the method; never the brokerage as
  the reason, never compensation. Join register (`identity/offer.md → Join-gated package allowed: yes` or
  `unknown`): *"If you want my help running this — the lessons, the templates, the community — partner with me
  and it's free. Grab [the call]."* Open register (`no`): *"I'll walk you through it, free, no strings."* The
  method (`## The framework`) is named every time. The spoken resource line and the spoken call line are the
  Week 3 Kit's chapter 7 CALLS TO ACTION table (the newest `exports/week3-kit-*.txt`), verbatim, said in the
  member's cadence — never a new join line of this plugin's own. **The help named is only what the offer
  records** (`offer.md → ## Value stack`): the offer's `Status:` reads `seeds`, or nothing is recorded there (the
  member skipped Week 2) → name no lessons, templates or community — *"If you want my help with this, it's free.
  [the booking line]."* — and where the method would be named, name the video's own topic.
- **Scalable by design** (the Brain's doctrine §10e). No line promises the member's time per partner — no
  in-person days beside the member, no chat group, no one-to-one calls, no "call me any time". What partners
  get: the recorded lessons, the templates, the community, the weekly group call only when
  `identity/operations.md` shows the member already runs one. Mike's own CTA phrasings are adapted into those
  assets. The member's OWN filming routine is theirs to show.
- **The whole method.** A niche video teaches one step of `offer.md → ## The framework` (or one page of the
  guide) in full and names the guide as where the rest lives; the 90-day outcome (`## The program`) is what a
  partner can show by day ninety (the routine running, a first step done) — an opening may name it as what the
  video teaches toward, never as a promise ("you will…"), never a number of agents, deals, views or dollars: the
  method is promised, a result never is.
- **True for a member with nothing.** No agent, no result and no testimonial is ever written into a script unless
  the Brain holds that person with consent. No banked story that fits → the member's own years as an agent
  (`journey.md`), or a line straight to the viewer ("If you're the agent who…") — never a made-up agent, client,
  name, number or win, never "one of my agents" as a figure of speech. No proof on file → no proof line; the step
  being taught carries the section.
- **Member-facing words:** Week 5; plain and warm; never a skill id or a file path in a sentence to the member;
  "your opening" / "how it opens" (never "the hook"), "extra footage" (never "b-roll"), "your thumbnail sheet",
  "your title and description", "your Game Plan", "a topic video"; never lane, bucket, pillar or packaging in a
  line or a document the member reads; the editor is "your AI Editing Studio" — never "Plugin 5", an engine's
  name or "marketplace".

> summaries). The older phrase **"script my first three videos" is retired as a path and never gets a question**: it
> scripts the FIRST video of the plan in full — the next `Idea` row of `memory/content-calendar.md → ## Long-form
> (YouTube)` that is not an interview with no guest named — and the reply says one line in its place, by
> `config.md → ## YouTube (Week 5) → Monday Kickoff task:`: a task id → *"I write one video at a time now, in full —
> this is your first, '[title]'. Your Monday Kickoff brings the next one, written, each Monday."*; anything else →

## Step 1b — No question before a script (the owner, 2026-11-16)
*"We don't really need more questions because they've already gone through a lot of this when they were building
their AI brain."* A script is written from the member's Brain — their voice, their story, their method — with
nothing asked first. Two things are read for the member's own words:
- **`identity/channel.md → ## In your words`** — an entry that holds the member's own WORDING for this video (a
  line they gave) opens the script (its YOUR OPENING band), and the script's top line says **Built on your
  words**. A topic pick, a filming day or a `skipped` entry is not wording and is never quoted.
- **The member's own wording is always welcome, never required.** The reply carries ONE line about it, a
  statement, never a question and never a task: *"Your own opening line always wins — say it any time and I'll
  rewrite the script around it."* When they do, their sentence opens the script (tidied only to be sayable —
  never a claim added; a named brokerage and any income figure left out, said once) and is appended to `## In
  your words` (`- **[title]** (YYYY-MM-DD): "[verbatim]" → [title]`, pushed at Step 7).

## Step 3 — Write the whole video: two forms, one document (`references/script-format.md` + doctrine §8)
**Form (a) THE BREAKDOWN** — one page the member can film from: the opening written word for word (the scripted
hook), then each section as bullets (what it covers · the point · the move), the resource line, the call line, the
payoff, the next video. **Form (b) THE SCRIPT** — the full teleprompter script, 8 to 12 minutes, every line sayable.
Both are written every time, in this order, in the one `Script` document — never one without the other, never a
summary in place of the script (Mike, 2026-11-23: "fully broken down"). The structure both forms follow:
- **THE OPENING (0:00–0:15/0:30 — printed as the band YOUR OPENING; "hook" is never printed)** — the pain point
  in the first ten seconds, **written word for word** (`/94`): call
  out the viewer's situation → the tension or question → what they'll have by the end. Never "welcome back," a
  long intro, or a credentials dump. It matches the title and thumbnail's promise.
- **THE GUIDE ASK (inside minute one)** — the kit's spoken resource line, in the member's cadence, naming the live
  guide from `## Current magnet`: *"[what the guide holds, one clause]. Comment GUIDE and I'll send it"* — or
  "link in the description". Section still empty (Week 2 skipped) → name the Starter Resource, and it is built
  before filming, on a Yes — never on its own: `yt-make-video` proposes it in its first reply and builds it on
  that Yes; a script asked for on its own says one line (*"You don't have a guide yet, so this script points to a
  one-page starter, 'Questions to Ask Before You Choose a Sponsor' — I write it next. Your own guide replaces it
  whenever you say 'launch my lead magnet plugin'."* — ending *"…(install the Lead Magnet plugin first, from the
  same place you installed this one)."* only when no Lead Magnet skill is in this session's skill list) and
  `yt-leads` builds it first on the Yes of the Hand-off's stop line. Never the call as the resource — the call has its own ask below. A script never ships promising a
  resource that does not exist.
- **VALUE** — 3–5 clear sections; each answers *what they need to know · why it matters · what to do*; one step of
  the member's framework taught in full, tactical enough to use today, the guide named as where the rest lives; a
  real story woven where it lands, and an agent's win only when `proof.md` holds one with consent (`/93`, draft:
  "this is legit") — neither on file → the member's own experience as an agent, never an invented one; bullets
  for lists; no tangents. Model scripts: mechanics and fit, never figures.
- **THE CALL ASK (~a third to halfway in)** — the kit's spoken call line in the law's shape, the method named:
  join register *"If you want my help running [the method] — the lessons, the templates, the community —
  partner with me and it's free. [the booking line]."*; open register *"I'll walk you through it, free, no
  strings. [the booking line]."* Never the brokerage as the pitch (`/94`, `/98`); never a promise of the
  member's time per partner. The same words every video — the kit decides them; vary only the sentence that
  leads into it.
- **PAYOFF** — deliver the promise; the reassurance the avatar came for.
- **NEXT VIDEO (the very end)** — a light repeat of the call ask is fine, never a third pitch; then the next
  logical video on the channel (an interview on the same pain, the model explained, the lane's playlist) — the
  last thing said (`/99`).
- **CHAPTERS** — the section names with PLANNED times (they feed the title and description as chapter names;
  the real times come from the edit — `yt-seo` writes them in then, never before).
- **POP-UPS AND END CARD** — the kit's rows, verbatim: the name card in the first three seconds; the guide pop-up
  (`Comment GUIDE · free guide`) beside the guide ask; the call pop-up (`My help, free · [the call in three
  words]`) beside the call ask; the end card in the last five seconds (the guide's title · Comment GUIDE · the
  stamp — or the call headline · the booking line · the stamp). The designed set sits in the member's Offer
  folder (Week 3).
- Cues: `[brackets]` = delivery / extra footage · `>>` = on-screen text. A one-line legend is printed under the
  script's title so the member never reads a cue aloud (`references/script-format.md`); sources are never inside
  the spoken lines — they go in the footer.

**Length (Mike, 2026-11-23): 8 to 12 minutes, every format** — ~140 spoken words a minute, so about 1,100 to 1,700
words; a Model Breakdown sits at the top of the range, never past it. Write the word count to fit; the breakdown is
one page. Value-dense; cut filler; easy to *speak*.

## Step 4 — Voice (critical)
Write for the ear in their spoken cadence: their sentence length, their verbatim signature phrases, their
energy; honor every never-say and hard-avoid. Read it aloud as them — a line that sounds salesy, hyped, or
un-sayable in their mouth gets rewritten (the two CTA lines stay the kit's words; only the lead-in bends).
**Stories:** use a banked story in the open or the close. None that fits, or an empty bank → the member's own
years as an agent from `journey.md`, or a line straight to the viewer — an example is never invented and never
told as if it happened. Anonymize agents and clients unless consent is on file; **stamp its Used-where** in
`story-bank.md`. A new story the member tells
in chat → use it, then say in one line how it is kept — stated, never offered: *"That story is worth keeping —
say 'add an attraction story' and it goes in your story bank."* (`attraction-story-bank` banks it on that
phrase). **Ship it complete — no blanks, no homework:**
never `[add your story]`; every line filmable as written.

## Step 5 — The first Short (the moment, not the Reel)
Name the strongest 30–45 s moment — the pain in line one → one point → the ask with one of the two keywords —
with its timestamps. The editor cuts a clip only when asked, so the band says, in these words: *"After the edit,
say 'cut reels from this' and your AI Editing Studio cuts this moment."* The Reels themselves come later, from the
clip queue: after the video is out, *"say 'repurpose my attraction video'"* writes three clip scripts and queues
them for the week's Reels. Nothing more is written here.

## Step 6 — Compliance + fact-check (before saving)
1. Every stat, quote, brokerage fact, or agent result is sourced `(title · outlet · date · link)` or left out —
   never flagged for the member to check; no invented numbers, stories or people; no testimonial without consent.
2. The gate, applied: cardinal rules (no negative word about a brokerage or person) · no compensation figures,
   rev-share tiers, stock numbers, or income · no earnings implication, and no figure even as a labeled example ·
   the former brokerage unnamed · brokerage name and license where
   `compliance.md` requires them (the description block, not the script) · AI-likeness disclosure if a clone
   will read it · recruiting scope respected in the CTA · **the scalable check:** no line promises the member's
   time per partner. Fix anything flagged; never ship it.
