# Script Studio — the video, in the member's voice, ready to read

Apply `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/house-rules.md` — the doctrine (#1), the Brain (#2), the 3-state gate (#3),
voice (#5), sourcing (#6), docs (#7). The Brain Contract (`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/brain-contract.md`):
this skill writes the **content-log row at Scripted**, stamps a story's **Used-where**, and nothing else.

**Lazy-load:** `references/format-playbooks.md` (the four formats + interview steps) at Step 2;
`references/script-format.md` (layout, conventions, the two CTAs on the page) at Step 3; doctrine §8 (structure)
and §10 (the two CTAs) only if a line needs re-grounding. Never the whole doctrine.

**Week 5 rules that bind every script** (the rewire brief, 2026-11-14 — where older text disagrees, these win):
- **Two CTAs, two keywords.** The resource is the Week 2 guide (`memory/magnets.md → ## Current magnet`; keyword
  GUIDE; "comment GUIDE and I'll send it" or "link in the description"). The call is the booking link, in the
  law's shape. The offer's `Comment keyword:` is the second word; both sit on `identity/publishing.md`'s
  `Keyword:` line. Never a third word in a script.
- **The call ask follows the law** — the person · their help · free · to run the method; never the brokerage as
  the reason, never compensation. Join register (`identity/offer.md → Join-gated package allowed: yes` or
  `unknown`): *"If you want my help running this — the lessons, the templates, the community — partner with me
  and it's free. Grab [the call]."* Open register (`no`): *"I'll walk you through it, free, no strings."* The
  method (`## The framework`) is named every time. The spoken resource line and the spoken call line are the
  Week 3 Kit's chapter 7 CALLS TO ACTION table (the newest `exports/week3-kit-*.txt`), verbatim, said in the
  member's cadence — never a new join line of this plugin's own.
- **Scalable by design** (the Brain's doctrine §10e). No line promises the member's time per partner — no
  in-person days beside the member, no chat group, no one-to-one calls, no "call me any time". What partners
  get: the recorded lessons, the templates, the community, the weekly group call only when
  `identity/operations.md` shows the member already runs one. Mike's own CTA phrasings are adapted into those
  assets. The member's OWN filming routine is theirs to show.
- **The whole method.** A niche video teaches one step of `offer.md → ## The framework` (or one page of the
  guide) in full and names the guide as where the rest lives; the 90-day outcome (`## The program`) is what the
  hook can promise — shown, never earned.
- **Member-facing words:** Week 5; plain and warm; never a skill id or a file path in a sentence to the member.

> **One chat = one video.** Normally Step 2 of make-video. A member can also come straight here with their own
> idea ("script my why I switched video") — this chat becomes the video's chat. Gather what is missing, plainly.
> **One exception, and only this one: the first batch** ("script my first three videos" — the section near the end):
> three scripts in one sitting, so a new member can film a first batch in one day.

## Step 1 — Gather (read; never re-ask)
- **The idea package** — from ideation / make-video (title · bucket · hook · avatar · pain · signal · thumbnail
  text) or the member's own. If their own: shape the title and angle with them in one exchange, assign the
  bucket (Problem · Situation · Future · Interview · Model), then write.
- **The Brain:** `brain.md`, then only three more files now — the rest open at Step 2 once the format is chosen:
  `identity/voice.md` (tone, hard-avoids) **and `voice-print.md`** (spoken cadence, signature phrases, never-say —
  the primary reference for a read-aloud script; empty = proceed on `voice.md`, never invent a personality) ·
  `identity/compliance.md` (the gate below — its first line, `Status:`).
  **Opened at Step 2, by the format's Pull list:** `avatars.md` (the viewer, their pain in their words) ·
  `identity/offer.md` (the header: `Join-gated package allowed:` — the register of the call ask; `Comment
  keyword:`; `## The framework` — the step this video teaches; `## Content branches` — the branch it serves;
  `## The program` — the 90-day outcome; `seeds` → Week 2 builds the offer, never demand it) ·
  `memory/magnets.md → ## Current magnet` (the live guide: its title, GUIDE, the funnel URL — or, only if the
  member skipped Week 2, the Starter Resource `yt-leads` builds once while the section is empty) · the newest
  `exports/week3-kit-*.txt` → chapter 7's CALLS TO ACTION table (the spoken lines, the pop-ups, the end cards —
  quoted, never rewritten) · `identity/channel.md` → the CTA line (it mirrors the kit) and the booking link ·
  `identity/publishing.md` → `Keyword:` (the two words) · `story-bank.md` (stories tagged to this pain or moment —
  rotate; check `content-log.md` for recent use) · `proof.md` (real lines only, consent respected) ·
  `journey.md` (Why I Switched only — the wall, never the company; `## Why join me` is the spine) ·
  `brokerage-model.md` (Model Breakdown only — mechanics; empty → *"say 'explain my model to me' first so the
  breakdown is accurate"* → `attraction-brokerage-model`, stop) · `memory/objections.md` (the objection this
  video answers) · `memory/intel.md` (a dated fact to cite, if relevant).
- **Research facts** — only sourced ones from the Brief or the member (`title · outlet · date · link`); anything
  else is `[double-check before filming]`, never a guess.
- **Compliance, 3-state (`identity/compliance.md` — read at Step 1, its first line `Status:`) — never a stop,
  never a detour:** a script is public, so **unset → write it anyway**, deliver and save it **stamped "hold until
  your compliance basics are set"** (the line under the script's title; the brokerage's wording never invented),
  and say ONE line at the close of the chat: *"One thing before you film this: say 'paste my compliance link' — two
  minutes — so your brokerage's wording is on it."* (inside `yt-make-video`, that chat's close says its own one
  line and this skill stays silent). set → write, apply, remind once at the close. confirmed → write, apply.
- Missing local Brain → `attraction-brain-sync` first; a tool error is never "no Brain."

## Step 2 — Choose the format (`references/format-playbooks.md`)
Open the chosen format's **Pull** files now (the Step 1 list) — only that format's.
Bucket → format: Situation with the member's own story → **Why I Switched** · Problem / Situation → **Pain Point
Series** · Model → **Model Breakdown** · Problem / Future deep teach → **Niche Breakdown** · Interview → the
**intro (recorded last) + outro + the casual call ask** (the question map and the guest's prep belong to
`yt-interview`). Each playbook fixes the steps, the length, the hook formulas that fit, the proof and story
pulls, and the lines that never appear.

## Step 3 — Write the long-form script (`references/script-format.md` + doctrine §8)
- **HOOK (0:00–0:15/0:30)** — the pain point in the first ten seconds, **written word for word** (`/94`): call
  out the viewer's situation → the tension or question → what they'll have by the end. Never "welcome back," a
  long intro, or a credentials dump. It matches the title and thumbnail's promise.
- **THE GUIDE ASK (inside minute one)** — the kit's spoken resource line, in the member's cadence, naming the live
  guide from `## Current magnet`: *"[what the guide holds, one clause]. Comment GUIDE and I'll send it"* — or
  "link in the description". Section still empty (Week 2 skipped) → name the Starter Resource and make its build
  the next step before filming (`yt-make-video` runs it at Step 1; a standalone script chat says *"next I build
  your starter resource — two minutes"* → `yt-leads`). Never the call as the resource — the call has its own
  ask below. A script never ships promising a resource that does not exist.
- **VALUE** — 3–5 clear sections; each answers *what they need to know · why it matters · what to do*; one step of
  the member's framework taught in full, tactical enough to use today, the guide named as where the rest lives; a
  real story or an agent's win woven where it lands (`/93`, draft: "this is legit"); bullets for lists; no
  tangents. Model scripts: mechanics and fit, never figures.
- **THE CALL ASK (~a third to halfway in)** — the kit's spoken call line in the law's shape, the method named:
  join register *"If you want my help running [the method] — the lessons, the templates, the community —
  partner with me and it's free. [the booking line]."*; open register *"I'll walk you through it, free, no
  strings. [the booking line]."* Never the brokerage as the pitch (`/94`, `/98`); never a promise of the
  member's time per partner. The same words every video — the kit decides them; vary only the sentence that
  leads into it.
- **PAYOFF** — deliver the promise; the reassurance the avatar came for.
- **NEXT VIDEO (the very end)** — a light repeat of the call ask is fine, never a third pitch; then the next
  logical video on the channel (an interview on the same pain, the model explained, the lane's playlist) — the
  last thing said (`/99`).
- **CHAPTERS** — timestamped from the sections (they feed the SEO package).
- **POP-UPS AND END CARD** — the kit's rows, verbatim: the name card in the first three seconds; the guide pop-up
  (`Comment GUIDE · free guide`) beside the guide ask; the call pop-up (`My help, free · [the call in three
  words]`) beside the call ask; the end card in the last five seconds (the guide's title · Comment GUIDE · the
  stamp — or the call headline · the booking line · the stamp). The designed set sits in the member's Offer
  folder (Week 3).
- Cues: `[brackets]` = delivery / b-roll · `>>` = on-screen text · `(source, date)` = not read aloud.

**Length:** ~140 spoken words per minute. Default 10–15 minutes (`/94`); Why I Switched 8–12; Model Breakdown
12–20; Niche Breakdown 10–20; interview intro 45–75 seconds, outro 30–45. Write the word count to fit.
Value-dense; cut filler; easy to *speak*.

## Step 4 — Voice (critical)
Write for the ear in their spoken cadence: their sentence length, their verbatim signature phrases, their
energy; honor every never-say and hard-avoid. Read it aloud as them — a line that sounds salesy, hyped, or
un-sayable in their mouth gets rewritten (the two CTA lines stay the kit's words; only the lead-in bends).
**Stories:** use a banked story in the open or the close before any example you would invent; anonymize agents
and clients unless consent is on file; **stamp its Used-where** in `story-bank.md`. A new story the member tells
in chat → use it, then say in one line how it is kept — stated, never offered: *"That story is worth keeping —
say 'add an attraction story' and it goes in your story bank."* (`attraction-story-bank` banks it on that
phrase). **Ship it complete — no blanks, no homework:**
never `[add your story]`; every line filmable as written.

## Step 5 — The first Short (the moment, not the Reel)
Name the strongest 30–45 s moment — the pain in line one → one point → the ask with one of the two keywords —
with its timestamps, so your AI Editing Studio cuts it at the edit. The Reels themselves come later, from the clip queue: after the video is out, *"say 'repurpose my attraction
video'"* writes three clip scripts and queues them for the week's Reels. Nothing more is written here.

## Step 6 — Compliance + fact-check (before saving)
1. Every stat, quote, brokerage fact, or agent result is sourced `(title · outlet · date · link)` or flagged
   `[double-check before filming]`; no invented numbers; no testimonial without consent.
2. The gate, applied: cardinal rules (no negative word about a brokerage or person) · no compensation figures,
   rev-share tiers, stock numbers, or income · no earnings implication (any unavoidable figure is illustrative,
   labeled, with the disclaimer) · the former brokerage unnamed · brokerage name and license where
   `compliance.md` requires them (the description block, not the script) · AI-likeness disclosure if a clone
   will read it · recruiting scope respected in the CTA · **the scalable check:** no line promises the member's
   time per partner. Fix anything flagged; never ship it.

## Step 7 — Save, log, push (write → push → verify)
1. The video folder `03 · Content/Long-Form/YYYY-MM-DD · [Title]/` (resolve per
   `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/yt-setup/references/drive-structure.md`; create only now). Render on the Script
   skeleton (`references/script-format.md` → `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/doc-format.md`) via `render_doc.py`;
   upload as **`Script`**. Confirm plainly: *"Saved under Content → Long-Form → [video] → Script."*
2. **The content-log row at Scripted** (`memory/content-log.md`, the locked shape): Date · Platform `YouTube` ·
   Format `long-form` (or `interview`) · **Pillar** = Authority (Problem / Situation / Future), Proof
   (Interview), or Perspective (Model) · Topic / hook = `[bucket] final title` · Avatar · Story used · CTA =
   `resource: [the guide's name] · call` · Status `Scripted` · Link `—`. If `yt-interview` or
   `yt-model-breakdown` already wrote this video's row at `Idea`, or the Monday Kickoff wrote it at `Scripted`,
   **update that row** — never a second one. A captured idea the member brought straight here → mark its
   `ideas.md` row used now (make-video does this otherwise). When they have the board, the script goes into the
   card's 🎬 SCRIPT section (make-video Step 6 writes the card; replaced in place, never stacked).
3. Stamp the story's Used-where. Push via `attraction-brain-sync`; verify. Save fails → say it is not saved,
   keep the script visible, retry once, stop.

## The first batch — "script my first three videos" (three scripts in one sitting; the owner, 2026-11-15)
**Why it exists:** a member who has just finished setup films best in one go — camera out once, three videos
recorded, three weeks covered. This writes the three scripts, and only the scripts; each video's own chat still
does the rest. Told, never asked: no "which three?", no "ready?".
1. **Which three — the next three the member films ALONE.** Read `memory/content-calendar.md → ## Long-form
   (YouTube)`: the rows at `Idea`, or at `Scripted` with only a script start, in date order, **skipping interviews**
   (a guest is booked, never batched). A new channel's trailer counts as one of the three. A model breakdown whose
   facts are not on file (`identity/brokerage-model.md` not built) is skipped for the next solo video, with one
   plain line. Fewer than three solo videos on the plan → script what there is and say the count. No plan yet →
   one line: *"Your Game Plan comes first — say 'set up my YouTube for agents'."* Say the three in one line:
   *"Your first three to film on your own: '[A]', '[B]' and '[C]'. Your interviews are booked separately."*
2. **Read once, write three.** Step 1's gathering runs ONCE for all three (the Brain files are opened one time).
   No web research here — the scripts are built from the member's own method, stories and proof; each card already
   carries its reference video for the member to watch first. Then Steps 2 to 6 for each script, in date order:
   its own format by bucket, **a different story from the bank for each** (each stamped Used-where), **three
   different ways of opening** (never the same hook shape twice in the batch), the two CTAs in the kit's words in
   every script, the compliance and fact check on each. The trailer is 60 to 90 seconds; the others the usual
   length. In the chat, show each script's title, its opening line and its length in minutes — never three full
   scripts in the chat; the documents hold them.
3. **Save each one where its video lives** (Step 7, three times, ONE push at the end): the `Script` document in
   each video's own folder; the content-log row at `Scripted` (update, never a second row); with a board, each
   card's 🎬 SCRIPT section — the full script, labelled *"full script — written in your first batch"* — replacing a
   script start, and the card's Status → `Scripted`; each row of the calendar → `Scripted`. **The filming day:**
   set the three rows' and cards' Recording Date to ONE day — the first of the three's own recording day (the
   plan's date, never today and never sooner than three days out) — and leave every Publishing Date as the plan
   wrote it, one a week. A row or a card the member has already moved keeps the member's date.
4. **The film-day sheet** — one short document, `First Batch — Film Day — YYYY-MM-DD`, in `03 · Content/Long-Form/`
   (the skeleton is in `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/doc-format.md`), and the same lines in the chat:
   the day · the three videos in filming order (the trailer last — the member is warmed up by then) with each
   one's length · **before you record** — the camera at eye level · face a window · a quiet room, or the
   microphone you already have · the script in large type beside the lens · a ten-second test, watched back ·
   **between videos** — change your top, so three weeks of videos do not look like one afternoon · **after each
   video** — five photos of your face for the thumbnail: a big smile, surprised, serious, pointing left, pointing
   right. Nothing in it promises anyone the member's time.
5. **The close — three numbered lines, and nothing after them:**
   1. *"Watch the video on each card first — one real example for each of your three."* (left out with no board)
   2. *"Film all three on [the day, in words]. Say 'block my attraction filming day' and it goes on your calendar."*
   3. *"After filming, one video at a time: open a new chat and say 'make my attraction video: [title]' — your
      script is already written, so I add your thumbnail sheet, your title and description, and your publish
      checklist. Then 'edit my YouTube video'."*
   A save that failed is said plainly, with what was not saved. Compliance `unset` → the scripts are written and
   stamped "hold until your compliance basics are set", and the one line is said once, last.
**After the first batch it is one chat per video again** — the phrase works once the plan has three solo videos
unscripted (a later month's batch is the same three-script sitting, never more than three).
