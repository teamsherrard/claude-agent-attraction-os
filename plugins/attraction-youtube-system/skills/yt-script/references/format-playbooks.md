# Format Playbooks — the four attraction formats + the interview intro and outro

Each playbook fixes the steps, the length, the hook formulas that fit (doctrine §9, numbered 1–7), what to pull
from the Brain, and the lines that never appear. The page layout is `script-format.md`; the structure every
format sits inside is doctrine §8 (hook → the guide ask → value → the call ask → payoff → next video). The vault
does not name these four formats — the cohort plan does; their content is grounded in `08-youtube/92, 93 (draft),
94, 96` (doctrine §12, §17).

**Every format, every time (the rewire brief, 2026-11-14):** the guide ask inside minute one names the Week 2
guide (`memory/magnets.md → ## Current magnet`, GUIDE); the call ask is the kit's spoken call line in the law's
shape — join register *"If you want my help running this — the lessons, the templates, the community — partner
with me and it's free. Grab [the call]."*, open register (`Join-gated package allowed: no`) *"I'll walk you
through it, free, no strings."* — the method named, the brokerage never the reason, compensation never; nothing
promises the member's time per partner (no in-person days, no chat group, no one-to-one calls); the whole method
is the guide's — the video teaches one step of `offer.md → ## The framework` in full and names the guide as where
the rest lives; the 90-day outcome (`## The program`) is shown, never earned.

---

## 1. Why I Switched  (bucket: Situation · pillar in the log: Authority, Story angle · 8–12 min)
*The video that brought Mike his first 30 agents in 30 days (`/92`). The member's own move, told as the wall
they hit — never as the company they left.*
- **Pull:** `journey.md` (the three turning points: where they started · the hardest stretch · the turning point;
  the `## Why join me` block — the Week 2 spine of this video) · `story-bank.md` (the moment they almost quit,
  the first win after) · `proof.md` (what changed, with real lines) · `offer.md` (`## The program` — what they
  now give agents: the recorded lessons, the templates, the community; `## The framework` — the method they
  built) · `avatars.md` (who this is for — the agent standing where they stood).
- **Steps:** the wall (the specific frustration — one of the five pains, in their words) → what they went looking
  for (support, a path past "sell more houses," modern systems, community) → the decision and the fear around it
  → what actually changed since (habits, the method, results they can state honestly) → what they would tell
  themselves a year earlier → the call ask.
- **Hooks that fit:** formula 3 ("the truth about changing brokerages no one tells you"), formula 7 ("if you're
  [situation], here's what I did"), or the straight story hook ("Two years ago I almost quit real estate. Here's
  what I changed instead.").
- **Never:** the former brokerage's name ("a franchise," "an independent," "a team"); a negative word about it or
  anyone there; compensation figures, rev-share, stock, or income as the reason; the new brokerage as the pitch
  (it appears where `compliance.md` requires, in the description). The reason is the problem solved, not the
  company. Re-title yearly so it stays current (`/92`).
- **Close:** the call ask is the natural end of the story — the kit's line, led in by the story: *"if any of this
  sounds like where you are — if you want my help running [the method], the lessons, the templates, the
  community, partner with me and it's free. [the booking line]."* (open register: *"I'll walk you through it,
  free, no strings."*).

## 2. Pain Point Series  (bucket: Problem or Situation · Authority · 10–15 min)
*One pain, one avatar, one tactical answer they can use today. "Every challenge an agent names is a video" (`/94`).*
- **Pull:** `avatars.md` (the pain in their words; what they've tried) · `objections.md` (the question this
  answers; the hidden fear) · `offer.md` (`## The framework` — the step that fixes this pain, taught in full;
  `## Content branches` — the branch this video serves) · `strategy.md` (the member's actual method) ·
  `story-bank.md` (a story that proves the point) · `proof.md` (an agent who did it, with consent) · the guide
  (`## Current magnet` — the page the fix lives on).
- **Steps:** name the pain and the agent who has it → why it keeps happening (the honest diagnosis, no blame)
  → the 3–5 moves — one framework step, each move with what · why · how → the story or the agent's win that
  shows it works → the one thing to do this week → the call ask.
- **Hooks that fit:** formula 1 ("why most agents can't… and how to be the exception"), formula 2 ("the N
  mistakes keeping agents stuck at…"), formula 7 ("N signs you've outgrown…", "if you're a struggling agent, do
  this for the next 90 days").
- **One step in full, the guide for the rest** (`/91`, the Brain's doctrine §10e): the video teaches this step
  completely — nothing held back on it — and names the guide as the whole blueprint; the call ask names what
  partnering adds (the recorded lessons, the templates, the community), never the member's time.
- **Never:** a brokerage or person blamed for the pain; income promises; a protected characteristic in who it's
  for.

## 3. Model Breakdown  (bucket: Model · Perspective · 12–20 min)
*Pull back the curtain, simplify what others overcomplicate, answer what they're already researching (`/96`,
`/93` draft). Requires `brokerage-model.md`; empty → route to `attraction-brokerage-model` first.*
- **Pull:** `brokerage-model.md` (mechanics as the member's materials state them, dated) · the Brain plugin's
  brokerage-models reference · `memory/intel.md` (dated changes, each `title · outlet · date · link`) ·
  `objections.md` (the myths and fears agents raise) · `positioning.md` (positioned, not pitched) · `offer.md`
  (`## The program` — the member's own lessons, templates and community that fill the model's gaps).
- **Steps (Mike's structure, `/96`):** overview and positioning (brief history, who it's built for) → how
  compensation is *structured* (the categories — splits, caps, fees — as concepts, **no figures**) → tools and
  technology (what's included; the gaps — and how the member's program fills them) → support and training (the
  brokerage's, then the member's recorded lessons, templates and community — never a promise of the member's
  time) → opportunities beyond closings (rev share, equity, leadership — the concepts, what they are for, **no
  numbers**) → culture and community (what it actually feels like) → fit: who this is for, who it is not for →
  the call ask in the law's shape, with the one honest addition: *"the actual numbers for your situation — we
  go through those on the call."*
- **Variants:** "[Model] explained ([year])" · one component explained (co-sponsorship, how rev share actually
  works — the concept) · myths and misconceptions · fit by avatar (new agents, team leaders, broker-owners) ·
  "how to choose a sponsor / before choosing a sponsor, ask these questions" · **"Do NOT join [brokerage] if…"**
  (Mike's best performer — about fit, honest, never a competitor named) · the yearly "N months in" review.
- **Hooks that fit:** formula 6 ("… explained," "should you join…," "how … actually works"), formula 7 ("do NOT
  join … if…"), formula 3 ("the truth about … no one tells you").
- **Comparisons ("[brokerage] vs [brokerage]")** — only on the member's explicit choice, built by
  `yt-model-breakdown` with Mike's warning repeated (`/96`: he took his own down; not recommended); facts only,
  dated and sourced, strengths and weaknesses of each model stated fairly, no negative word about a brokerage,
  sponsor, or person; compliance-gated.
- **Never:** splits, caps, fees, tiers, percentages, stock amounts, income or projections in the script; "#1 /
  fastest-growing" without a dated source; any disparagement; the brokerage name as the CTA. The disclosure block
  goes in the description (`yt-seo`). Remake or re-title yearly (`/96`).

## 4. Niche Breakdown  (bucket: Problem or Future · Authority · 10–20 min)
*The deep teach on the thing the member is known for — "cast a wide net around your niche so whatever they
search, they find you" (`/93`, draft). The Future bucket when it points at leverage and the next stage.*
- **Pull:** `offer.md` (`## The framework` — the method, step by step; its one line is the hook; `## Content
  branches` — the branch; `## The program` — the 90-day outcome, shown never earned) · `strategy.md` (known for) ·
  `proof.md` (results from the method — the member's own, labeled as theirs, or an agent's with consent) ·
  `story-bank.md` · `avatars.md` · the guide (`## Current magnet` — the blueprint the video points at).
- **Steps:** the outcome and who it's for → the method, step by step (the exact system the member uses — the
  chosen step taught in full, the rest named with the guide as where it lives) → the case study woven in ("this
  is legit": an agent who used it, or the member's own numbers stated honestly as production facts — only where
  `compliance.md`'s rev-share/income policy line allows production figures, the member's own and cited, never
  in the title by default, never income promises) → the mistakes that break it → the next stage (Future: what
  this makes possible — leverage, a team, an organization — concepts only) → the call ask in the law's shape
  (the recorded lessons, the templates, the community — partnering, free).
- **Hooks that fit:** formula 4 ("the top N things to … as a real estate agent"), formula 1, formula 5 as a
  self-case-study ("How I [outcome] from YouTube" — `[outcome]` reads as the result, never the member's own GCI,
  deal count, or a dollar figure by default; the one exception: `compliance.md`'s rev-share/income policy line
  explicitly allows production figures and the figure is the member's own, cited).
- **Never:** income figures as the promise; the member's own GCI, deal count, or dollar figure in the title (the
  compliance exception above is the only one); a competitor's method run down; a stat without a source.

## 5. Interview — intro and outro  (bucket: Interview · Proof · intro 45–75 s, outro 30–45 s)
*The guest is the star. The intro is recorded LAST — after the conversation — so it hooks the best things the
member now knows are coming (`/95`). `yt-interview` owns the guest list, the question map, consent, and the
distribution ask; this playbook writes only the two spoken pieces.*
- **Pull:** `memory/interview-pipeline.md` (the guest's transformation in their words, type, consent) ·
  `proof.md` · the recording notes (the two or three moments that landed) · `offer.md → ## The framework` (the
  method the guest ran — named in the call ask).
- **Intro steps (recorded last):** the transformation as the title promises it ("How [guest] [outcome]
  [constraint]" — the guest's own stated result or none) → who this is for ("if you're [avatar situation], this
  one's for you") → two or three specific things coming ("the exact [method], what the first 30 days actually
  looked like, the mistake that almost stopped them") → edify the guest in one honest line
  (`03-model-positioning/17`: recognition is retention) → the guide ask in one breath ("comment GUIDE and I'll
  send it") → "let's get into it."
- **Outro steps:** thank the guest and name the one thing to take from them → the **casual call ask** in the
  law's shape, the guest's example as the lead-in: *"if you want my help running what [guest] just walked you
  through — the lessons, the templates, the community — partner with me and it's free. [the booking line]."*
  (open register: *"I'll walk you through it, free, no strings."*) — never a promise of the guest's time or the
  member's → the next video (another interview on the same pain, or the model explained) → the end screen.
- **Hooks that fit:** formula 5 only — the hook-and-transformation title; never "tell me your story."
- **Never:** interrupting or making it about the member (the interview itself — `/95`); results the guest did
  not state; an income figure; a guest's result without recorded consent; a negative word about the guest's
  former brokerage or anyone there.

## Lines every format carries
- The guide ask inside minute one; the call ask a third to halfway in (the kit's words, the law's shape); the
  next video last (doctrine §8).
- One story from the bank where it lands, stamped Used-where; one real proof line where honest.
- Nothing that promises the member's time per partner; the compliance footer; the disclosure block in the
  description, not the script.
