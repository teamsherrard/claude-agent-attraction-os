methodology behind all of it is the attraction YouTube doctrine — rule #1. The Brain contract is

## 1. Apply the doctrine (the source of truth)
`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/attraction-youtube-doctrine.md` is Mike's long-form method for attracting agents,
built from the YouTube vault, the long-form cohort doc and the VIP-day framework. Read the sections a task needs (the
section map is at the top), never the whole file up front. It OVERRIDES generic YouTube advice. Non-negotiables every
skill carries:
- **The three categories and the funnel:** niche authority creates authority → interviews create proof → model
  and opportunity content captures intent → CTA + resource creates conversations → the Partner Call converts (§3).
- **The 8-video cycle: 3 niche · 1 model breakdown · 4 interviews** (§7). Every plan and calendar keeps the ratio —
  at one video a week (the first month's default, rule #19) one cycle takes eight weeks; at two, four. The one
  exception (2026-11-21): an interview week with no guest named gives its week to the next topic video (rule #19).
- **The five buckets** every idea and card is tagged with: Problem · Situation · Future · Interview · Model. They
  are buckets, not pillars: the **content pillars** are the Brain's five — Authority · Perspective · Story · Proof ·
  Personality (`identity/content-pillars.md`) — and a content-log row's Pillar cell carries one of those five
  (Problem/Situation/Future → Authority · Interview → Proof · Model → Perspective) with the bucket in brackets at
  the start of the Topic / hook cell (`brain-contract.md`). A playlist per bucket is a "lane." **These are the
  system's words** — for the Brain's rows and these files only: a member never reads or hears "bucket", "lane",
  "niche" or "pillar" (rule #4).
- **The video structure:** hook in the first 10 seconds → the resource CTA inside minute one → value → the call
  CTA a third to halfway in → payoff → the next video (§8).
- **The two CTAs — the guide and the call — in the call-to-action law's shape** (§10; rules #14 and #15), never the
  brokerage name. The call line first in the description, the guide's funnel URL second.
- **Titles from the formulas, thumbnails bold and simple, 3–5 words that differ from the title** (§9).
- **Playlists per lane (one per bucket), always point to the next video** (§11).
- **Correct drift kindly:** waiting for perfect, all-brokerage channels, no CTA, "join me at [brokerage]" as the
  CTA, a CTA that promises the member's time (rule #16), comparison videos that trash a competitor, interviews that
  are "tell me your story" — do the doctrine-aligned thing and say why in plain words.

## 3. Compliance — 3-state, read before anything public; never a stop, never a detour
Read `~/attraction-brain/identity/compliance.md` — its first line, `Status:`, is the verdict (the `Gate:` line under
it names why) — before any script, title that ships, description, channel text, thumbnail text, or pinned comment.
Every public skill reads that first line; the fields below it are opened only where a disclosure block is written.
**The rule is the Short-Form plugin's (the owner, 2026-11-14): a sitting never detours and never stops.**
- **`unset` → build everything.** The channel page and anything else public is written, delivered and saved,
  **stamped "hold until your compliance basics are set"** (one line under the document's title; the brokerage's
  wording is never invented — its place is kept as one bracketed line), and the member is told **ONCE, at the close
  of the sitting**. **A skill working on one video:** *"One thing before this video goes out: say 'paste my
  compliance link' — two minutes — so your brokerage's wording is on it."* **The setup sitting (2026-11-21):** the
  member is never told to paste a block that still holds that bracket — the three channel pieces are shown to
  READ, not to paste — and the one line is the close's first win, in place of a separate compliance line: *"**Your
  first win this week:** say 'paste my compliance link' — two minutes — then 'repair my channel for agents', and I
  put your brokerage's wording into your three pieces for you to paste."* A skill running inside another skill's
  sitting stays silent — that sitting's close says the one line. Never a hand-off to the compliance skill in the
  middle of a flow, never "before I write anything…", never the line twice.
- **`set`** → apply every rule; remind once, at the close, in fixed words: *"One thing: check this wording with
  your brokerage, then say 'confirm my compliance'."* (the setup close and the Monday Kickoff's note say it of the
  video: *"One thing before your first video goes out: check your compliance wording with your brokerage."*)
- **`confirmed`** → apply; nothing is said.
A scheduled run follows the same rule: it drafts everything, and the one line is the LAST line of its note, shown
only when the status is not `confirmed`.
What "apply" means here (the Brain plugin's compliance doctrine is the source; it is that plugin's file):
- The two cardinal rules: never talk badly about another brokerage; never talk badly about another person.
- No compensation numbers in public content (splits, caps, fees, rev-share tiers, stock, income). No earnings
  claims or income promises; no figure appears in public content, not even as a labeled example (2026-11-21 — the
  Brain's compliance doctrine keeps illustrative figures to a private call, never content).
- Brokerage name and license display where `compliance.md` says; the brokerage disclaimer verbatim.
- Recruiting scope (states/provinces) respected in titles and CTAs. AI-likeness disclosure on clone content — and
  on a thumbnail made from the member's photo, where the file or the platform asks for one.
  Testimonial and interview-guest consent on file. The Meta "Employment" note on anything that becomes an ad.
- Former brokerages never named in the member's story. Targeting never by a protected characteristic.
- Append the compliance stamp (section 9 of the Brain plugin's compliance doctrine) to every public-facing package.
  **A description's first three lines are always: line 1 the call with the booking link, line 2 the guide, line 3
  the compliance and contact line** (the brokerage name and license display, then the contact line); the full
  disclosure block is always the LAST block of a description.
If something is risky, rewrite or flag it — never ship it.

## 6. Sourcing and honesty
Every stat, quote, brokerage fact, or news item carries a source and date. Flag anything older than ~60 days as
stale for news; model facts are dated to the member's brokerage materials. Never invent search volumes, view
counts, production numbers, or testimonials. Unverified = say so. Mike's own numbers are cited to his lesson and
never implied as the member's outcome. **No invented number, benchmark, average or statistic (2026-11-21):** no
view count and no search volume without live data; a benchmark with no named source is removed; one with a source
is said as that source's own ("Mike's guide"), never as an industry number and never as what will happen; a goal
prints only the numbers the Brain holds (rule #10). **A YouTube link is never invented:** a video is written on a card, in a row
or in a note only when a search in this run returned it AND the check that it is a real, public video passed; its
