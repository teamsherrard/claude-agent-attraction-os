# Video Idea Templates — agent attraction (plug-and-play)

Ideation and the Game Plan fill these with the member's real specifics from the Brain: `[avatar]` (a named type
from `avatars.md`), `[niche]` and `[known for]` (`identity/offer.md → ## Content branches` FIRST — the UVP's segments
and their topics are the first source of the lanes and the title bank; then `strategy.md` and the offer's UVP),
`[method]` (`offer.md → ## The framework` — the named steps, hook material), `[promise]` (`## The program`'s 90-day
outcome — what a partner can show by day ninety; named as what the video teaches toward, never promised as a
result), `[pain]` (one of Mike's five),
`[model]` (`brokerage-model.md` — the model's name is fine in public; the figures are not), `[brokerage]` (the
member's OWN brokerage only, by the name `compliance.md` allows in public — never another company's), `[guest]`
(`interview-pipeline.md` — a real guest, with consent; none on file → no interview title is filled), `[story]`
(`story-bank.md`), `[outcome]` (a skill or a routine an agent wants — more conversations, a steadier pipeline, a
first listing — never income, a dollar figure or a number of deals promised to the viewer). **Swap every
`[bracket]` for a real value — never leave a placeholder, never invent one.** Every batch is **bucketed**: Problem ·
Situation · Future · Interview · Model, and every plan keeps the 8-video cycle (3 niche · 1 model · 4 interviews —
doctrine §7).

Buckets are not pillars: the OS pillars are the Brain's five (Authority · Perspective · Story · Proof ·
Personality); Problem / Situation / Future → Authority, Interview → Proof, Model → Perspective in every
content-log row (`brain-contract.md`).

Aligned to `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/attraction-youtube-doctrine.md`: the three categories (§3), the niche
buckets (§4), interviews (§5), model breakdowns (§6), the title formulas (§9 — numbered 1–7 below), the four
script formats (§12), and the cardinal rules (§15). Titles never carry compensation figures, income, a result
promised to the viewer, or a negative word about a brokerage, a model or a person — and every title here is for
agents, never for buyers or sellers.

## INTERVIEW — the hook-and-transformation title (`08-youtube/95`, VIP day)
*Never "tell me [guest]'s story." Standalone value for a viewer who has never heard of the guest. Every outcome
below is the guest's own stated result, said with their consent — never an income or a dollar figure, never
implied as what the viewer will get. No guest on file → none of these is filled: the plan's row reads "A
conversation with an agent — guest to be named".*
- How [guest] [achieved outcome] [surprising constraint — part-time · in a small market · as an introvert · in their first year]  *(5)*
- How [guest] went from [before, in their words] to [after] in [timeframe] — [the method]
- [Guest] closed their first deal in [N] days — here's exactly what they did
- How [guest] turned their business around after [N] years of [the struggle] — [the shift]
- [Guest] on [the niche topic]: the [system / routine / content process] behind [the result]
- Team leaders / broker-owners: How [guest] runs a [type] team on [the model] — what changed
Sequence by relatability (doctrine §5): across ages, backgrounds, markets, personalities — so any viewer finds
someone like them.

## The seven title formulas (doctrine §9 — cited there; use the numbers)

3. The truth about [X] no one tells you

7. Do NOT join [X] if… · [N] signs you've outgrown [X] · If you're [situation], do this for the next 90 days

## Rules
- **Bucket every idea** and keep the cycle: a batch of 4 is 2 niche (Problem/Situation/Future) · 1 interview ·
  1 model (one more niche when the model video for this cycle already shipped; the interview's place goes to a
  topic video while no guest is named); the Game Plan's first ninety days are cycles of 3 + 1 + 4 — one cycle in
  eight weeks at one video a week (the first month's default), in four at two (the step-up); the first four at
  one a week run niche · interview · niche · model. The bucket is a working tag — it is never printed in a line
  the member reads.
- **Lanes and titles come from `identity/offer.md → ## Content branches` first** (the rewire, 2026-11-14), the
  Brain's other signals second, research third, the starter bank last.
- **Every video's two asks are the guide and the call** — "comment GUIDE" / the guide's funnel URL for the resource,
  the booking line in the call-to-action law's shape for the call (house rules #14 and #15, the kit's CALLS TO
  ACTION lines verbatim). An idea or title never proposes a keyword, a comment word or a join line of its own, and
  never promises the member's time (house rules #16).
- **One avatar per title, named inside the plan** (the member's avatar, never "agents" in general).
- **One pain per title** where it applies, mapped to Mike's five.
- **Every idea cites a real signal** (demand · a dated news item from `memory/intel.md` · a captured question
  from `memory/ideas.md` or `objections.md` · a proven outlier · a coverage gap in the member's own channel).
- **Public titles never carry** compensation figures or compensation words as the promise (splits, caps, fees,
  rev-share tiers, stock, income — a component may be named only as the concept a video explains), a result
  promised to the viewer ("get your first [N] deals", "you'll…"), "#1 / fastest-growing" without a dated source, a
  protected characteristic, or a negative word about a brokerage, a model or a person. "Do NOT join [brokerage]
  if…" is about *fit*, and `[brokerage]` is always the member's own.
- **The member's own ideas come first** (`memory/ideas.md`, tag `youtube` / `interview`).
- Titles say "real estate agent(s)" or the agent type when the search needs it — the viewer is an agent.
