# Why Join Me — the 60-second story and the long version (Brain, Week 2)

Most agents at a revenue-share brokerage cannot answer "why should an agent join you?" without naming
their brokerage, and that is the whole problem: the brokerage is not the offer; the member is. Agents join
a person for three things — will you guide me, will you help me succeed, do you have a clear path I can
follow (`03-model-positioning/17`) — and the fastest way to answer all three is the member's own story
told in the right shape (`04-value-proposition/34`). This skill builds that story from what the Brain
already knows, in two lengths, in their spoken voice. **Zero to two questions.**

*Follow `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/how-we-speak.md` and `shared/ask-once-default.md`. Read
`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/attraction-doctrine.md` only for the two cardinal rules and the "attraction,
not recruiting" stance if they are not already in context.*

## Step 1 — Load the Brain (this is where the story comes from)
Read `~/attraction-brain/brain.md`, then:
- `identity/journey.md` — the three beats, the leader moment, the WHY (the **past** and **transformation**).
- `identity/profile.md` — the real reason they joined and the one line out loud.
- `identity/proof.md` — agents helped, organization size, reviews from agents (the only proof allowed).
- `identity/positioning.md` — the one-liner seed, if any ("why I'm here", said out loud).
- `identity/offer.md` — read the `Status:` line first. See Step 2.
- `identity/story-bank.md` — one story for the long version, by type and pain of the primary avatar.
- `identity/avatars.md` — who the story is for (the primary type's pain and words).
- `identity/voice-print.md` if it exists, else `identity/voice.md` — the story is read aloud, so it is
  written for the ear in their cadence.
- `identity/compliance.md` — 3-state; see Step 4.
If the local copy is missing, pull with **attraction-brain-sync**; a connector error is never "no Brain".
Never re-ask what these files answer. If `journey.md` is still a template, say so in one warm line and
point to **attraction-brand-persona** ("say 'my leader profile'"); do not interview here.

## Step 2 — Read the offer's status and respect it
This is a Week 2 skill, and many members run it before the offer session. `offer.md` carries a `Status:`
line:
- **`seeds (Week 2 builds the offer)`** → the **value** line of the story uses only what is in hand:
  what their brokerage gives every agent, what their upline provides, and the one thing they can teach
  first, as recorded. Say once, plainly: *"Your value line uses what you've already got. The offer session
  this week sharpens it, and I'll update the story then."* Never imply the offer is missing; never ask
  them to describe their offer.
- **`finalized by member`** or an offer built by **attraction-offer** → use the UVP one-liner ("I help
  [agent] achieve [outcome] through [mechanism]") and the value stack as the value line.
- **No `offer.md` at all** → same as seeds, from `profile.md` and `strategy.md` alone.
The brokerage is part of the value line as the platform, never as the reason: *"you don't just get [the
brokerage]; you also get…"*.

## Step 3 — Build the story (the five parts, Mike's shape)
From `04-value-proposition/34`, every version has five parts, in this order:
1. **Past** — where they started and the struggle, in one specific scene (Beat 1 or 2 of the journey).
2. **Transformation** — what they learned, built, or overcame (Beat 3; the leader moment).
3. **Mission** — why they care about agents winning (the WHY: who it is for, not "I want 5,000 agents").
4. **Value** — what an agent gets access to by joining them (Step 2 decides the source).
5. **Vision** — where they are taking the organization and how the agent benefits from it (the vision
   line in `strategy.md`: a future big enough that the agent's goals fit inside it).

The template that pulls it together (adapt the words to their voice; never read it as a form):
> *I know what it's like to [the pain, from Beat 2]. That's exactly why I [built / do / show] [the thing,
> from Beat 3 or what they teach first]. Now I help [the type of agent] [the outcome] so they can [the
> transformation]. When you join me, you don't just get [the brokerage]; you also get [the value line].
> [One line of vision.]*

Three outputs, all in their spoken voice:
- **The 60-second version** (about 140–170 words at their pace). Camera-ready; the top of a partner
  call; the answer to "so why should I join you?". One scene, one proof line at most, no list.
- **The long version** (3–5 minutes, 500–800 words). The "why I joined" video Mike made in his first 30
  days so nobody could make assumptions about why he moved (`01-foundation-mindset/01`); the story told
  properly on a call. Opens on the scene, carries one story from the bank, names one or two real agents
  helped (permission-checked), closes on the vision and one invitation: "book a call with me".
- **The one-breath version** (two sentences) for a bio line or a DM reply. Drafted, never asked.

Then the **differentiator check** from the same lesson, run silently before you deliver: what makes them
different from every other leader at their brokerage · which pain they solve that others do not · what
outcome an agent can expect · how they are uniquely equipped. If any answer is not in the story, revise;
if it is not in the Brain, say so in one line after delivery instead of inventing it.

**Hard rules on every version:**
- **Compensation never appears.** No splits, caps, stock, tiers, or rev-share numbers; "the model" is
  mentioned as the platform only. That conversation is a private call (`04-value-proposition/33`).
- **Former brokerages are never named**, and nothing negative is said about any brokerage or person
  (`03-model-positioning/13`). The past is the wall, not the company.
- **No income promises or earnings claims**, for the member or for the agent. Proof lines are only what
  `proof.md` holds, stated as the member stated them; any number is theirs and labelled theirs.
- **Nothing invented**: no agent, result, quote, or detail that is not in the Brain.
- **The brokerage is never the reason.** If the draft reads like "come join [brokerage]", it failed.
- The quality bar: the delete test, the any-agent test (could another leader at another brokerage say
  this line? then it is not done), the so-what test, no hedging.

## Step 4 — Compliance, then deliver
Read `identity/compliance.md`:
- **set / confirmed** → deliver all three versions. Note in one line what the public skills will add when
  the story goes in a video or post (brokerage-name display, license, the income disclaimer if the long
  version mentions earnings in any way; it should not).
- **unset** → still write the story to the Brain and show it; it is the member's own words for a private
  call. Add one plain line: *"Before this goes in a video or a post, your compliance rules need setting —
  say 'set my attraction compliance rules'."* Never "if empty, proceed" for anything public.

Deliver in chat in this order: the 60-second version · the long version · the one-breath version · one
line on where each is used · one line on what sharpens it next (the offer session, a voice-print
session, a story from the bank). Then: *"Anything that doesn't sound like you? Tell me and I'll redo it."*

**Save a clean doc** per `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/doc-formatting.md`: structured text rendered through
`shared/render_doc.py` to a styled `.docx` named `Why Join Me · [First Last] · [YYYY-MM-DD]`, saved to
the Offer folder of the workspace per `shared/drive-map.md`. The fallback chain is the one that file states;
never upload the raw structured text, never install packages in a loop.

## Step 5 — Write to the Brain and push
Write the three versions under a `## Why join me` block at the end of `identity/journey.md` (the member's
journey file; this skill owns that block and only that block; **attraction-brand-persona** preserves it on
every update), with *last updated* and the offer status the story was built from (`built from: seeds` /
`built from: finalized offer`). Write → push → verify: run **attraction-brain-sync** (PUSH) immediately;
an unsynced write is a lost write.

Confirm, no file names: *"Your why-join-me story is in your Brain — the 60-second one for camera and the
top of a call, the long one for your 'why I joined' video. Your scripts, your call prep, and your bio pull
from it now. When your offer is finalized, say 'update my why join me' and I'll sharpen the value line."*

## Update mode (trigger: "update my why join me")
Re-read the Brain, rebuild only the parts whose source changed (usually the value line after the offer
session, or proof after an agent's win), keep the member's edits to the rest, push, confirm in one line.

## How every output uses this (the payoff)
Content skills open the "why I joined" video and the first story reel from the 60-second version; call
prep reads it as the member's own answer to "why you?"; the bio and profile rewrite in Week 2 pull the
one-breath version; the Brain Book renders it in Part III. None of them invent a reason to join; they read
this one.

## Demo mode
Only when the request explicitly frames a fictional member: same five parts, every number "(illustrative
— demo)", no real agents or brokerages as references. A demo keyword aimed at the member's own story is a
real build; in doubt, ask the one question.
