# Interview guide — UVP Builder (Week 2)

One stop, 2–5 questions, only the gaps — **every question pre-answered with a recommended default built from
the Brain, so "yes" finishes the stop.** Everything from setup's Phase 4 (Stops 8–9) and Phase 2 is already
in the Brain and is read back, never re-asked. This should feel like ten minutes with a coach who has
already read the file and drafted the program, not a questionnaire. Per `shared/ask-once-default.md`: 2–4
questions is the norm, the question card holds four; five only when the program questions genuinely all
need the member (a Brain with no operations file and no name for anything).

## The stop (pick the 2–5 that are actually gaps; the rest are shown as "what I'm assuming", confirmed with one word)

Orient first, one line: *"This is the week we turn what you've got into a program an agent can join. I've
drafted most of it from your Brain — a few things only you can confirm, each with my recommendation, so
'yes' works. About five minutes."*

**Q-A · What you already have** — *"Here's what I've got on what your brokerage and your upline already give
an agent on day one: [the list, generic names]. What am I missing, and what would you personally hand an
agent in their first week?"* Listen for: a weekly call, an onboarding doc, templates, a group, a mentor
program, access to the member. Capture in their words.

**Q-B · The one outcome** — *"For [the one-line target], what's the ONE result you can honestly help them
get in their first 90 days — something you've produced yourself?"* Push past "more deals": *"what does
that look like on a Tuesday — three buyers from open houses? their first listing from their sphere?"*
This becomes `The 90-day outcome:` — what the partner can SHOW, never earn.

**Q-C · The first lesson** (only if Stop 8 Q32 was blank) — *"If a partner joined tomorrow, what would you
sit down and show them in the first hour? The actual sheet, the actual message, the actual setup."*

**Q-D · Proof not yet written down** — *"Any agent you've helped, even informally, with a result you can
point to? Or your own result with this method — the number you'd say out loud?"* "None yet" is a real
answer and goes in as an open item.

**Q-E · The program type and format (pre-answered)** — *"From what you do today, this is a **mentorship
program**: a weekly call plus a 1:1 to start. Right?"* The default comes from the type rule (`SKILL.md`
§5): a weekly call plus a 1:1 → mentorship program · recorded lessons plus a group → course plus community
· a dated 90-day push with milestones → accelerator · 1:1 coaching as the spine → coaching program · a team
with shared systems and a shared brand → team system · mostly the upline's and brokerage's assets plus
access to the member → partnership program. Name the format mix in the same breath: *"Live weekly call,
one recorded lesson, 1:1 on Zoom for your first [Sunday]. Yes?"*

**Q-F · The cadence (pre-answered from `operations.md`; asked only where it is silent)** — *"The weekly call
is **Tuesdays at 9:00, 30 minutes on Zoom**, and the group chat is where the link lives — right? If you
don't run a call yet, I'll write 'a Tuesday call that starts when the first partner joins' and you pick the
day."* Then the 1:1 rhythm: *"Day 1 welcome call, you on Zoom for their first [Sunday], a day-30 check,
then one 1:1 a month — keep that?"* The cadence must fit the hours in `goals.md`; if it doesn't, say so
and propose the smaller version.

**Q-G · The framework name (three options from their words, recommend one)** — *"Your method needs a name
an agent can repeat. From your own words, three options: **'[the thing they call it]'** (what you already
say), **'[the outcome]'** (what it produces), **'[the object]'** (the sheet or tool at the centre). I'd use
'[recommended]' because [one line — it's the phrase in your own story / it's what the lead magnet will be
cut from]. Yes, or a different one?"* Options only from `strategy.md`, `journey.md`'s turning point, the
Teach-first lesson, and the member's signature phrases in `voice.md` — never a generic name.

**Q-H · The program name (three options: plain · outcome-led · branded, recommend one)** — *"And the
program itself: **'[Plain — e.g., Partner with Taylor]'** · **'[Outcome-led — e.g., The No-Lead-Bill
Program]'** · **'[Branded — e.g., The Sunday Table]'**. I'd go with '[recommended]' — [one line why: it's
already on your Instagram / it's the outcome in four words]. The comment keyword comes from it: [KEYWORD]."*
An organization name already in `brand-visual.md` or `profile.md` is the branded option and usually the
pick. The program and the offer share one name; the keyword never changes once content has used it.

**Q-I · Recorded vs live (pre-answered)** — *"Here's what I'll write as recorded today: [Module 1, the
sheet walkthrough]. Everything else is taught live at the [Tuesday call] and recorded with your first
partners. Right?"* A lesson the member says they "will record this week" is still written as "recorded
with my first partners" until it exists.

**Q-J · The support expectation (pre-answered from `operations.md` → Response commitment)** — *"Support, in
plain words: 'I reply the same day on weekdays; Sunday evening for anything about a sign-in sheet.' Keep
that, or tighten it?"* With no commitment on file, the default is "within one business day; the weekly call
for everything that can wait" — never "24/7", never a promise the hours in `goals.md` can't carry.

End the stop: *"**Your turn** — 'yes' takes all my recommendations; short, messy edits are perfect."*

## Consultant moves for "I don't know" — one per question, exact wording shape (never reuse the words)
Never write "TBD". Offer 2–3 concrete options built from THEIR data, say why each fits in one line, write
the exact wording, recommend one. Their choice is final.
- **Q-A, thin "you" layer, strong upline:** *"Your offer this quarter is 'I get you plugged into [upline's
  weekly calls and onboarding] in week one and I personally walk you through the first [strategy] setup' —
  small, true, and it's what you'd actually do."*
- **Q-B, strong strategy, no proof:** *"Lead with your own result — '[the number they said]' — and say
  you're taking your first three partners through it for free to build the case studies. That's exactly
  how Mike started."*
- **Q-C, several strategies:** pick the one with "Teach it: yes" that maps to the avatar's #1 pain; the rest
  are lessons 2 and 3. *"Lesson 1 is [the sheet] because it's the thing you can put on camera tonight."*
- **Q-E, "I don't know what to call the type":** *"You run a Tuesday call and you sit with a new partner on
  their first Sunday — that is a mentorship program by definition. If you'd rather lead with the recorded
  lessons, it's a course plus community, but today you have one recording, so mentorship is the honest
  word."* Recommend the type with the member's own time in it.
- **Q-F, no weekly call yet:** *"Then the program says 'a weekly call that starts when the first partner
  joins — Tuesdays at 9, 30 minutes' — an agent reads that as a plan, not a gap. Pick the day you'd
  actually keep."* Never invent a call that is running.
- **Q-G, no name for the method:** build three from the transcript of their own answers — the phrase they
  repeat (*"the Sunday routine"*), the outcome (*"sign-in to showing"*), the object (*"the two-question
  sheet"*) — and recommend the one that names what the lead magnet will be cut from.
- **Q-H, no name for the program:** *"Plain: 'Partner with [First name]' — honest, forgettable.
  Outcome-led: '[the outcome in four words]' — says what it does. Branded: '[the organization name]' —
  already on your profile. I'd take the branded one and let the outcome live in the subtitle."*
- **Q-I, "I'll record it all next week":** *"Then I'll write it as 'recorded with my first partners' and
  flip it the day the file exists — the offer is only ever what is true today."*
- **Q-J, "I'm always available":** *"I'll write what you can keep for a year: same-day on weekdays, the
  Tuesday call for everything else. 'Always' is the promise that breaks first."*

## Vague answers and the follow-up
| They say | Ask |
|---|---|
| "Training and support" | *"Which training, and what does an agent do differently after it?"* |
| "I'll be there for them" | *"What does that look like on a calendar — a weekly slot, a first-week 1:1, a group?"* |
| "Our brokerage has everything" | *"Name three things, in plain words, that an agent uses in their first month."* |
| "I'm good at marketing" | *"Which one thing produced the most business — and could you show someone the exact setup?"* |
| "It's a coaching program" (with no 1:1 slots on the calendar) | *"Where does the 1:1 time live each week? If it's the Tuesday call, it's a mentorship program — and that's the stronger word for what you actually do."* |
| "It's an accelerator" (with no dates or milestones) | *"What's the start date and the day-30, day-60, day-90 milestone? Without them I'll write it as a mentorship program with a 90-day on-ramp."* |

Maximum two follow-ups. Then build with what's there and mark open items.

## Never ask
Splits, caps, fees, stock, rev share, income — not for the program's "what it costs" line either (that line
is "nothing today — it comes with the partnership", plus any discounted item free-vs-paid priced). Anything
the Brain answers. A second stop of questions before the draft exists. "What's your offer?" — the member is
here because they don't know yet. "What type of program is it?" as an open question — always pre-answer it.
