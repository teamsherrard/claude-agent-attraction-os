# The Partner Offer doc — what renders to `05 · Offer`

A clean, member-facing document in the one neutral house style (`shared/doc-formatting.md`): the
member reads it, says it on calls, and hands the Design Package the substance for the offer stack and
the "Join My Team" one-pager. Branding happens later in Claude Design, never here.

Written in the member's first-person voice (`identity/voice.md`), fifth-grade reading level, outcomes
not features (`04-value-proposition/32`). Reads like an article they'd be proud to send, not a brochure.
Its last line is always the compliance reminder.

## Structure (structured text for `render_doc.py`)

Title: `[Offer name]` · meta line: `[Member] · [brokerage, generic if compliance requires otherwise] · [date]`

**THE PROMISE** — the UVP one-liner as the opening, then two or three sentences that put the primary
avatar on a Tuesday (from `avatars.md`'s portrait): what their year looks like, what they've tried, what
they're quietly worried about. This is the article's opening, not a heading-and-bullets block.

**THE PROBLEMS I SOLVE** — a table: `| Their problem | Why it hurts right now | What I do about it | Proof |`
Three to five rows, only from the overlap. Proof reads "[first partner's result goes here]" when none yet.

**WHAT YOU GET DAY ONE** — the list, each item one bold lead-in and one outcome sentence. Real today.
Then: "And everything my brokerage provides — I walk you through that on a call."

**WHAT I TEACH YOU FIRST** — the three lessons as a table: `| Lesson | After this you can | What we build together |`.
Lesson 1 concrete enough to start tonight.

**THE [PRODUCT NAME]** — the digital product promise: what it is, who it's for, the three things it does
for them. If it is still being built: "First version with my first partners — built this quarter." Never a
promise of a product that will not exist.

**YOUR FIRST 30 DAYS** — the week-by-week table: `| Week | You | Me | Done when |`.

**WHY NOW** — the honest paragraph. No countdown clocks.

**NEXT STEP** — the ask (book a call — the booking line), the comment keyword line, and one sentence in the
member's voice about what the call is and isn't ("30 minutes, about your business, no pitch").

Last line, verbatim: *"Compensation isn't on this page on purpose — it's a private, brokerage-approved
conversation. Before this goes anywhere public, check your brokerage's advertising and rev-share
marketing rules."*

## What never appears
Splits, caps, fees, tiers, stock, rev share, income figures, another brokerage's name as a comparison,
a former brokerage by name, any claim not in `proof.md`, bracketed template text.
