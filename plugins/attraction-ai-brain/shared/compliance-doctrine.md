# Compliance Doctrine — the rules that bite in agent attraction

*Read by `attraction-compliance` (which writes `identity/compliance.md`) and by every public-facing skill in every
Agent Attraction plugin before output. Grounded in: the two cardinal rules (`03-model-positioning/13`), the
no-income-claims stance across the vault (`02-prospect-targeting/21`, `03-model-positioning/14`,
`04-value-proposition/28`), the franchise look-period warning (`02-prospect-targeting/26`), the ICA note
(`11-objection-handling/58`), the brokerage-logo note (`11-objection-handling/56`, `02-prospect-targeting/24`), the
ads note (`01-foundation-mindset/02`), the plan's compliance list, and the cohort doc's risk decisions.*

> **This is assistance, not legal advice.** The member stays responsible for their marketing and confirms the
> brokerage-specific and jurisdiction-specific items with their brokerage's compliance desk and their regulator.
> Where this file has no source, it says so. It never guesses at legal text.

---

## 1. The gate is three-state (never two)

`identity/compliance.md` carries `Status: unset | set | confirmed`.
- **unset** — the member has not answered Stop 15. **No public output.** Say it plainly and warmly: *"Before I
  write anything public, I need your compliance basics — how your brokerage name appears, your license display,
  and your brokerage's rule on talking about income. Say 'set up my compliance' and it takes three minutes."*
  Private outputs (the model script, the money scenarios, the top-50) still run.
- **set** — captured; apply every rule and remind the member once per session to confirm with their brokerage.
- **confirmed** — the member confirmed on a date; apply.
"If compliance is empty, proceed" is **banned** — it shipped `[Brokerage Name]` placeholders in three realtor
plugins. A placeholder in a public piece is a FAIL.

**Public** means: posts, reels, scripts, captions, bios, DM and email templates, lead magnets, landing pages,
ads, webinar copy, anything an agent outside the organization will see. **Private** means: the member's own
notes, the Brain Book, the scorecard, the model script for a one-on-one call, the money scenarios.

## 2. Earnings claims and income promises

**Rule:** no output ever states, implies, or illustrates what a partner will earn. No "you'll make $X," no
rev-share earnings projections, no "six figures a month," no stock-value projections, no lifestyle framed as
income proof.
**Grounding:** Mike never shows his revenue share on an agent call (`02-prospect-targeting/21`); he warns that
projecting a stock's future is "SEC-regulated" and "a facade" (`03-model-positioning/14`); the cohort doc flags
"earnings disclaimers … given the 6-figure per month promise" as a risk decision; the workshop skills' rule:
"results are framed as what the member will SHOW, never what the partner will EARN."
**How the OS applies it:**
- Rev-share scenarios (`attraction-rev-share-calculator`) are arithmetic on the member's own assumptions, every
  number labeled "(illustrative)", compliance-stamped, private-call only, never in content.
- Mike's own figures are quoted as his, cited to the lesson, never implied as typical.
- Where a piece must mention earnings at all (a private one-pager, a webinar slide the member insists on), the
  income disclaimer from `compliance.md` is appended verbatim. Default wording when the brokerage has none:
  *"Results vary. Nothing here is a promise of income; any figures are illustrative examples, not typical
  results."* **Mike's own disclaimer wording is not in the vault — plan §9 item 4 asks for it.**

## 3. Non-disparagement — the two cardinal rules (`03-model-positioning/13`)

1. **Never talk badly about another brokerage.** It looks unprofessional and desperate; it means you don't know
   how to position your own value. Give credit ("Real is an incredible brokerage"), then "here's what I can do
   for you."
2. **Never talk badly about another sponsor or person.** Word gets around; agents repeat it. If you've heard
   negatives and even have proof, don't acknowledge them — shift back to your value.
**Corollaries the OS enforces:** former brokerages are never named in stories ("a franchise", "an independent");
competitive-landscape research reports observable facts with sources and never a verdict; "intel" rows are
facts, never ammunition; an objection answer about another sponsor never names a flaw — it asks whether they
showed proof (`11-objection-handling/52–53`). A skill asked to "call out" another brokerage or person says so in
one line and writes the strength instead.

## 4. Brokerage advertising and rev-share marketing policy

**Rule:** the member's brokerage policy on marketing the model, revenue share, and the brokerage itself governs
every public piece. Captured at Stop 15 (Q59): know it, or default to the strictest — no splits, caps, stock,
tiers, or income figures in public content; compensation explained on a private call only.
**Grounding:** Mike notes that at his brokerage "you're not allowed to run ads to a brokerage-explained" video —
value first, then convert (`01-foundation-mindset/02`); compensation and "the model" are presented on the call,
tailored (`02-prospect-targeting/19`). **The specific policy text of any brokerage is not in the vault** — the
member supplies it (a document in `06 · Materials`, cited with its date) or the strict default applies.
**Agents-only audiences:** content that promotes the model is aimed at licensed agents; consumer-facing content
(the member's selling brand) never carries recruiting language.

---

## 5. Brokerage name, license display, and logo

**Rule:** the brokerage's legal name appears in public content the way the brokerage and the local board
require; the license number appears where the jurisdiction requires it (bios, email signatures, marketing);
the brokerage logo appears on signage and materials per the local board — Mike: "in most cases the brokerage's
logo has to be the same size as your own personal logo on signage" (`11-objection-handling/56`); "for compliance
you need to have it on signs, business cards, brochures" (`02-prospect-targeting/24`).
**Grounding gap:** the exact rule differs by board and brokerage; `compliance.md` records what the member's
board and brokerage say, with the source. The brand doctrine keeps the leader brand visually distinct from the
brokerage's colors; it never removes a required mark.

## 6. Recruiting scope: jurisdiction, licensing, inducements, look periods

- **Scope by state/province (Stop 15, Q60):** the member names where they may attract licensed agents; outputs
  that invite agents from outside that scope are flagged. Licensing law on recruiting across jurisdictions is
  **not in the vault** — the member confirms with their brokerage; the cohort doc lists "a review of state and
  provincial rules on inducements when recruiting licensed agents" as a pending decision before any transition
  plan or comparison one-pager ships.
- **Inducements:** nothing the OS writes offers a cash, gift, or fee inducement to switch. Free training that
  the member gives all their agents is value, not an inducement — but the member confirms their jurisdiction's
  rule.
- **Franchise look period (`02-prospect-targeting/26`):** a franchise owner may only discuss moving inside the
  window before their agreement ends; approaching them outside it is tortious interference ("you can get sued,
  and you probably will"). Any broker-owner outreach asks about this first and defers to the brokerage's
  guidance.
- **Independent contractor agreements (`11-objection-handling/58`):** termination terms and listing/commission
  treatment on exit live in the agent's ICA. The OS tells the prospect to read theirs; it never advises on it.
- **Fair housing** still applies to any real-estate example: describe properties and lifestyle, never who
  "belongs" somewhere. Targeting agents is by career stage, production, model, and mindset — never a protected
  characteristic.

## 7. AI-likeness disclosure and testimonial consent

- **AI clone / AI voice content** (the cohort's AI Clone Employee): disclosed on screen and in the description
  with the wording captured in `compliance.md`; nothing presents a clone as a live recording. The disclosure
  field is captured at Stop 15 even if the member has no clone yet ("no clone content" is a valid value).
- **Testimonials from agents:** consent on file or ask every time; verbatim, attributed as given, never
  improved; the cohort doc lists "testimonial consent and AI-likeness disclosure captured in the Value Vault
  templates and the clone setup" as a decision — this doctrine assumes yes.
- **Platform rules** on synthetic media change; the member checks the platform's current label requirement.

## 8. Ads: the Meta Employment special-ad-category note

Anything that becomes a paid ad to attract licensed agents may fall under Meta's **Employment** special ad
category (the cohort doc's guardrail for the future Ads plugin), which restricts audience targeting. The OS
flags this on any output that is, or will become, an ad, and the member's brokerage policy on ads (§4) applies
on top. The OS does not build ads in Plugin 1.

---

## 9. The compliance stamp (what every public output appends)

One short block, in the member's voice where possible, built from `compliance.md`:
- brokerage name as required · license display as required · the brokerage disclaimer verbatim if any ·
  the income disclaimer only when earnings were mentioned · the AI-likeness line only on clone content.
Plus one reminder line to the member, not for publishing: *"Check this against your brokerage's advertising and
revenue-share marketing rules before it goes out."*
