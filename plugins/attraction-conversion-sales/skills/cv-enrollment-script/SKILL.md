# Enrollment Conversation Script — the call, in the member's words

Not a script to read; a script to know. It follows the sequence of events exactly as lesson 42 states it,
with the Week 5 names on each step and Mike's plain gloss beside them (framework lock:
`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/conversion-doctrine.md` §2–§3). Every line is built from the member's Brain so
it passes the any-agent test. House rules: `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/house-rules.md`.

## Before writing (silent)
`brain.md` first (pull if missing) · `identity/voice.md`, `voice-samples.md`, `voice-print.md` (the script
sounds like them or it's useless) · `identity/journey.md` incl. the `## Why join me` block (the 60-second
story) · `identity/offer.md` (the Partner Offer: UVP line, what's included, first 30 days; at `Status: seeds`
→ build from "what you have to give so far" and say once that Week 2's offer sharpens this) ·
`identity/positioning.md` (the 2-minute model script, what stays private) · `identity/brokerage-model.md`
(the model per type; the join steps need the brokerage's onboarding document from `06 · Materials` — missing
→ a labeled block for the member to fill, never invented) · `identity/avatars.md` (the primary type — the
script is written for them; a secondary type gets a short variant block) · `identity/story-bank.md` (two or
three stories by pain; stamp `Used-where` when placed) · `identity/proof.md` · `memory/objections.md` (the
objections this member actually hears) · `config.md` Conversion block (`Partner call length`).
Compliance: the script is private-call material; the next-steps and welcome email templates inside it are
public → `identity/compliance.md` **unset holds those two blocks** with one line; `set` → apply and remind once;
`confirmed` → apply; the rest renders either way.

**Fast lane:** Brain loaded → ONE line and the script. ONE question only if the Conversion block has no call
length: *"Are you running hour-long calls or 30 minutes right now? (Mike's rule: an hour until you've done
about 30, then 30.)"* **Your turn.**

## The script (both lengths share this spine; minutes shown for 60 / 30)
**OPEN · rapport (0–5 / 0–2)** — energy, looking forward to it, one specific thing from their booking answers or
content, "how's your business been this year — what's working, what isn't?" (`bonus/perfect-presentation`).

**1 · DISCOVERY — current state (5–15 / 2–7)** — the discovery questions for the primary type
(`cv-question-funnel`): what piqued your interest · what do you love about where you are · what's frustrating
you most · what's missing. Listen; let every answer finish; the member talks ~30%.

**2 · DIAGNOSIS — go deeper (15–22 / 7–11)** — what it's costing them · what they've tried · "if nothing changes,
12 months from now?" · "what would [their goal] mean for you and your family?" Mirror their vision back in
their words. This is the open loop.

**3 · FIT — the three things that matter most (22–27 / 11–14)** — *"Based on what you told me, there are three
things that matter most here: [pain 1], [pain 2], [goal]."* Chosen live from what they said, never from a
list. The member's journey beat: "I was there — [the story hook]."

**4 · POSITIONING — the bridge, through their goals (27–40 / 14–21)** — the member's support (the offer parts
that answer the three things; the first 30 days as a partner) · the upline and the community (as it is today,
real and small beats big and promised) · the brokerage's model, in plain English, only as far as their pain goes
(production pain → training and the member's system, "and everything [brokerage] provides — I'll walk you
through it"; exit or time-freedom pain → residual income as the vehicle, from `brokerage-model.md`, every number
from the member's materials and labeled). **Compensation:** answered when asked — honest, plain, no projections,
no income figures for anyone — then back to their goal. Never led with. Proof: one real line from `proof.md`;
none yet → "you'd be early, and here's what I'll do for you in week one."

**5 · QUESTIONS — their questions and objections (40–50 / 21–26)** — *"What questions do you have? What would
stop you?"* The three or four objections this type throws, pre-handled with Listen → Validate → Reframe →
Invite from the Brain doctrine §14 handlers rewritten in the member's voice, plus the commitment questions:
*"If we can solve [what they named], would you be ready to go all in?"*

**6 · NEXT STEP — assume the close, make it foolproof (50–60 / 26–30)** — energy up, future-pace: *"This is going
to be an exciting new chapter. The number one thing people say is they wish they'd come earlier. When it comes to
a transition date, what feels least disruptive? Time's never perfect — what works so we can put a proactive plan
in place and you launch with momentum?"* Then exactly what happens next, with confidence and clarity ("so you
don't go into paranoia mode" — `bonus/perfect-presentation`): *"What's the best email to send the steps to?
You'll get one email from me today with how to join and what happens after; once you're active you get the
welcome email with every link."*

**The three endings (`/42`):**
- **Ready →** the NEXT-STEPS EMAIL template: personalized first paragraph (`[what they said that showed you
  listened]`), the model video link, the funnel or page with everything the member provides, then
  `JOIN STEPS — [brokerage]` as a labeled block: the exact link, the exact button, "when it asks who introduced
  you, put [member's name]," "this doesn't notify your current broker," "solo or team" — filled from the
  brokerage's onboarding document, or left clearly marked for the member. Then the WELCOME EMAIL template
  (personal welcome · the walkthrough video · the community link · the training links · "questions go to me
  first, then [support]") and the line to book the week-one STRATEGY CALL.
- **Hesitant →** *"I'd love you to meet [upline partner] — [one edification line] — they can answer anything
  about the model. Does [day] at [time, time zone] work?"* → `cv-three-way`.
- **Not ready →** *"No pressure at all — this is about the right time for you. Let's connect in a couple of
  weeks and see how things are going."* The resources email still goes out today. → `cv-follow-up`.

Then **THE 30-MINUTE VERSION** as its own section (same spine, the minutes above, the two or three questions
per step that matter most, the same endings), and a one-page **CARD** (the step names with the one question
each, for the member's second screen).

## Rules
- The step names the member sees are Discovery · Diagnosis · Fit · Positioning · Questions · Next Step (the
  locked default, doctrine §3); the workshop names are accepted as synonyms in input and never corrected out loud.
- Cardinal rules throughout: nothing against another brokerage, sponsor, or person; former brokerages unnamed.
- No income claims anywhere; every number in the positioning step is from `brokerage-model.md` and labeled.
- Zero fabrication: no invented proof, no borrowed stories, no join steps the brokerage document doesn't state.
- The week rule: a seeds-stage offer is "what you have to give so far," never missing.
- Quality bar on every line: delete · any-agent · so-what · no hedging · no filler headings.
- Banned words and the recruiter register per `how-we-speak.md` §7. "Enroll," "partner," never "recruit."
