# 3-Way Call — edify, then step back, then own the outcome

Three people: the member, the prospect, and somebody from the upline who understands the model, has more
credibility, and can get the prospect over the hump. Mike's own line, quoted: *"in the beginning, leverage
3-way calls"* — he put all of his first 30 agents on 3-way calls with his sponsor and studied every one
(`10-presentation-delivery/43`). Edification "increases show-up rates and conversion rates, the two most
important metrics." Doctrine: `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/conversion-doctrine.md` §5. House rules:
`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/house-rules.md` (#1 — the invite, the brief, and the emails are drafts; the
member sends and books).

## The pack (exact sections)
**WHO SHOULD BE ON IT** — one line: the best communicator in the upline who understands the model ("it might
not be your sponsor," `02-prospect-targeting/19`); if the Brain names several, the pick and why.

**BEFORE — getting the commitment and the invite**
- The line to the prospect that edifies the partner and asks for the 3-way (from the Hesitant ending): *"I'd
  love you to meet [partner] — [one true accolade] — they'll answer anything about the model. Does [day] at
  [time] [time zone] work?"*
- The calendar invite copy (title, two-line description, the Zoom link slot) with the **time zone written out
  twice** ("10:00 am MDT / 12:00 pm ET") — Mike's own mistake, "tons of times."
- The check list: both accepted? If not, the one-line confirmation text to the prospect. Reminders are
  `sales-show-up`'s job; this pack names them.

**DURING — the edification scripts (both directions)**
1. **Edify the guest FIRST** — even a brand-new agent: something true and specific that makes them feel
   special (from the intel report and their booking answers) — *"[Partner], this is [Name]. [One true thing —
   their move, their work ethic, their result, their reason for the call].”* It sets the whole call on the
   right track.
2. **Edify the partner** — stack real, verifiable accolades, then what they're there to do: *"And [Name],
   [Partner] is [accolade 1], [accolade 2], [accolade 3]. They're going to be able to answer any and every
   question you have about [brokerage] and our group so you can see exactly how we'd help you."* Never the bad
   version ("has a YouTube channel and is active on social media, I'll let you take it from here"). No income
   figures, no "makes $X a month," no projections; organization size and awards only if the partner stated them.
3. **Then stop talking.** The member's only lines after the introductions: none, unless the guest or the
   partner asks them a direct question. Camera on, notes open, no phone — "we can see you." Take notes on how
   every objection is handled (that is the member's education).
4. **The partner's version** — a short script the partner can use to edify the MEMBER back to the prospect
   (two or three true things from `proof.md` and `journey.md`), so the prospect hears why the member is the
   right partner from someone else's mouth.

**THE UPLINE BRIEF** (one page the member sends the partner before the call — a draft):
who the prospect is (type, brokerage type, market, how long licensed, how they got here) · what they said they
want (their words) · the three things that matter most (from the prep) · the open objections and what has
already been said · what the member has already offered · what NOT to raise (compensation unless asked; the
prospect's broker; anything they're sensitive about) · the ask for the call (commit, or a dated next step) ·
the proposed next steps if they say yes.

**AFTER — the member owns the outcome**
- Said yes → the member sends the next-steps email (template from `cv-enrollment-script`), today.
- Not yet → the member follows up, not the partner — the dated touch with a reason (`cv-follow-up`); "this is
  your prospect. Own the outcome."
- The thank-you line to the partner, and what the member learned (two lines for their own notes).

## Log, then push
When the member confirms the 3-way is booked: a `memory/conversations.md` row (Channel = 3-way is used for
the held call; for the booking, Channel = the channel it was agreed on, Next step = the 3-way date, **Stage
after = 3-way**). Admin installed → the column is the request and the output ends with
**STAGE MOVE REQUESTED: [Name]: Call held → 3-way**; absent → also the Board and Stage-moves-log rows in
`memory/pipeline.md`, `Logged by: cv-three-way`, and that agent's Last touch · Next move · Due cells on the
Top-50 (the interim allowance in `shared/brain-contract.md`). After the call, `cv-debrief` logs what happened. Push
immediately. Render the pack per `shared/doc-formatting.md` → `3-Way Pack · [Prospect] · [Date].docx` →
`04 · Agents/Prospects` on request.

## Rules
- "Leverage" appears only inside Mike's quoted line, never in the pack's prose.
- Every accolade is true and verifiable; nothing invented about the partner or the member.
- No income claims in any edification — not "top earner," not "seven figures," not rev-share size.
- Cardinal rules: the prospect's brokerage and broker are never discussed; a competing sponsor is never named badly.
- The member never speaks for the partner's calendar; the invite is a draft they send.
- Quality bar: delete · any-agent · so-what · no hedging.
- Banned words and the recruiter register per `how-we-speak.md` §7; "upline partner," "sponsor," never "downline."
