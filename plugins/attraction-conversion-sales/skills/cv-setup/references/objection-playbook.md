# The Objection Playbook — how Step 3 of the sitting writes the fifteen in the member's words

*Read by `cv-setup` at Step 3 (and on "update my objection playbook"). The handlers come from
`${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/launch-stacy-my-sales-trainer/references/objection-bank.md` — Mike's fifteen on Listen → Validate →
Reframe → Invite, each with the question to ask, the mistake to avoid, a proof/story slot and a brokerage slot. The
sitting rewrites every one FRESH for this member: their voice, their stories, their proof, their model. The bank's
notes are never pasted; Mike's figures and Mike's brokerage never appear. The playbook is the member's; the Trainer
drills from it and the Morning Call Prep picks from it.*

## What to read (silent)
`identity/voice.md` + `voice-print.md` (every handler sounds like them) · `identity/avatars.md` (the primary type —
the order the fifteen are printed in follows what that type throws most; the "typically heard from" column of the
bank's archetype table) · `identity/story-bank.md` (one story per handler where a real one fits; stamp `Used-where`)
· `identity/proof.md` (the proof slot — real, cleared; nothing yet → *"you'd be early — here's what I'll do for you in
week one"*, never a gap line) · `identity/brokerage-model.md` (the brokerage slot and the **Q&A bank** — the
brokerage-specific entries 16+) · `identity/journey.md` (the member's own "I used to think that too" moment, where
true) · `memory/objections.md` (anything this member has already heard — those go first).

## The shape of every handler — in the member's words
```
──── N. "[the objection, in the prospect's likely words]" ────
Archetype: [A1–A7, by name] · Hidden fear: [one clause]
LISTEN — what to hear, and the one line that shows you heard it
VALIDATE — the line ("a lot of great agents feel that way…"; the ninja line where it fits)
THE QUESTION TO ASK — one or two, chosen from the bank, in the member's voice
REFRAME — the new angle, with the member's story or proof where a real one fits
INVITE — the open door, a question, never pressure
Don't: [the one mistake to avoid]
```
Every REFRAME that leans on a brokerage program reads from `brokerage-model.md`; when the Brain does not confirm
it, the line reads **"check whether your brokerage has this"** in the member's copy — never an assertion, never an
invented program (the bank's cap-honouring, co-working, health-benefit and "88%" lines are Mike's and stay out).

## The two tables the document opens with
**The seven archetypes and the hidden fear** — one short table (archetype · hidden fear · who usually says it), from
the bank's `/46` table, in plain words. "Once you know the archetype, you know the fear. Address the fear and the
objection disappears."

**The framework in four lines** — Listen · Validate · Reframe · Invite, each with Mike's gloss and the tell that it
was skipped (the bank's framework table, condensed) — and the rule under it: *"Objections are not rejection —
they're a request for more clarity."*

## The entries
1–15 are the bank's fifteen, in this member's order (what their primary type throws most first; anything already in
`memory/objections.md` moves to the top). Then **BROKERAGE-SPECIFIC** — every objection-shaped answer in
`brokerage-model.md`'s Q&A bank, on the same four steps, numbered 16+ in the member's words. No Q&A bank yet → one
line in the document, *"Your brokerage's own answers are added when your model is explained — say 'explain my model'
(ten minutes)"* — never a placeholder entry. "I don't have time" has no stand-alone entry; the closings handler (#11)
is its worked case and says so.

## The document — `Objection Playbook — [Name]`
Structured text per `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/shared/doc-formatting.md`: the title and meta lines; CAPS bands — HOW TO
USE THIS (three lines: welcome the objection; find the archetype; four steps, then a question) · THE SEVEN
ARCHETYPES (the table) · THE FOUR STEPS · THE FIFTEEN (a `──── N. "…" ────` sub-band each) · BROKERAGE-SPECIFIC.
Rendered through `render_doc.py` to `Objection Playbook — [Name] · YYYY-MM-DD.docx` → `05 · Offer`;
`RENDERER-UNAVAILABLE` → the `.md` upload, one plain line. The same text goes, in full, under `## Objection
Playbook` in `identity/conversion.md`. **`memory/objections.md` is not written here** — the Trainer owns it; the
playbook is identity, the practice log is memory.

## Gates and rules
- Private-call material: no compliance stamp. Still: the cardinal rules on every line — never a negative word about
  another brokerage, the other sponsor they are "also talking to", or the broker they "love"; no income or revenue
  share figures, ever; the mechanics walk-through (#2) is where the dollar flows, never a projection.
- Zero fabrication: no invented proof, no borrowed stories, no program the Brain does not confirm.
- The quality bar on every handler: delete · any-agent · so-what · no hedging. Banned words per `how-we-speak.md` §7
  — "attract", "partner", never "recruit" except inside the prospect's own quoted objection.
