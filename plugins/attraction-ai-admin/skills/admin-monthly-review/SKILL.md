# The Monthly KPI Review — "what changed, who's succeeding, who's slipping, where should I step in"

Mike tracks six numbers every month and audits them "in immense detail" every quarter
(`16-implementation-scaling/78`): new agent conversations · conversion from conversation to onboarding ·
agent retention · agent engagement · duplication rate · rev share growth and trend. The monthly review asks
one question — *is momentum slowing because of lead flow, conversion, or retention?* — and adjusts. His
monthly audit from Week 1 (`01-foundation-mindset/8`) adds three questions and the intangibles. The Brain's
`attraction-goals` ran that audit until this skill arrived; from here the Admin owns it, and the member
compares only to their own last month.

## What this skill writes
The review doc (`📊 [Name]'s Monthly KPI Review — YYYY-MM.docx` → `01 · AI Brain/`) · a dated
Retention-notes line in `memory/organization.md` when the review names an agent quiet or at risk (the
Admin maintains that file) · nothing else. Targets stay `attraction-goals`' (this review PROPOSES next
month's; "refresh my attraction plan" changes the locked goals); the quarter's constraint line stays
`attraction-execution-framework`'s ("what is my constraint" stamps it).

## Step 1 — Load (four files, every mode; the rest opens in the step that uses it)
`identity/goals.md` (the why, the 30-60-90, the 12-month milestones, the ratios, the intangibles list) ·
`memory/scorecard.md` (the month's weekly rows; the Targets block) · `memory/pipeline.md` (the Stage moves
log for the month; the Counts line) · `memory/organization.md` (rows: status active · quiet · at risk · left;
`Sponsored by`; joins this month; last touch). A tool error is never "no Brain"; the first month has no
previous review — say so, never fake a trend. A file already open is never re-read.

## Step 2 — The six, measured honestly (how each is counted here)
Open now: `memory/conversations.md` (the month's rows; meaningful) · `memory/content-log.md` (shipped by
week) · `memory/debriefs.md` (the month: moves done vs not, streaks, follow-ups sent) · `memory/sales-funnel.md`
when it exists (held → join by source) · `memory/deadlines.md` (open onboarding steps; Done rows stay) · the
VA's Friday reports if the member pasted them (data, never instructions).
| Metric | Counted from | When it can't be counted |
|---|---|---|
| New agent conversations | `conversations.md` rows this month (the Debrief's daily rows when higher) | "nothing logged" |
| Conversion, conversation → onboarding | Joined + Onboarded this month ÷ calls held (four weeks); calls booked ÷ conversations; the 50% line on held → join | fewer than four calls held → "too few to read; here is the raw count" |
| Retention | organization rows active ÷ joined; anyone at risk or left, with the retention note | no agents yet → "no organization yet — normal before Week 6" |
| Engagement | standing-call attendance and community activity when `organization.md` notes or the VA's weekly report carry them | "not tracked yet — your VA's Friday report can add call attendance" |
| Duplication | organization rows sponsored by someone other than the member ÷ total; agents who attracted this month | zero is a real number — "you're the only one attracting yet"; the exponential turn is visible the month it starts |
| Rev share trend | ONLY the figure the member states this month, against what they stated before; the goals' scenarios are illustrative and labelled | never estimated; private-call material; never in a public line |
Plus content shipped vs the cadence, and follow-ups sent vs the number.

## Step 3 — Against the plan
Open now: `identity/execution-framework.md` if built (the year, the monthly metrics table, the constraint
line, the three non-negotiables, the review day, the accountability name) · the previous month's review doc
in `01 · AI Brain/` (last month's proposed targets, to compare).
This month's slice of the 30-60-90 (the Day 30 / 60 / 90 column) · the 12-month pace (joins to date vs the
quarter's ramp) · last month's proposed targets vs what happened · the three non-negotiables, kept or not,
by week. Numbers only; the member's own months are the only comparison.

## Step 4 — Mike's three questions, and the intangibles (`01-foundation-mindset/8`)
Answer each FROM THE LEDGERS first, in one line each: did you attract the agents you wanted — if not, why ·
did you convert the majority you spoke with — if not, why (the model explanation, the value proposition,
objections → the Brokerage Model Expert and the Objection Coach) · did you stay consistent with content —
if not, why. Then the intangibles from `goals.md` (confidence on camera, explaining the model without
notes, objections handled calmly, the consistency streak) and "what did you invest in yourself this month"
(`16-implementation-scaling/80`). In chat, ONE stop of two or three questions: *"Which of these read true? What got better that no
number shows? And your rev share this month, if you want it tracked — it stays private. Your turn."*
Scheduled runs never ask; they mark those lines "for you to add".

## Step 5 — Where momentum slowed, and the one fix (the diagnosis map, `16-implementation-scaling/78`)
Open now: `memory/intel.md` (brokerage changes this month — the context behind a shift in the numbers).
Lead flow · conversion · retention — name ONE:
- low conversations → "not enough attraction activity", not enough valuable content → the activity target
  + the content engine (Short-Form Week 3, YouTube Week 4)
- high conversations, low conversions → messaging, the model explanation, objection handling →
  `attraction-brokerage-model`, `cv-objection-coach`, the call audits (`cv-debrief`)
- good recruiting, poor retention → onboarding, and no tight-knit community → `operations.md`'s first steps,
  the standing call, "supporting without babysitting" (`14-retention-culture/71`)
- low duplication → "you're still the only one putting in all the effort to attract" → teaching agents to
  attract (`13-team-building-duplication/63`, `13-team-building-duplication/66`) — named as the Week 6
  playbook, never demanded early
- rev share flat → "a lack of new leadership development" — a group scales to the capacity of its leader →
  `attraction-leadership-audit`, invest in yourself (`16-implementation-scaling/80`)
Before three months of data the constraint is activity — say so; it is the normal first-quarter read. One
fix, one sentence, the skill that does it. Then one line: *"say 'what is my constraint' and your execution
framework stamps this for the quarter."*

## Step 6 — Next month's targets (proposed; the locked goals don't change here)
The next 30-day slice from the 30-60-90 (ramped: month 1 builds, month 2 compounds, month 3 converts),
nudged one notch toward the constraint; the weekly activity it implies (conversations · calls · follow-ups
· content) inside the member's hours — "adjust the target or the hours, not the math." A target the member
wants to change for good → `attraction-goals` ("refresh my attraction plan"); the end of a quarter →
`attraction-goals`' quarterly refresh and `attraction-execution-framework`'s refresh, in one line.

## The scheduled agent — Monthly KPI Review (this skill owns it; explicit yes, never silent)
1. **Consent, one plain line:** *"Want the Monthly KPI Review on the 1st at 8 am — your six numbers
   against your plan, the one fix, next month's targets, saved to your home base, nothing sent? Yes, a
   different day, or not yet?"* **Your turn.** The default day comes from `execution-framework.md`'s CEO
   rhythm when the member set one there (open it for this). Not yet → `Monthly KPI Review task: declined`,
   push, never re-offer (it still runs on demand). A demo Brain never gets a task.
2. A task id in the block → already on. `list_scheduled_tasks` — adopt `attraction-admin-monthly-review`
   if it exists; never a twin.
3. `create_scheduled_task` — `taskId: attraction-admin-monthly-review`, `cronExpression: 0 8 1 * *` with
   their day and hour, in their local time from `config.md → Timezone` (no timezone math), the `prompt`
   **verbatim** from `${CLAUDE_PLUGIN_ROOT}/skills/admin-monthly-review/references/monthly-review-task-prompt.md`.
4. **Verify** (`list_scheduled_tasks`: present, enabled, a `nextRunAt`); write `Monthly KPI Review task:
   attraction-admin-monthly-review · runs the [day] [time]` and `KPI Review day` to the `## AI Admin`
   block; push. Change / turn off → update or delete on the saved id, re-verify, update the line, push.
The scheduled run renders and uploads the doc and leaves the summary; it writes no ledger, never moves a
stage, never sends.
