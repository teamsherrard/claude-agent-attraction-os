# Playbook Specs — part of the ds-playbook skill

Read in full before building any page.

## 1. THE THREE SHAPES — WHEN EACH ONE IS RIGHT

| Shape | When (free-vs-paid's format rule, `04-value-proposition/31`) | Pages | The reader keeps |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Worksheet** | one lesson the member can teach today; a tracker, a checklist, a script sheet, a weekly scorecard | 1 (two at most, as a spread) | the sheet on the fridge |
| **Playbook** | templates and a routine — three or more lessons that form a system | 12–30 | the system, with the real scripts and sheets inside |
| **Workbook** | the playbook where the doing matters more than the reading | 20–40 | their own answers, numbers, and 30-day plan |

Three or more RECORDED lessons → `ds-course` (the workbook per module is built there, on these mechanics).

## 2. PAGE ANATOMY — THE PLAYBOOK (the workbook adds the fillable spreads)

1. **Cover** — the canonical cover from `ds-product-mockup` (`product-cover-flat.png`, letter ratio),
   reused exactly; or designed here (rules in §6) and announced as canonical.
2. **Imprint page** — the product name · "for partners of [organization]" (or the member's own line) ·
   version and date · the member's name and role · the compliance line (brokerage display · licence
   where required · any required disclaimer verbatim) · "© [year] [member]" · one line: "this is my own
   method — use it, share it with your agents when you lead your own".
3. **Welcome** — the member's headshot in the Design System's frame; who this is for (the primary type
   of agent from the Book); why the member built it (one journey line, former brokerages never named);
   one real proof line if the Book has one; what to do first.
4. **How to use this playbook** — three to five steps ("read section 1 tonight · run the sheet on
   Saturday's open house · bring your numbers to Tuesday's call"); the tab colours explained in one line.
5. **Contents** — built LAST; section names with their tab colours and page numbers.
6. **Sections** (one per lesson, each opened by a full-page opener in the dark register with the
   section number, its name, and its one-line claim) — inside: **the claim** (what you can do after this
   section, as a skill statement) · **the steps** (numbered, verb-led, the member's words) · **the figure**
   (the script, the template, the sheet — set as the real thing) · **the example** (the member's own
   story or a partner's result with consent) · **"do this now"** (one action, with a checkbox).
7. **The checklist page** — 8–14 verb-led items grouped under 2–4 phase headers, real checkbox
   squares, one page, its own compact footer (member · organization · where to ask); the FRIDGE TEST.
8. **The worksheets** — every fillable sheet the training asks for (§4), each one page.
9. **The plug-in page** (`13-team-building-duplication/65`) — "You're not doing this alone": the weekly
   call (day · time · link), the community (what it is · how to get in), who to message when stuck, the
   first three things to do this week, the member's one line. From the Book's How You Operate chapter;
   anything unset becomes an "ask me" line.
10. **Back cover** — the organization's logo (or the member's), the member's name and role, contact in
    full plain text, the compliance line, the one-breath why from the Book.

**The workbook** adds, after each section: a fillable spread (3–6 prompts with lines; a scorecard or
tracker where the section has numbers), a **"my numbers"** page (the member's own tracking sheet, empty
cells), and a **30-day page** that mirrors the Book's first-30-days table (week · what the partner does ·
what the member does · done when) with a checkbox per row.

## 3. PRINT MECHANICS (the print-kit rules, applied to a document)

- **Trim US Letter 8.5×11 in portrait** (A4 210×297 mm via tweak); **bleed 0.125 in** on every edge for
  anything that runs to the edge (openers, tabs, grounds); **safe margin 0.5–0.75 in** for all type.
  300 DPI. Print-safe colour.
- **Type:** body 11–12 pt at 1.35–1.5 leading; headings roughly 3× body; a kicker above every heading
  (small caps or tracked, the accent colour); section numbers large on openers; the script face never
  body text; all-caps headings get a touch of extra letter spacing.
- **Reversed type** (light on the dark register) ≥ 8 pt at a regular-or-heavier weight; no hairlines,
  no fine white detail on dark.
- **Page numbers and a running footer** on every page after the imprint (the product name left, the
  page number right, or the Design System's footer rule).
- **Section tabs:** a 0.4–0.6 in tab on the outer edge of every page in a section, in that section's
  tint, the section name set vertically; the contents page repeats the colours. Tabs run into the bleed.
- **Grounds alternate:** openers dark, working pages on the light tint (never default white), figures on
  a plate.

## 4. FILLABLE COMPONENTS (designed, so a pen can use them)

| Component | Spec |
|---|---|
| Ruled lines | pitch 0.35–0.4 in; 0.5 pt in `colour.neutral` at low strength; never black; the first line starts 0.25 in under the prompt |
| Answer box | min 1.2 in tall for a sentence, 3 in for a paragraph; a 0.75 pt rule or a tinted plate; the prompt inside the top edge |
| Checkbox | 0.18 in square, 0.75 pt rule, 0.12 in gap to the label; aligned to the text baseline |
| Scorecard / tracker table | header row in the tint; empty cells ≥ 0.3 in tall; columns sized to the longest expected entry; weekly rows with a date cell |
| Script sheet | the lines the agent says on a plate, the cue in the kicker colour, a blank line under each for their own version |
| Rating scale | 1–5 circles 0.2 in, labels under the ends only |

A fillable page keeps 60–70% of its area as writing space; a prompt with no room fails the page.

## 5. THE WORKSHEET (one page, the fridge test)

A title that says what the sheet does ("My Saturday open-house follow-up sheet") · 5–12 fields,
verb-led, in the order the agent does them · the lines and boxes from §4 · one "done when" line · its
own compact footer: the member's name · the organization · where to ask for help (the call or the
community line) · the compliance line only when the sheet will be posted publicly (it usually will — as
proof of what partners get). Exported as a page of the PDF AND as `worksheet-[n].png` through the
button.

## 6. THE COVER — RULES WHEN DESIGNED HERE (otherwise reused exactly)

The title is the promise: seven words or fewer, benefit- or outcome-led as a skill ("The Open-House
Pipeline Playbook"), the single largest element (≥ 3× body); the subtitle names the payoff or the
method ("the sheet, the script, and the 7 pm text"); the member's name and the organization's name;
the Design System's photo treatment on a real photo of the member, or a brand graphic; the year badge
small; the brokerage only where the display rule puts it on a cover. **THE THUMBNAIL TEST:** at 150 px
wide the title reads and the brand is recognizable — it becomes the mockup in the offer stack. No fake
badges, stars, or "bestseller" marks. Exported as `product-cover-flat.png` and announced as canonical.

## 7. THE THIN-LESSON RULE AND THE ECHO TEST

A lesson with no steps, no figure, or no example is laid out with what exists and flagged in the notes
file and in chat ("section 2 has the routine but not the sheet — paste the sheet and it becomes the
figure page"). The skill never writes the missing step, invents a number, or adds a generic tip. A
lesson pasted under a heading is not a page: every section has the claim, the steps, the figure, the
example (or a flag), and the "do this now".
