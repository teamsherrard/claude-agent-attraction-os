# Thumbnail Patterns — part of the ds-thumbnail-layout skill

Read in full before laying out any direction. Three sections: what Mike's lesson gives us (confirmed),
where his swipe file will go (PENDING), and the Studio's layout library (ours, built on the lesson).

## 1. THE RULES FROM THE LESSON (`08-youtube/97` — confirmed, citable, in layout terms)

| Mike's rule | What it means on the canvas |
|---|---|
| Click-through is the gate — a beautiful video nobody clicks gets 17 views | design for the click, not for the brand sheet; the thumbnail is judged in a feed at phone size |
| Curiosity · emotion · clarity | the word opens a gap (a pain to run from or a result to run toward); the face carries the emotion; title + thumbnail together say what the video is in seconds |
| Bold and emotional beats complex and clever | one face, one word block, one ground, at most one supporting element; nothing that needs a caption |
| Three to five words, large bold type — never a sentence | the Studio's ceiling is four (a fifth word is the one that dies at phone size); 120–200 px heading face |
| The thumbnail's text is different from the title's | the two-chances test: at most one meaningful word shared |
| A real facial expression that matches the title's emotion | the expression families: concern · surprise · confidence · delight — the brief names one per direction; the photo must show it |
| Contrast colours for attention (his purple grounds, white or yellow type) | the MEMBER's brand at its highest-contrast pairing from the Design System — never Mike's palette, never the brokerage's |
| Visuals support the title without repeating it | the supporting element adds (a guest, a product, a pointer) — it never restates the title's words |
| "This is me, I need to watch this" | the word and the face land with the Book's primary type of agent — their career stage, their pain |
| Three thumbnails per video — Test & Compare | three directions = three ideas = three layouts; YouTube picks |
| Review the click-through monthly; aim for the 6–10% band after a month; low → new title + new thumbnail | the notes file carries the 30-day reminder; re-cut mode keeps the winning style |
| Pay attention to which titles and visuals consistently perform | the notes file records which direction won so the member's style settles |

## 3. THE STUDIO'S LAYOUT LIBRARY (ours, built on Section 1 — pick by the direction's composition line)

Every layout: 1280×720 · the face a third of the frame (≈ 400–520 px wide), cut out, bottom-anchored,
full head with air, eyes toward the text · the text block on the opposite third · the ground from the
Design System · nothing in YouTube's two corners (bottom-right ≈ 190×70 px: the video length;
top-right ≈ 130×130 px: the hover icons) · nothing important inside 64 px of the sides or 36 px of the
top and bottom.

| # | Layout | Face | Text | Supporting element | Best for | Default expression |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | **Face right · word stack left** | right third, large | two or three stacked words, left-aligned, the strongest word in the accent | none, or a pointer toward the word | Problem · Model | concern (Problem) · confidence (Model) |
| L2 | **Face left · word stack right** | left third | stacked words, right-aligned | none | Situation · Future | surprise (Situation) · confidence (Future) |
| L3 | **Two faces · the word between** | the member left, the guest right, matching scale, both bottom-anchored | one or two words centred between them, on a plate | the guest IS the element | Interview | delight (the member) · the guest's natural shot |
| L4 | **Big word · small face** | bottom corner, smaller (≈ 300 px), looking up at the word | ONE huge word or question (160–220 px) across the top two thirds | none | Model ("WORTH IT?") · Problem (the mistake named) | concern or surprise |
| L5 | **"This is you"** | right third, the member looking or pointing at the text | the "this is you" phrase on a plate at the member's eye line | a plain arrow or circle | Situation | surprise |
| L6 | **The number** | left third | a non-money number large, the noun under it ("5 / MISTAKES", "90 / DAYS") | none | Problem · Future · Interview (the guest's "FIRST DEAL · 90 DAYS") | concern (mistakes) · delight (results) |

**Rules that hold in every layout:** three directions use three different layouts · the number in L6 is
never money, a split, a cap, stock, or rev share · L3's guest photo only with consent noted in the
brief · the member's logo chip only where the Design System's logo card puts it and the brief's logo
rule allows it; the brokerage mark only where the Book's Compliance chapter requires it on video
packaging · a treated video frame may replace the brand ground (duotone wash, darkened toward the dark
anchor, a scrim under the text) — never a raw frame under text.

## 4. THE SCORE THE BRIEF USED (re-run it as your self-check — plain words)

The brief scored each direction 0–2 on nine criteria (18 max): curiosity · emotion · clarity · text
differs from the title · three or four words (never five) · the expression matches · contrast · simplicity · mobile
legibility. After layout, re-check the last five on the canvas — they are the ones a layout can lose:
the words still differ from the title; four words or fewer; the photo shows the named expression; the
pairing is the brand's highest contrast; the phone row at 320 px reads instantly. A direction that
drops a point on the canvas is fixed before it is presented, never explained away.

## 5. YOUTUBE TRUTH (platform facts, not taste)

- **Size:** 1280×720, 16:9, PNG or JPG, under 2 MB. The exporter writes PNG; a PNG that YouTube
  refuses for size is re-saved as a JPG by the member — nothing else changes.
- **Where it shows:** the home feed and search (desktop ≈ 360 px wide, mobile ≈ 320–360 px), the
  suggested column (≈ 168 px), the channel grid, and the share card. Judge at 320 and 168.
- **What YouTube draws on it:** the video length bottom-right; on desktop hover, the "watch later" and
  "add to queue" icons top-right; on some surfaces a progress bar along the bottom edge.
- **Test & Compare:** up to three thumbnails per video; YouTube serves them and keeps the one with the
  best watch-time share. Upload all three; the member reads the result after about a month.
- **The read:** the YouTube plugin's analytics skill reads the packaging after 30 days; this skill only
  builds the next set (re-cut mode) when the brief says so.
