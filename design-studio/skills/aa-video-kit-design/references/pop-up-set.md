# The Pop-Up Set — the pop-ups piece of the aa-video-kit-design skill — chapter 7's CALLS TO ACTION, on the video

Read in full before building the pop-up set, with `references/video-kit-specs.md` §1 (the zones and
the editor's text rule), §3 (the stage) and §4 (the video-safe rules). The set is part of the member's
OFFER KIT — the kit's chapter 7 names it ("the video pop-up set as PNGs with transparent backgrounds,
at 1080 by 1920 and 1920 by 1080") — and it is ADDED to the Riverside Video Brand Kit v3 contract,
never a replacement: the kit's canonical names, zones and eight `cta_pack` keys stay exactly as the
SKILL file states them.

You design five small pieces, each in two sizes, that the member or their editor drops onto a video:
the name card for the first three seconds, two pop-ups for the middle, two end cards for the last five
seconds. Every word is the kit's; your job is the placement and the brand.

## WHERE EACH PIECE SITS — the placement the rules allow

- **9:16 (1080×1920).** The face sits mid-frame; captions live in the lower quarter (y 1440 → 1920)
  — **nothing of this set ever enters it.** The platform draws its rail down the right edge —
  nothing past x 950 but a colour block.
  - **Upper third (default):** a panel x 60 → 950, inside y 160 → 640 (the top 160 px is platform
    chrome). Name card: y 180 → 400, two lines (the name 72 px bold · brokerage · city 48 px). Guide
    and call pop-ups: y 200 → 420, one or two lines at 72 px, the keyword in caps. End cards: a
    taller panel y 180 → 620 — line 1 the title or headline (72 px, two lines at most), line 2 the
    ask (64 px), line 3 the stamp (44 px).
  - **Side band (the tweak):** a vertical panel on the left, x 60 → 520, inside y 640 → 1400 — for a
    member whose framing puts their face high; the same words, stacked. Never the right side (the
    rail), never below y 1400.
- **16:9 (1920×1080).** The face sits upper-centre; the lower third is the editor's band for
  everything (`video-kit-specs.md` §1).
  - **Lower third (default):** the kit's banner geometry — x 160 → 1760; the pop-ups and the name
    card y 770 → 920 (one line at 64 px; the name card's second line at 40 px inside the same 150);
    the end cards y 740 → 960 (title 64 px · ask 56 px · stamp 32 px). Nothing starts above y 740.
  - **Side band (the tweak):** a panel x 96 → 700, y 200 → 880, stacked words — never across the
    centre.
- **The face is sacred** in both: nothing in the middle of the frame, ever. The stage
  (`video-kit-specs.md` §3) under every frame while designing shows you where the face is.
- **Safe margins:** 5% from every edge (54 px on 9:16 sideways, 96 px on 16:9), the right 12% clear
  on 9:16.

## THE RULES EVERY PIECE OBEYS (the kit's `Pop-up rules:`, restated)

- **Six words or fewer, two lines at most** — count them on every pop-up and the name card; a cell
  longer than six words is the kit's to shorten (say so), never yours to cut without saying which
  word went.
- **Three seconds** on screen — the piece must read in one: type floors 64 px on 16:9, 72 px on
  9:16; the stamp 32 / 44 px; contrast carried in lightness; panels 100% opaque; no hairlines.
- **Never over the face; never in the lower quarter of a vertical frame** — the zones above, and
  only those.
- **The name card in the first three seconds** of every video: the kit's bracket exactly — `[First
  Last · Brokerage · City]` — the brokerage as the kit spells it (the compliance display form), the
  city as the kit prints it.
- **The stamp on every end card** — the kit's closing line, verbatim, the third line; never on the
  pop-ups or the name card (they are on screen for three seconds; the end cards hold).
- **THE REGISTER:** chapter 2's `The register:` — at the open register no public line says "join
  me"; the kit's cells are already in the right register; copy them. Never add a line, a tier, a
  headline or a status of your own.
- **No public line says what the model pays, ever;** nothing in this set carries a split, a cap,
  revenue share, stock or an income figure; no ranking word; no other brokerage's name or logo.
- **THE VERBATIM LAW:** the table's cells, exactly — the keyword in caps as printed, the `·`
  separators kept or turned into a line break (the only change allowed), nothing re-ordered,
  nothing added.

## THE LOOK — the kit's brand, the kit's panel

The pop-up set shares the Video Brand Kit's language so a video reads as one brand: the same panel
colour and accent, the same edge style (`bannerEdge`), the same headline font (or the editor font
when the brand font will not load — say so in the notes), the same corner radius and accent stripe.
The name card carries the logo mark (or the initials tile when the Design System has no mark — never
an invented logo) at its left end; the pop-ups carry a leading glyph from the kit's icon family
(document for the guide, phone for the call); the end cards carry the mark and the member's real
headshot as a small circle only when it is in the Design System or the brief (never a stock,
illustrated or generated face). The energy level from the Design System's direction sets how loud
the shapes are, never whether words are in them.

## THE RUN — the masters, ONE checkpoint, then everything

1. Read chapter 7's table and `Pop-up rules:`, chapter 2's register, the stamp, chapters 1, 3, 4 and
   5 for the cross-checks; the brief and the Design System. **The intake form is nearly empty:** one
   confirmation line (*"From your kit: the name card [First Last · Brokerage · City] · the guide
   pop-up 'Comment GUIDE · free guide' · the call pop-up '[the cell]' · the end cards [the guide's
   title] / [SECTION 10's headline] · stamp set — say the word to change any of these"*), then only:
   where your face usually sits in your vertical videos (middle, or high — this picks the upper
   third or the side band), anything to avoid. **"Your turn."** "Just make it" = zero questions.
2. **THE MASTERS:** the 9:16 name card and the 9:16 guide pop-up, each on its stage. Show both.
3. **THE ONE CHECKPOINT:** *"These two set the look for the whole pop-up set — your name card and
   your guide pop-up, as they'll sit on a vertical video. Say yes, or name the one change."* Wait.
   The only stop.
4. On the yes: the 9:16 call pop-up and the two 9:16 end cards → the five 16:9 pieces →
   `pop-up-notes.md` → the export page → the hand-off, in one flow; "say go" phrasing is BANNED;
   never more than ~6 full-size frames per render; after every render, list the frames that exist
   against the ten names and re-render only what is missing.
5. Every frame WITH its stage from its first render, in its own row of the board (row "0 · Pop-up
   set", above the kit's rows when both are built in one session: the five 9:16, then the five 16:9).

## TWEAKS (the panel: plain labels; shared tokens with the kit when both are on the board)

- **popupPlacement** *(Upper third / Side band, default Upper third — the vertical set; the
  horizontal set stays in the lower third unless Side band is chosen for both)*. It moves a piece
  between the two zones the rules allow and nowhere else.
- **accentColor** · **panelTheme** · **bannerEdge** · **energyLevel** · **showHeadshot** — the kit's
  controls, shared.
Never a tweak that rewrites a cell, drops the stamp, or distorts the logo.

- On 9:16 nothing below y 1400, nothing past x 950 but a colour block, nothing over the face; on
  16:9 nothing above y 740 in the lower-third placement; the side band never across the centre?
- Type floors held (72 / 64 px; the stamp 44 / 32) and THE 25% TEST passed at 50% zoom on the stage?
- The register honoured — at the open register no "join me" anywhere; no line added by you; no
  figure, split, cap, rev-share, ranking or other brokerage anywhere?
- The real headshot or none; the logo mark or the initials tile; no stock, illustrated or generated
  face?
- `pop-up-notes.md` on the export page with every file's words, zone and timing, the rules line
  verbatim; `KIT_REQUIRED` the ten names; the button's status line "complete"?
- The compliance state honoured: the stamp on both end cards when set; no export page and no push

- **THE ANY-LEADER TEST:** cover the name. The shapes may belong to anyone; the words may not — the
  keyword, the guide's title, the headline, the booking line are the member's. If a cell reads
  generic, the kit is thin there — say which line.
