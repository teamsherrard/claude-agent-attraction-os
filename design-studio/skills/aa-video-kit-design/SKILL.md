# Attraction Video Brand Kit (aa-video-kit-design) — overlays, not slates; the kit exports itself

You design the branded layer that sits ON TOP of the member while they talk to agents: the pieces a
real editor keeps in a template folder. Their AI Editing Studio (the **Riverside** editor, directed from
Cowork) places these exact files on the timeline, slides and pops them in, and types each episode's
words into the empty zones. So every piece must be pixel-real, video-safe, TRANSPARENT wherever the
speaker should show through, and named EXACTLY as specified — the editor registers the files by name
once and reuses them on every edit, so the names and the zones are a contract (the editor's Video Brand
Kit v3 shape), not taste. Built once per brand; used on every video; costs nothing at edit time. The
brand itself (logo, colours, fonts, headshot treatment) is already locked in the member's Design System
by `aa-logo-design` and `aa-style-sheet-design`; the social kit is `aa-brand-kit-design`'s; a thumbnail
is a `yt-thumbnail` brief pasted into the Brand HQ project. This kit can run any time after the style
sheet exists — most members run it in Week 3, the week their editor is installed.

**WHERE THIS RUNS — CHECK FIRST.** Claude Design only. Anywhere else, say exactly: *"This one only
works in Claude Design. Open claude.ai/design, open your Brand HQ project, attach your Design System
and your Brain Book, and type: 'design my video brand kit'."* — and stop.

## THE ONE RULE THAT CHANGED (read this before designing)

**These are overlays, not full-screen slates.** A talking-head video never cuts away to a poster: the
viewer keeps seeing the member, and the brand arrives as a banner across the lower part of the frame,
a name strip in the corner, a pop-up beside a key word, a call-to-action that appears while the member
is saying it. Every piece except the optional END CARD and the YouTube END SCREEN is a **full-canvas
transparent PNG with the element drawn in its zone and nothing anywhere else.** If a piece would cover
the member's face, it is wrong. If a piece has words baked into a zone the editor is supposed to fill,
it is wrong. If it only makes sense on its own and not over a person, it is wrong. The PREVIEW SHEETS
exist so you and the member judge every piece over a real frame.

## WHERE THIS FITS (the two-layer hand-off to the editor)

**Layer 1 — pixels.** You design; the member clicks **Download the kit** on the board's last page and
gets ONE zip of the PNG assets below → they drop them into their workspace's brand folder
(`02 · Brand/Video Brand Kit/`, Drive or OneDrive) or straight into their Riverside media panel → in
Cowork they say **"set up riverside"** (the first time) or **"register my video brand kit"** (a kit
added later), which registers each file once. During every edit the editor places them by its format
rules (9:16 → the top zone; 16:9 → the lower third), animates them, and types the episode's words into
the empty zones.

**Layer 2 — parameters.** Captions can't be imported as a file — the editor styles them from settings
— so you also produce the **Video Brand Spec**, saved INTO the kit folder as a text file the editor's
setup reads (`video-brand-spec.md`), beside the designed one-pager.

These are deliberately STATIC PNGs (the engine notes in the reference): the editor itself slides, pops,
and fades each piece in and out, so a still file becomes a moving graphic on the video. The only moving
pieces the editor accepts are full-frame and opaque — an animated end card and a logo sting — and
neither is built in this Studio; the reference says how a member who makes them elsewhere drops them in
under their canonical names.

## THE ASSETS — CANONICAL FILE NAMES (the sync contract with the editor — do not rename)

Every asset is its own frame at EXACT size, drawn as ONE inline SVG (`<svg data-file="…">`, see THE
EXPORT PAGE); the kit's own Download button renders it to a PNG of exactly that size. The export names
are the CONTRACT the editor registers — keep them exact. "Transparent" means the FULL canvas is exported
with true alpha: only the drawn element is opaque.

**RENDER RULE for every overlay frame (hook, name strip, emphasis, CTA, CTA pack, icons) — apply it
literally:** the frame has NO background fill. Do not set a frame colour, a body/background colour, a
white or black fill, a gradient, a drawn checkerboard, or a grey "transparent" pattern of any kind — a
drawn checkerboard is PIXELS and ships on the video. If the tool asks for a frame background, choose
none/transparent. The ONLY opaque pixels in the export are the drawn element itself: the panel, its
accent stripe, the logo mark, and any baked text. The Download button writes PNG with true alpha; there
is no menu export to choose, and JPG never enters the picture. While designing, view the frames on a
mid-grey BOARD background so white type stays visible, but never put that grey inside the frame as a
fill. The ONE design-time exception is THE STAGE (the reference's board section): a stand-in video
frame drawn as a layer UNDER the piece inside every overlay frame, switched by the single project tweak
"Show stage" and never inside the SVG. Nothing else ever fills an overlay frame.

## THE CTA PACK — the eight asks, in attraction wording (the same eight keys the editor reads)

The same banner template as the primary CTA, one file per ask a leader makes on camera, so the editor
can pop up the RIGHT one the moment they say it. Eight variants under the editor's eight keys —
`subscribe` · `comment` · `guide` · `call` · `book` · `dm` · `link` · `follow` — the words baked
VERBATIM from this table with the real values substituted (the keyword, the guide's name and keyword,
the booking link, the @handle, the website — from the Book and intake Q4), NO compliance line, a
LEADING GLYPH from the icon family at the left end, identical outer geometry so the zone and animation
match. **The Brain's own words win:** when the Book or the member gives the exact line they say ("Drop
PARTNER below"), bake that instead of the default; a value that is missing means DROP that detail from
the variant, never render a bracket. When the primary ask equals a pack variant (a `book` CTA), still
ship both files — the editor picks by transcript; they differ by the compliance line. Asks ≤ 34
characters on 16:9, ≤ 20 on 9:16; an ask over 22 characters takes the two-row layout (the reference).

## BUILD ORDER (never more than ~6 full-size frames per render — kits rendered all at once die mid-render)

Build in stages, self-checking each stage hard, and roll straight through without stopping to ask —
the turn never ends at a stage, and "say go" phrasing is BANNED. Build every overlay frame WITH its
stage from its first render and drop it straight into its row (THE BOARD, in the reference): a frame
added without a stage is the one that ends up floating.

1. **Stage 1a — the 16:9 core:** hook banner, name strip(s), emphasis pop-up, the primary CTA banner,
   then `preview-16x9.png`. Look at the preview at 50% zoom before moving on: if a piece covers the
   face, crowds an edge, or reads as a poster, fix it now. **Stage 1b — the 16:9 CTA pack:** the eight
   variants cloned from the primary, then `preview-cta-pack-16x9.png`.
2. **Stage 2a / 2b** — the same split for 9:16 (the short asks), then `preview-9x16.png`.
3. **Stage 3** — the twelve icon frames, the end screen, the optional end card, the spec page with its
   text in the export block, the full numbered file manifest, and THE EXPORT PAGE with the Download
   button.
4. **Stage 4 — the board finish:** every frame in its row of the layout map, scratch frames deleted,
   "Show stage" ON, zoomed to fit, the overview row on top. Present the preview sheets first, then tell
   the member to click **Download the kit** on the last page and read you the status line — it must end
   in "complete".

After EVERY render, list the frames that exist against that stage's file list; if any are missing or
blank, re-render only the missing ones and continue — never restart the stage or the kit, and never
move to the next stage with a gap. You cannot see the exported files' transparency yourself: the
member runs the TRANSPARENCY CHECK in the hand-off.

## COMPLIANCE — THREE STATES, NEVER TWO (three pieces carry the line — and only three)

The **primary CTA banner**, the **end card**, and the **name strip** are the public-facing pieces that
carry the member's compliance details, from the Book's Compliance chapter (the render of the Brain's
compliance file, whose first line is `Status:`): the brokerage name exactly as the display rule says
(and the organization's name paired with it the way the rule says — "[Team], brokered by [Brokerage]"
where that is the rule); the licence number where the rules require it on video — on the CTA banner
and the end card, never a third line on the strip; REALTOR® marks only if the Book confirms
membership, "BROKER" only when licensed as one, the regulated title where the region has one; and no
absolute or fair-housing-risky claim anywhere ("best", "#1", "top team", "fastest-growing",
"guaranteed"). The eight CTA-pack variants, the hook banner, the emphasis pop-up, and the icons carry
NO compliance line, brokerage, or licence — the pack pops for 4–5 s mid-video and the primary banner
plus the name strip already cover the video.

**THE INCOME RULE — nothing in this kit carries a production or income figure.** Not a baked word, not
a pill, not a sample line on a preview sheet, not the spec: no split, cap, stock, rev share, income,
earnings, or dollar figure, and no "CAPPED" status — the hook and emphasis zones ship EMPTY, and the
words the editor types come from the member's compliance-checked script, never from this kit. If the
Book's AI-likeness rule requires a disclosure line on screen when a clone clip is cut in, say so once
in the hand-off — the editor's final check applies it; it is not a kit piece.

- **Set or confirmed:** bake the line on the three pieces exactly as the rule says. Build, export,
  hand off.
- **NOT SET YET** (the brokerage name or the licence rule missing, or marked skip/confirm in the Brain):
  design the whole kit (the member needs it this week) but **do NOT build the export page and do NOT
  push anything to `02 · Brand/Video Brand Kit/`** — an overlay with a placeholder ships on every video
  and the editor cannot remove it, so nothing leaves until the line is real. Say once, plainly: *"Your
  kit is designed. Before it can be exported for your editor, your Brain needs your compliance basics —
  say 'set up my attraction compliance' there (three minutes), then come back here and say 'add my
  compliance line'. I'll stamp the three pieces and export."* Never bake a placeholder ("[Brokerage]",
  "[LICENCE #]"); never "if empty, proceed".
- **"Add my compliance line"** (the return trip): read the now-set rule, stamp the name strip, the
  primary CTA banner, and the end card, run the self-check, build the export page, hand off.
- Always: the two cardinal rules (never talk badly about another brokerage or another person — never a
  former brokerage on any piece); if a video the kit dresses ever becomes a paid ad to attract licensed
  agents, it may fall under Meta's Employment special ad category and the brokerage's rule on ads —
  say so. This is assistance, not legal advice.
