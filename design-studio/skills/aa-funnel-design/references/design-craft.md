# Design Craft — part of the aa-funnel-design skill

Read IN FULL before designing any page. The specs are the ones proven in the sister suite's funnel
skill; the audience is an agent tapping in from a Reel, a bio link, or a video description — on a phone,
sceptical, and tired of sponsors.

## BUILD IT LIKE AN EXPERT (specific specs, not vibes)

- **Grid & width.** Centre all content in a max-width column (~1140–1200px desktop) with equal side
  gutters; a 12-column grid; everything aligned to it. Colour bands run full-bleed; their inner content
  stays in the column. Nothing floats off-grid or drifts to one side.
- **Type scale (a real hierarchy).** Hero H1 huge and tight — ~64–80px desktop / ~36–44px mobile,
  line-height ~1.05, heavy, with ONE word in the accent (the Design System's `type.heading`). The
  pre-headline above it ~16–18px, letter-spaced or in the accent. Section H2 ~36–44px. Body ~17–18px at
  line-height ~1.6. A small **eyebrow label** above each headline (~13px, uppercase, letter-spaced, in
  the accent — "03 · THE PRODUCT"). Max two brand fonts; contrast with weight and size, not extra fonts.
- **Spacing & rhythm (8-pt system).** ~100–140px vertical padding per section on desktop (~56–72px
  mobile), consistent gaps, a steady rhythm down the page. Whitespace reads premium; cramped or random
  spacing is the #1 amateur tell.
- **Section rhythm — alternate, don't repeat.** Adjacent sections carry different grounds (the Design
  System's light tint → paper → the dark anchor or the brand colour → the tint) so each reads as its own
  beat; two-column blocks alternate image-left / image-right. Every section gets an eyebrow + the doc's
  `Headline:` — never a headline of your own.
- **Colour discipline (60/30/10).** ~60% the light ground, ~30% the brand colour and tints, ~10% the
  accent — reserved for the CTAs, the eyebrows, and key highlights so the buttons truly pop. Text is
  high-contrast (ink on light, paper on dark) — never grey-on-grey.
- **Components, consistent everywhere.** One radius scale from the Design System (cards and inputs ~16px
  or the brand's radius, buttons the brand's pill or 8px), one soft shadow for elevated cards and forms,
  1px hairline dividers, inputs with visible labels and comfortable height (~52px). Buttons get bold
  labels and a trailing arrow. The same components on the page, the pop-up, and the thank-you state.
- **Imagery treatment.** Consistent rounded corners + a subtle shadow on photos; a scrim behind any text
  over an image; never stretch, squish, or distort. The mockup gets a soft realistic shadow and sits large
  and balanced in the Product section.
- **Polish that signals "designed":** the eyebrows, section numbers (01 / 02 / 03), a short accent rule
  under headlines, one simple icon style, the doc's trust line as a slim strip under the hero button
  (plus the brokerage chip where required — never an invented rating).
- **Reference calibre:** the restraint and polish of a top studio landing page — confident, spacious,
  premium. Never a copy of a specific site.

**Amateur tells to eliminate:** flat default type with no scale · grey-on-grey text · everything
centre-aligned · cramped or uneven spacing · off-grid floating elements · inconsistent radii · clip-art
icons · too many colours · heavy drop-shadows · tiny body text · big empty dead zones · a stock
"happy agents" photo · a printed label ("Pull quote:", "Bullets:") anywhere on the page.

## ART DIRECTION — PREMIUM, ON-BRAND, CONVERSION-CLEAN

Pull the member's logo, colours, fonts, and toolkit devices through the whole page so it matches the
banners, posts, and guide they already have. Execute the page in the brand's direction (calm-premium,
bold-modern, editorial…) — backgrounds, accents, type, and finish consistent with it. The leader palette,
visibly distinct from the brokerage's own; the brokerage's mark only where the compliance stamp puts it.

- **One cohesive system** — one accent, one button style, one type hierarchy, one section spacing,
  buttons identical everywhere.
- **Strong hierarchy, generous whitespace** — one dominant headline per section, calm spacing, never a
  wall of text.
- **Real, legible imagery** — the member's video at the top (or their cut-out standing in for it), the
  cut-out (from the kit), the guide's canonical mockup, the offer stack object, the member's real photos
  (the weekly call, events, agents' wins — with their permission), the toolkit's devices, simple icons.
  Never repeat one photo to fill space.
- **Use ONLY the member's uploaded photos for realistic imagery — never fabricate it.** This tool can't
  fetch stock or render believable photos; with none, design with brand fields, gradients, the pattern,
  the logo, and the headshot cut-out. A clean brand-colour layout always beats a fake-looking crowd.
- **RESERVE ZONES — nothing overlaps.** Headline, subhead, video, form, button, photo, mockup, embed, and
  footer each get clear space with real gaps; text never runs under a photo, a video, or a button. The #1
  cause of a messy page.
- **Buttons look clickable** — solid high-contrast fill, generous padding, a clear label, consistent
  everywhere; the primary CTA the most prominent element on each screen, in the brand's brightest accent
  even if that is the one place it appears. A button that blends into its section kills conversion.
- **Balance every section — no awkward dead space.** In the hero, the headline + CTA on one side and the
  video on the other, both filling their side and vertically centred; the video or cut-out sized to
  anchor its half. A section that looks empty gets its content enlarged or its layout tightened.
- **Premium, not template-y** — polished, restrained, the kind of page that says "this person has a
  standard".

## THE HERO PER SHAPE (80% of the result)

- **Opt-in:** the doc's `Pre-headline:` small above the headline · the `Headline:` (the magnet's promise,
  verbatim) · the `Subhead:` · **the video block** (below) as the hero visual — large, premium, the first
  thing the eye hits beside the headline · the `Attraction line:` bold directly under the video · the
  button **"Get the Free Guide"** · the `Trust line:` small under the button. The guide's mockup lives in
  the Product section, not the hero (unless the doc's hero carries an `Image idea:` that names it small).
  On mobile the headline + CTA lead and the video sits right under them, full width; the attraction line
  stays glued to the video. A light directional cue (the member's gaze in the poster, an arrow) points at
  the button.
- **Partner Call booking:** the one line as the headline (the Sales system's), the outcome for the
  type of agent as the subhead, the member's cut-out large (this page is about a person), the button
  **"Book a call with me"** that scrolls to the calendar, the dated proof strip under it or nothing.
- **Workshop registration:** the workshop's promise as the headline, the date · time · format line as
  a designed strip, the member's cut-out (and the co-host's when there is one), the button
  **"Save my seat"**, a quiet "free · live on Zoom / at [venue]" line. Honest urgency only — a real
  date, a real seat count the member stated.

## THE HERO VIDEO BLOCK (opt-in — the member's face and voice at the top)

The doc's `Video:` line decides it:
- **A link** (YouTube, Vimeo, Loom — the 2-to-3-minute video sales letter or the 60-second why-join-me):
  a responsive 16:9 frame in the brand's radius with a soft shadow, a poster (the member's frame from the
  video, or their cut-out on the brand ground when no frame exists), an on-brand play button, the
  `Attraction line:` set bold directly beneath. A plain lazy `<iframe>` with the privacy-enhanced embed
  URL, `allowfullscreen`, **never autoplay with sound**, never a rival CTA on it.
- **`record the 60-second why-join-me`**: the member's cut-out headshot in the SAME footprint — a brand
  field behind it, the attraction line still beneath it (the doc wrote it; it reads as the member's
  promise either way), never an empty frame, never a "video coming soon" badge. The hand-off tells the
  member how to get the script and that "here's my video" swaps the embed in.
Both versions share one footprint so the swap never reflows the hero.

## THE CREDIBILITY AND SNEAK-PEEK BLOCKS (Who I Am · The Offer)

- **"Why partner with [first name]"** (Who I Am): the title verbatim as a sub-headline, the 6–8 bullets
  as a two-column list on desktop (one column on mobile) or small cards — one icon style or none, each
  bullet one line, the headshot large beside the three lines above them.
- **"A sneak peek inside [program]"** (The Offer): the title verbatim, the 6–8 bullets as cards on a
  tinted ground (or a numbered list), the program name + type as the section's strip above, the
  framework line as a designed one-line chain (step → step → step), the first-30-days line beneath it,
  the brokerage line small and quiet; the offer stack object beside the block when it exists. Nothing
  here gets a button — the opt-in CTA is the Product section's and the Opt-in section's.

## THE JOURNEY AND THE STATEMENT (The Journey · Why Join Me)

- **The Journey:** three stacked blocks or a three-step vertical timeline — where I was · what I tried ·
  what changed — each with a small eyebrow of its own, the doc's `Pull quote:` set large between or after
  them, a real photo with a scrim when the doc names one and the member supplied it.
- **Why Join Me:** the `Statement:` as a full-width quote band on the dark anchor or the brand colour,
  the member's cut-out or a real photo beside it, the `Pull quote:` as the band's lead line when the doc
  has one. One band, no button.

## THE PROOF SECTION (results · the YouTube block · the photo strip)

- **The results:** the doc's `Proof:` lines as quote-and-name rows or cards (or the doc's one honest
  "none yet" line set plainly — never padded, never a star rating).
- **THE YOUTUBE BLOCK** — only when the doc carries `YouTube:`: two or three responsive 16:9 embeds in a
  row (stacked on mobile), each a lazy `<iframe>` with the privacy-enhanced URL, the doc's one-line
  caption under each. No block, no empty row, when the doc has none.
- **THE PROOF PHOTO STRIP (the moving part of the opt-in page):** under the results, one horizontal row
  of the member's real photos (the doc's list, 8–12, consistent height ~200–240px desktop / ~140px
  mobile, rounded, even gaps) that glides slowly and continuously left — CSS-only: the track duplicated
  once for a seamless loop, `@keyframes` translateX from 0 to −50%, ~40–60s per loop, pauses on hover, a
  static row under `prefers-reduced-motion`. No JS carousel libraries. Fewer than ~6 photos → no strip,
  say so once. Agents' faces need the agent's OK (the doc says so).

## THE BOOK-A-CALL AREA (opt-in — the one secondary path, near the bottom)

A calm band after the Organization and before the final opt-in — never above the Product, never louder
than the opt-in CTA. Two columns on desktop, stacked on mobile:
- **Left:** the doc's `Headline:` · `What the call is:` and `What it isn't:` as two short paragraphs ·
  the three questions (`Bullets:`) · **"How it works"** as three numbered steps (01 / 02 / 03 with the
  doc's words) · **"What we'll cover"** as a tidy list (5–6 lines, one icon style or none).
- **Right:** **the booking embed** — the `Booking embed:` link in a lazy `<iframe>` (Calendly ·
  GoHighLevel · Cal.com; ~700px tall on desktop, full-width on mobile) in the brand's radius, with a plain
  text link under it as the fallback; when the link doesn't embed, the button **"Book a call with me"**
  (the doc's words) to the link instead, large, the accent.
- No countdown, no "spots left", no second button, nothing about compensation. The sticky mobile bar
  never carries this button.

## THE FAQ (opt-in — inside the Opt-in section, right before the button)

The doc's `FAQ:` pairs (three) as a CSS-only accordion (`<details>` / `<summary>`, the question bold, the
answer one line, the first open on desktop) or as three stacked question-and-answer rows — the tweak
decides. Compact: it sits between the top-3 recap and the button, never a section of its own, never a
"Frequently asked questions" heading of your own.

## THE OPT-IN POP-UP AND THE OPTIONAL QUALIFIER

The one modal: the doc's pop-up headline, `First name` · `Email` · `Phone`, the honest contact line
under Phone, the reassurance by the button, the submit. When the doc's `Optional qualifier:` line is not
`off`, ONE extra `<select>` — `years licensed` (a short range dropdown) or `where are you today` with the
three options the doc names — sits under Phone, same component style; when it is `off`, nothing. Never
a text box, never "brokerage", never "production".

## THE STICKY MOBILE CTA

A persistent bottom bar on phones carrying the opt-in CTA only (full-width, ~48px tall, the accent), in
the easy thumb zone — never covering the form's submit button, the booking embed, or the thank-you's
download button; never the call button.
