# Design Studio rewire brief — 2026-11-09 (binding for every `aa-*-design` skill)

The ten Claude Design skills were written for an older product: seven separate Week 2 documents, brand work in
Week 1, a "Brand HQ" project, and briefs from plugins that are not shipped. The product is now THREE FILES and
ONE design week. This brief is the contract every skill is rewired to. Where a skill's text contradicts it, this
brief wins.

## 1. The three files a member uploads to Claude Design (Week 3)

1. **The Week 3 Kit** — `Week 3 Kit — [First Last] — YYYY-MM-DD.docx`, built by the Brain in Week 2. A book: a
   cover, a CONTENTS page where every line is a link, eight chapters, each on its own page, under 25 pages. The
   kit is the only source of WORDS for every offer, page, post and pop-up piece. Its chapters and bands
   (bands are the sub-headings; `Label:` lines are bold lead-ins; every value is final copy):
   - **Chapter 1 — SUMMARY**: a table (What · Program · For · The promise · The method · What's free from day one ·
     What it costs · Why me · Next step) · YOUR LEAD MAGNET (the guide's outline) · YOUR SALES FUNNEL OUTLINE ·
     WHERE EVERYTHING LIVES · HOW TO USE THIS IN WEEK 3 (the upload and the order to ask for the pieces).
   - **Chapter 2 — YOUR OFFER**: THE PROGRAM (`Program:` · `The method:` · `The promise:`) · THE FIVE PROBLEMS IT
     SOLVES (five rows: pain → what you get → outcome) · WHAT YOU GET, AND WHAT'S FREE (the stack rows: what you
     get · what it does · free / included / discounted; `The free line:` · `The up-front cost line:` · `Honest
     value line:`) · PROOF, AND THE PROOF PLAN (`Proof:` bullets as consented · `Proof plan:`) · WHY NOW, AND WHAT
     MY PARTNERS CAN OFFER (`Why now:` · `What my partners can offer:` · **`The register:`**) · YOUR DIGITAL
     PRODUCT (`Product:` · `Format:` · `For:` · `Promise:` · `Outline:` · `Cover line:` · `Subtitle:` · `Three
     promises:` · `Version one, this month:` · `The full version:` · `Status:`).
   - **Chapter 3 — WHY JOIN ME**: THE STATEMENT (`One breath:` · `Sixty seconds:`) · THE ONE LINE, FOUR WAYS
     (`The promise:` · `Outcome-led:` · `Story-led:` · `Mechanism-led:` · `Why me:`) · THE WORDS TO USE (`The
     30-second explanation:` · `DM:` · `Caption:` · `Bio:` · `Video intro:` · `Comment keyword:` · `The free guide
     is cut from:`).
   - **Chapter 4 — LEAD MAGNET**: THE IDEA YOU SAID YES TO (`Title:` · `Promise:` · `For:` · `Why this one:`; the
     line "Your guide is its own file") · THE OUTLINE (`Hook:` three bullets · `Pages:` one bullet per page, each
     ending `· picture: …`) · **`DESIGN BRIEF — [GUIDE NAME]`** (`What to build:` · `Who reads it:` · `The brand:`
     · `The cover:` · `The pages:` · `The mockup:` · `When it's done:`).
   - **Chapter 5 — SALES FUNNEL**: `SECTION 1 — HERO` … `SECTION 11 — THE OPT-IN`, then THE OPT-IN POP-UP, THE
     THANK-YOU PAGE, FOOTER — the page word for word, every section with its own `Headline:`; then THE WORDS FOR
     THE GUIDE (`CTA button:  "Get the Free Guide"` · `Keyword:  GUIDE` · `The call:` · `The DM and the delivery
     email:` · `Words we don't use:`) and HOW TO BUILD THE PAGE (`In Claude Design:` · `If a web person builds
     it:`).
   - **Chapter 6 — BIOS**: INSTAGRAM (150) · FACEBOOK (101 intro · the About) · LINKEDIN (220 headline · the
     About) · YOUTUBE (1,000) · TIKTOK · X · THREADS · GOOGLE BUSINESS · YOUR SITE · EMAIL SIGNATURE — paste-ready,
     with character counts. Not a design input except the brand-kit banners' copy spine.
   - **Chapter 7 — DESIGN ASSETS**: THE JOIN MY TEAM ONE-PAGER (`Headline:` · `Subhead:` · `The promise:` · `What
     you get:` · `How it works:` · `Proof:` · `Why me:` · `Call to action:` · `Compliance:`) · THE OFFER STACK
     (`Stack title:` · `Items:` → the rows of chapter 2 · `The free line and the up-front cost line:`) · THE
     PRODUCT MOCKUP (`Product:` · `Format:` · cover line, subtitle, three promises as chapter 2 · `Status line:`) ·
     THE CALL DECK, SLIDE BY SLIDE (ten `Slide N — …:` lines; slide 9 never carries a figure) · THE WELCOME PACK
     (DAY 1) (`Welcome letter:` · `Day 1 checklist:` · `First lesson:` · `Calendar:`) · **SOCIAL GRAPHIC POSTS**
     (six posts — `Post 1 — The guide:` carousel 5 slides · `Post 2 — Five problems, five fixes:` carousel 7 ·
     `Post 3 — My story:` carousel 5 · `Post 4 — What you get:` single · `Post 5 — Proof:` quote card · `Post 6 —
     Book the call:` story + single; each with `Slide N:` lines and a `Caption:`; carousels and singles 1080×1350,
     stories 1080×1920 with text clear of the top and bottom 250 px; the compliance stamp on the last slide or in
     the caption) · **CALLS TO ACTION** (a three-column table — `Where it goes | Ask for the guide | Ask for the
     call` — rows: Caption, last line · Comment keywords · Video pop-up, mid-video · Spoken, in the video · End
     card, last 5 seconds · Story · Bio, the one link · DM, when someone comments; then `Pop-up rules:` six words or
     fewer, two lines, three seconds, never over the face, never in the lower quarter of a vertical video, the name
     card `[First Last · Brokerage · City]` in the first three seconds, the stamp on every end card) · **`DESIGN
     BRIEF — [PROGRAM NAME] OFFER KIT`** (`What to build:` · `Who sees each:` · `The brand:` · `The rules:` · `When
     it's done:`).
   - **Chapter 8 — YOUR NOTES**: the member's private pages (assumptions, how to create it, upline, hosting,
     content branches). **Never designed, never shown to an agent.**
   - The last line of the kit is the **compliance stamp** (`▸ COMPLIANCE`). It goes on every public piece.
2. **The guide** — `Lead Magnet — [Title]` (.docx, or the PDF the member exported): the lead magnet's words, a
   cover page, one page per section with a Title Case heading, checklists, fill-in lines, message bubbles, a
   closing page and a back page. The designer reads the words HERE; chapter 4 of the kit says what to build.
3. **The Design Package brief** — the block that starts `AGENT ATTRACTION DESIGN PACKAGE — [First Last] · issued
   [YYYY-MM-DD]`, handed over by the Brain's Week 3 brand chat (fields: the three brand skills in order with the
   skip rule · Brand name(s) · Leader brand vs selling brand · Tagline · Headshots · CREATIVE DIRECTION: Mood ·
   Energy · Type · Colours · Photos · Motifs · Avoid · Reference recipes · Examples · the compliance line, which may
   read NOT SET YET). Optionally the **Brain Book** ("the AI Brain file") for who the member is.

An older input that no longer exists must not be asked for: the Opt-In Funnel document, the Lead Magnet
Package, the Design Brief document, the Offer Stack Brief, the Why Join Me document, the Platform Profiles
document, the opportunity-deck brief (`cv-presentation`), the booking brief (`sales-booking-page`), the Model
Positioning Sheet as a required input. Where a skill keyed on one of them, it now keys on the kit chapter or band
named above. The Short-Form, Events and Admin briefs (`sf-carousel`, `ev-promo`, `ev-registration`,
`ev-runofshow`, `admin-newsletter`) belong to plugins that ship later: keep those branches as **"when that
document exists"** side doors, never a requirement, never the default path, never named by skill id to the member.

## 2. The week map (the cohort's, 2026-11-08 ruling)

Week 1 = the Brain only. Week 2 = the offer, the guide, the kit (words only, in Cowork). **Week 3 = ALL design
work, in this order inside one Claude Design project the member names themselves** (never "Brand HQ"): the Design
Package brief → the brand three (logo · style sheet · brand kit; skip the logo if they love it) → the offer kit
(one-pager, stack, mockup, deck, welcome pack) → the guide PDF with its cover → the opt-in page and the thank-you
page → the six social posts → the video pop-up set → the video brand kit (with the Riverside editor plugin).
Week 6 = events and the Value Vault. No skill may say "Week 1" or "Week 2" for design work.

## 3. The register (read once, obeyed everywhere)

Chapter 2's `The register:` line decides the words. **Join register** (`yes` or `unknown`): the program is what an
agent receives when they partner with the member; "Join My Team" wording is allowed where the kit uses it. **Open
register** (`no`): every public line is what the member teaches, their story and how they mentor; nothing is gated
on joining; no piece may read "join me and get X"; the product status line reads "Free to any agent who asks". The
kit's copy is already in the right register. **The skill copies it verbatim and never adds a join line, a tier, a
headline or a status of its own.** The band called THE JOIN MY TEAM ONE-PAGER is an internal name: what prints is
its `Headline:` line.

## 4. The small product (Mike's ruling: realtors are not course creators)

Version one of the digital product is **a template the member already uses plus ONE short recording** (10 to 15
minutes). The mockup is that version's cover: `Cover line:` · `Subtitle:` · `Three promises:` from chapter 2, as a
flat cover first, a 3D mockup only if the tool exports pictures. Never a course box, a bundle, a stack of binders,
a device wall or "12 modules" unless chapter 2's `Format:` says course AND its `Status:` says built. The Value
Vault skill's default piece is the one-recording playbook; the course and the ebook are side doors for a member
whose chapter 2 says so.

## 5. The verbatim law and compliance (unchanged, restated)

Every word on every piece comes from the kit or the guide. Nothing is invented, punched up, re-ordered or
"improved": no new claims, no new numbers, proof lines only as written, `(demo)` tags kept on demo builds. Never:
compensation, splits, revenue share, stock, income figures; ranking words or "best"; another brokerage's name or
logo; stock people. The compliance stamp (the kit's closing line, or the brief's compliance line) sits on every
public piece. If the brief's compliance line reads NOT SET YET: build, but say in one plain line that nothing
here may be published until the member says "paste my compliance link" in Cowork.

## 6. What the member says back in Cowork when a piece is done (the exact phrases; print them at the finish)

- Logo → **"the logo is done"** · Style sheet → **"the style sheet is done — colours and fonts locked"** · Brand
  kit → **"the kit is done"** (the Brain's brand direction records each).
- Offer kit, the six posts and the pop-up set (all saved to the Offer folder) → **"my offer kit is designed"**.
- The guide PDF → **"the PDF is done"**.
- The opt-in page, once it is live and test-submitted → **"my funnel is live"** with the link.
- The video brand kit → **"register my video brand kit"** (needs the Riverside editor plugin; without it the
  files simply wait in the Brand folder).
- Events and Value Vault pieces → the phrase their own plugin names; until it ships, "my [piece] is designed".
Files land in the member's drive folders when a Drive or OneDrive connector is attached ("push this to my
Drive"), otherwise the export list: brand files in `02 · Brand`, the offer kit, posts and pop-ups in `05 · Offer`,
the guide PDF and its cover and the page files in `03 · Content/Guides`, the video kit in `02 · Brand/Video Brand
Kit`. Say folder names in plain words ("your Offer folder"), never as a path.

## 7. Member-facing language

Members are often 60 to 75 years old and not marketers. Plain words, one question at a time, never a menu when the
phrase or the file already names the piece, never a skill id in a sentence to the member (skill names appear
only in the Start Here table under "type this"), never a file path spoken as a path, never "Brand HQ", never
"Cowork plugin" jargon beyond "your Brain" and "your Lead Magnet plugin". A missing input gets one warm line
naming the Week 2 phrase that creates it ("build my partner offer" · "launch my lead magnet plugin" · "my
attraction brand direction"), never a lecture.

## 8. What stays exactly as it is

The export page and its exporter (`references/export-page.md`, byte-identical across the skills that carry it —
the build asserts it) · the canonical cover rule · the kit-run pattern (masters → one checkpoint → the rest) ·
the three tests (authority · relatability · aspiration) · the tweak controls · demo mode · the quality bar · the
Design System file's structure (sections 0–12) · descriptions as `description: >` block scalars ≤ 1,024
characters, `name:` equal to the directory name, never a hyphenated word split across lines.

## 9. The two new modes

- **`aa-content-design` — "design my posts"** (also "design my six posts", "design post 3", "my story carousel",
  "my proof post", "my book-a-call story"): reads chapter 7's SOCIAL GRAPHIC POSTS and CALLS TO ACTION. Builds
  the six posts at their sizes, slide text verbatim, one ask per post exactly as the table gives it, the stamp
  on the last slide, captions handed back as one text file (`post-captions.md`) beside the PNGs. Post 3 ("My
  story") IS the five-slide story carousel the old skill called the "Why Join Me five-slider". The carousel craft
  references stay; the Short-Form carousel and the recognition posts stay as side doors for when those plugins
  ship.
- **`aa-video-kit-design` — "design my pop-ups"** (also "my video pop-up set", "my end cards", "my name card"):
  reads chapter 7's CALLS TO ACTION table and `Pop-up rules:`. Builds transparent PNGs at 1080×1920 and 1920×1080:
  the name card (`[First Last · Brokerage · City]`), the guide pop-up (`Comment [GUIDE] · free guide`), the call
  pop-up (the call's six-word line), and two end cards (the guide's title + comment line + stamp; the book-a-call
  headline + booking line + stamp). Placement obeys the rules: never the lower quarter of a vertical frame, never
  over the face (upper third or a side band), six words or fewer, two lines at most. The Riverside Video Brand
  Kit v3 contract (canonical names, zones, the eight `cta_pack` keys) stays as the kit's spec; the pop-up set is
  added to it, not a replacement.

## 10. Done means

Every skill: reads the three files by the names in §1 · says Week 3 (or Week 6) and nothing else · obeys §3–§5 ·
prints its §6 phrase at the finish · passes §7 · keeps §8 · its description re-written to name the kit chapters it
reads and its real phrases. The Start Here and the README describe exactly this, in this order, with the three
files at the top.
