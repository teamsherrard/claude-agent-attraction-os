# START HERE — The Agent Attraction Design Studio

Fifteen Claude Design skills that build and run the visual brand agents will follow — the brand of
YOU as a leader, not a listing brand. This page is the map. **Three skills ship with your Brain in
Week 1 (the Design Package); the rest arrive with the week that needs them.**

This is a Claude Design **skill set**, not a Cowork plugin. Claude Design cannot run plugins; it accepts
uploaded skill files. Your Brain (in Cowork) decides your brand *direction* and hands you a paste-ready
brief; Claude Design *builds* the visuals from it.

## THE FIVE GOLDEN RULES (everything depends on these)

1. **ONE project.** Create a single Claude Design project — your **Brand HQ** — and run EVERY skill
   inside it. That is how each skill finds your logo, colours, fonts, and headshot automatically.
2. **Attach two things to every chat:** your **Design System** (start with the Agent Attraction Design
   System file in this folder; after skill 2 it becomes *your* "[Name] Design System") and your
   **Brain Book** — "the AI Brain file", the one document your Brain built, in your workspace's
   `01 · AI Brain` folder (download the newest one, or let the skill read it through your Drive
   connector). Never a DEMO-watermarked Book.
3. **Build in order the first time.** 1 (logo) → 2 (style sheet) LOCK the brand — colours, fonts,
   your name and your organization's name, one brand or a separate leader brand, language. 3 (brand kit)
   and everything after inherit it and never re-ask. *(Love your logo already? Skip 1 — start at 2 and
   upload it; it is used exactly as-is, never redesigned.)*
4. **Set once, never again:** your brand language(s), your brand shape (one brand with a leader lane,
   or a separate leader brand), your organization's name, and your compliance basics (in your Brain:
   "set up my attraction compliance" — three minutes; nothing public exports until it is set).
5. **Say "push this to my Drive"** when a piece is approved — with a Drive or OneDrive connector the
   skill files everything into your workspace's `02 · Brand` folder; without one it hands you an
   organized export list. Every file lands in `02 · Brand`; that is where your editor, your thumbnails,
   and every later graphic read your brand from.

## SET-UP (ten minutes, once)

1. In claude.ai → **Customize → Skills → Upload a skill**, upload the three Week 1 files from `_dist/`:
   `01-ds-logo.zip`, `02-ds-style-sheet.zip`, `03-ds-brand.zip`.
2. Open **claude.ai/design**, create a project named **Brand HQ**, and attach
   `agent-attraction-design-system.md` (this folder) and your **Brain Book**.
3. In your Brain (Cowork), finish Setup through your brand direction — or say **"my attraction brand
   direction"** — and copy the **Design Package brief** it hands you.
4. Paste the brief into a new Brand HQ chat and type the first trigger below.

## WHERE FILES LIVE

Your Agent Attraction workspace (Google Drive or OneDrive) is home base — rename it whatever you like;

## WHEN SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG

- **"It forgot my brand"** → attach your Design System to the chat and say "use my design system".
- **Wrong colours or fonts appear** → same fix; the Design System always wins.
- **It asked me things my Brain already knows** → attach the Brain Book (newest) and paste the brief;
  the skills prune every question those two answer.
- **A piece looks off** → ask for the ONE change ("make the headline bigger"); skills change only what
  you ask, never rebuild.
- **"Where are my picture files?"** → Claude Design's Export menu has no picture option; skills 1 and 3
  put a **Download** button on the board's last page that packs every file into one zip. The style
  sheet exports as a PDF from the Export menu.
- **"It won't export my kit"** → your compliance basics are not set yet. In your Brain: "set up my
  attraction compliance" (three minutes), then back here: "add my compliance line".
- **It used my brokerage's colours** → say so; the leader brand stays visually distinct from the
  brokerage's palette by rule, with the brokerage mark only where your compliance rules require it.
- **A skill won't upload** → the file is a zip with no nested zips; if claude.ai rejects it, send the
  message to support ("support-diagnose" in your Support plugin) — it is usually a description-length limit.
- **Starting a brand-new chat?** → attach the Design System + Brain Book first. Always.
