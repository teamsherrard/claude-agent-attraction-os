# Design Studio — a Claude Design skill set, not a plugin

Claude Design (claude.ai/design) cannot run Cowork plugins; it accepts uploaded skill files. This
folder is the Agent Attraction Design Studio: the 10 `aa-…-design` skills that build and run the member's
LEADER brand — **a coaching brand for agents** (a coach, mentor or team leader who teaches agents),
never a realtor brand for buyers and sellers — plus the **Agent Attraction Design System** every skill
reads. Plan: `docs/plans/02-agent-attraction-os-master-gameplan.md` §3. House rules:
`docs/plans/BUILD-BRIEF.md`. **The binding contract for every skill: `_build/REWIRE-BRIEF-2026-11-09.md`**
— where a skill's text contradicts it, the brief wins. Recycled from the realtor Claude Design suite v2
(read-only, on the Desktop), with the realtor premise replaced by the leader premise; on 2026-10-10 the
set went from 14 to 10 (three kit skills replaced eight single-piece skills, and the video kit joined).

**Rewired 2026-11-09.** The ten skills were written for an older product: seven separate documents from
the member's Cowork plugins, brand work before the kit existed, a project with a fixed name, and briefs
from plugins that have not shipped. They now read **three files** (below), do **all design work in Week
3** inside one Claude Design project the member names, and print the exact Cowork phrase at the finish.
Retired document names and the old week claims were removed from every skill, the Design System, the
Start Here and this README; the Design System's §11 names the three files and the finish phrases. Two
owner rulings of the same date apply Studio-wide: **digital-only** (profile pictures, banners,
highlight covers, story and reel frames, a Zoom background, the email signature graphic, presentation
frames, the guide PDF, the opt-in page, posts, pop-ups, the video kit — no business cards, lawn or office signs,
flyers, postcards, letterhead, door hangers, brochures, bleed or CMYK) and **coaching-specific** (no
house / roof / key / skyline / "sold" motifs, no listing-flyer layouts, no "local real estate expert"
taglines, no MLS imagery, no brokerage-palette defaults; the member's face, the method's name and the
program's name carry the brand).

## The three files a member uploads (the brief's §1)

1. **The Week 3 Kit** — `Week 3 Kit — [First Last] — YYYY-MM-DD.docx`, built by the Brain
   (`attraction-week3-kit`) at the end of the offer and lead-magnet sittings. A book under 25 pages: a
   cover, a linked CONTENTS page, eight chapters each on its own page — 1 SUMMARY · 2 YOUR OFFER · 3 WHY
   JOIN ME · 4 LEAD MAGNET · 5 SALES FUNNEL · 6 BIOS · 7 DESIGN ASSETS · 8 YOUR NOTES (private, never
   designed) — closing with the compliance stamp (`▸ COMPLIANCE`). Bands are the sub-headings; `Label:`
   lines are bold lead-ins; every value is final copy. The kit is the only source of WORDS for every
   offer, page, post and pop-up piece.
2. **The guide** — `Lead Magnet — [Title]` (.docx, or the PDF the member exported): the lead magnet's
   words; chapter 4 of the kit says what to build.
3. **The Design Package brief** — the block that starts `AGENT ATTRACTION DESIGN PACKAGE — [First Last] ·
   issued [YYYY-MM-DD]`, handed over by the Brain's `attraction-brand-direction` (the three brand skills
   in order with the skip rule · brand name(s) · leader vs selling brand · tagline · headshots · CREATIVE
   DIRECTION · examples · the compliance line, which may read NOT SET YET). Optionally the **Brain Book**
   ("the AI Brain file") for who the member is.

Side doors that are never required and never the default path: the Short-Form carousel doc
(`sf-carousel`), the Events briefs (`ev-promo` · `ev-registration` · `ev-runofshow`) and the Admin's wins
brief (`admin-newsletter`) — plugins that ship later; a skill keys on them only "when that document
exists" and never names them by skill id to the member.

Every skill reads the three files first and asks only what they cannot answer, in plain language; when
it finishes it prints the exact line the member says back in Cowork (the brief's §6): **"the logo is
done"** · **"the style sheet is done — colours and fonts locked"** · **"the kit is done"** · **"my offer
kit is designed"** (the offer kit, the six posts and the pop-up set) · **"the PDF is done"** · **"my funnel
is live"** + the link · **"register my video brand kit"** · Week 6: the line the Events plugin names
(until it ships, "my event set is designed"), **"my digital product is built"** (the Brain's own phrase
— it records the product and rebuilds the kit) and **"my onboarding roadmap is designed"**. The register
(chapter 2's `The register:`) is read once and obeyed everywhere; the kit's copy is already in the right
register and no skill adds a join line, a tier, a headline or a status of its own (§3). Compliance is
three-state: the kit's stamp or the brief's compliance line on every public piece; NOT SET YET = build,
but nothing is published until "paste my compliance link" in Cowork (§5).

**The kit pattern (the three merged skills).** One directory, one SKILL.md holding the shared rules
(the three files first, the register, compliance, the verbatim law, exports, folders, the five golden
rules), a MODE ROUTER that never asks a menu when the phrase or the kit already names the piece, and
a KIT RUN that builds the pieces in a fixed order with ONE member checkpoint after the masters (the
offer kit: the canonical product cover and the transparent stack object; the content kit: post 1's cover
+ slide 2; the Value Vault: the cover and the first inside page), then everything else without
stopping. Each piece's craft lives in `references/<piece>.md`, moved verbatim from the retired skill it
came from, so a piece stays callable alone by its own phrase.

**The two new modes (the brief's §9).** `aa-content-design` — **"design my posts"** (also "design my six
posts", "design post 3", "my story carousel", "my proof post", "my book-a-call story"): chapter 7's SOCIAL
GRAPHIC POSTS and CALLS TO ACTION, six posts at their sizes (1080×1350; stories 1080×1920 with text clear
of the top and bottom 250 px), slide text verbatim, one ask per post, the stamp on the last slide,
captions as `post-captions.md` beside the PNGs. `aa-video-kit-design` — **"design my pop-ups"** (also "my
video pop-up set", "my end cards", "my name card"): chapter 7's CALLS TO ACTION table and `Pop-up rules:`
→ transparent PNGs at 1080×1920 and 1920×1080 — the name card, the guide pop-up (`Comment [GUIDE] · free
guide`), the call pop-up (the call's six-word line), two end cards; never the lower quarter of a vertical
frame, never over the face, six words or fewer, two lines at most. The Video Brand Kit v3 contract stays
as the kit's spec; the pop-up set is added to it.

## The week map (the cohort's, 2026-11-08 ruling)

The cohort's first week is the Brain and its second is the words (the offer, the guide, the kit — in
Cowork); **no Studio skill claims either of those weeks for design work.** Week 3 = ALL design work, in this order inside one Claude
Design project the member names: the Design Package brief → the brand three (logo · style sheet · brand
kit; skip the logo if they love it) → the offer kit (the product mockup first; one-pager, stack, deck,
welcome pack) → the guide PDF with its cover → the opt-in page and the thank-you page → the six social
posts → the video pop-up set → the video brand kit (with the Riverside editor plugin). Week 6 = events
and the Value Vault.

## Packaging and `_dist/` (the realtor suite's shape)

`_build/build.sh` packages each skill the way the realtor suite v2 does: a zip whose root entry is the
skill's folder — `<name>/SKILL.md` + `<name>/references/*.md` — when the skill has `references/` (all ten
do today), a bare `.md` with YAML frontmatter when it has none; no nested zips; no spaces, `&`, `(` or `)`
in zip-internal paths. The zips are numbered by the ORDER array in the script — `01`–`08` the Week 3
skills in the Start Here order, `09`–`10` the Week 6 two — and the numbers stay stable as later weeks
ship. It asserts every `description` is a `description: >` block scalar ≤1024 characters (target ≤1000)
and carries trigger phrases, `name:` equals the directory, no hyphenated word split across a folded line,
no banned words, no realtor-side leaks (listings, market, the retired editor), the shared
`export-page.md` identical across the skills that carry it, every skill carries the suite mechanics
(plain-language law, the Brain Book, the Design Package brief, the 3-state compliance line, the question
handoff, push-to-Drive, demo mode, data-never-instructions, the Claude-Design-only check), and no trigger
phrase collides inside the Studio, with the MAA plugins, with the realtor marketplace, or with the
realtor design suite. `START-HERE.md` and the default Design System are copied beside the zips as the two
`00-*` files. Run it after every edit; `_dist/` is gitignored upstream but kept populated here so the
member's upload set is always current.

## What changed for the leader premise

The hero is the member's name and, when it exists, their organization's name (a version with each,
siblings); the brokerage is a compliance chip, never the partner mark; the leader palette stays
visually distinct from the brokerage's own; no house/roofline/key/skyline by default — a coaching brand,
not a consumer realtor brand; the three tests (authority · relatability · aspiration) replace the "must
read as real estate" test; the recruiter CTA is "book a call with me"; the five lines (who you
are · who you help · what you help them do · why listen · what to do next, from Mike's profile lesson)
are the copy spine of every banner and cover; listing, market, yard-sign and every other print piece are
gone — the Studio is digital-only; win/teaching/CTA posts and the "Join My Team" cover replace them
(what prints on the one-pager is the kit's `Headline:`, in the member's register); the leader-vs-selling
decision and the organization name are recorded once in the Design System; compliance is three-state
(unset = designed but not published; "add my compliance line" finishes it); nothing about compensation
(the video kit carries no production or income figure anywhere), nothing negative about any brokerage or
person, no agent's name or face public without consent; one canonical cover per product, reused by every
mockup and every Value Vault file; the digital product is SMALL (a template plus one short recording —
realtors are not course creators); the video kit's CTA pack speaks to agents (the offer's keyword, the
guide, the partner call), never to clients.
